# figure: the published figures and their confidence

Experiments on what a survey states: the vehicle count, the speed percentiles (p85, p98 over a
population of vehicle maximum speeds) and the per-track confidence behind them. How much each
figure moves with the pipeline, what bounds it, and what the platform can state about it. Specs are
named `figure0001` onwards; see the [README](../README.md). The area was opened by the
[November 5 review](../../../docs/lidar/operations/experiment-review-2026-11-05.md), which found
no report had compared the figures between arms. [figure0003](figure0003.md) was opened by the
[Wayve series review](../../../docs/research/2026-11-05-wayve-learned-safety-series.md),
which found no figure carried an interval.
