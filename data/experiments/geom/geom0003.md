# geom0003: retention and fragmentation per reviewed mask

**Question.** How much of each reviewed object reaches its cluster, and into how many clusters do
its returns fall? The measure the geometry plan's W3 asks for, and the gate l4clus0001 is scored
by.

**Answer (October 13, 2026).** Half the labelled returns reach a cluster (0.494 pooled, 0.214 per
instant), and 36% of the instants that do are split, peaking at 20 to 50 m (54% and 46%) and on
trucks (59%); no cluster merged two reviewed objects. The baseline for l4clus0001:
[report](../../../docs/lidar/operations/w3-retention-fragmentation-kirk0-2026-10.md).
