# geom0005: a published box that holds its points and turns like a vehicle

**Question.** The macOS visualiser and the scene assets on velocity.report draw the tracker's
published box. Cars' boxes rotate so that their points fall outside them, and turn in ways no
vehicle can. Why, and what pipeline or configuration change fixes it?

**Method.** A replay tool, `lidar-box-eval`, scores the drawn box of every confirmed track against
its own cluster's members (containment) and its own motion (turn rate against speed, turning at
rest, turns over 30° in a frame, the axis against the direction of travel). A box model, built in
L5 behind the `box_model` tuning block, runs beside the tracker in the same replay under a set of
variants. Variants were chosen on kirk0, kirk1 and eight corpus sites, then scored on the other
fifteen and on the second segment of 22.

**Answer (October 16, 2026).** The drawn box is three estimates that disagree: the tracked
position (a medoid on the visible surface), a PCA heading smoothed at 8 % a frame behind guards,
and this frame's PCA extents measured along a different axis. On the eight tuning sites 97 % of
vehicle frames leave a point outside it. The box model holds every point by construction,
draws the axis within 1.6° of the direction of travel at the median (15.4° drawn today), and
cuts turn-rate violations by three quarters. The tracks are untouched. See the
[report](../../../docs/lidar/operations/published-box-rotation-2026-10.md).
