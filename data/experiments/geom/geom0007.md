# geom0007: the published box's length

**Question.** On kirk0's reviewed poses the box model draws the two trucks short: 3.8 m (the
vehicle floor) while they approach end-on, 10.7 m while they pass, then 6.5 m as they leave,
because the length is the third largest span of the last 50 and forgets the full view five
seconds later. The centre is 2.0 m from the reviewed body centre at the median. Does a longer
memory keep a truck whole without holding a merge's size? Does a length floor for a tall body,
which a car never is, draw an approaching truck nearer its length?

**Candidates.** The extent window at 150 spans and over the whole life (the top three spans ever
admitted); a 6 m length floor for a labelled body of a vehicle's width whose corroborated
vertical extent is 2.6 m or more (a high-roof van is about 2.7 m tall and 5.9 m long; a car or an
SUV is under 2.1 m).

**Rule, written before any replay (2026-10-17).** A candidate passes on the tuning set (the first
segment of 23 sites and the two kirk-labelled captures) when:

1. its target improves on kirk0's 44 reviewed keyframes: the median distance from the drawn
   centre to the reviewed body centre falls by 0.5 m or more and the median ratio of drawn to
   reviewed length rises by 0.1 or more, the 90th percentile of the centre distance is no worse,
   and the car's keyframes are drawn no more than 0.5 m longer than the box model draws them;
2. nothing else gets worse, pooled over vehicle frames: no return outside; visible turn-rate
   violations, turning at rest and visible jumps each no more than the box model's × 1.1 + 0.1
   points; the straight-road error's median and p90 no more than 0.5° worse; kirk0's reviewed axis
   p90 no more than 1° worse; and, against holding a merge, the share of boxes holding five or
   more returns of another cluster, the share overlapping another confirmed track's box by more
   than a tenth of the smaller box, and the share longer than 13 m, each no more than the box
   model's × 1.1 + 0.1 points.

A candidate that passes is then scored on the fifth segment of the 11 sites that have one, read
for the first time, where the second clause must hold again (no reviewed poses exist there). If
a memory and the floor both pass, their combination must pass both clauses too. The one that
passes with the largest centre gain becomes the default; a candidate that fails stays an option.
