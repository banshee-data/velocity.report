# geom0008: an exclusive published box

**Question.** The box model holds every return of its own, but draws more than it sees: the
vehicle length floor, the corroborated extents held after the view shrinks, and the unseen
remainder placed away from the sensor. On the 23 first segments and kirk0 it holds five or more
returns of another cluster on 6.0 % of vehicle rows and overlaps another confirmed track's box on
17.6 %, against 1.3 % and 10.7 % for the legacy box. If the part of the box beyond its own returns
stops a margin short of the nearest return of another cluster in the same frame, does it stop
drawing into its neighbours without drawing worse anywhere else?

**Candidates.** `exclusive_margin_metres` at 0.1 m and 0.3 m: along each axis the box may extend
beyond its own returns only up to that distance short of another cluster's return in its path,
never toward the sensor past its own nearest return (space the sensor saw through), and never
shorter than its own returns' span. (The sensor-side clause was added before any replay, when the
unit test showed a floored box sliding toward the sensor once its far side was stopped.)

**Rule, written before any replay (2026-10-17).** A candidate passes on the tuning set (the first
segment of 23 sites and the two kirk-labelled captures, pooled over vehicle rows) when:

1. its target improves: the share of boxes holding five or more returns of another cluster falls
   by half or more, and the share overlapping another confirmed track's box by more than a tenth
   of the smaller box falls by 3 points or more;
2. nothing else gets worse: no return outside; visible turn-rate violations, turning at rest and
   visible jumps each no more than the box model's × 1.1 + 0.1 points; the straight-road error's
   median and p90 no more than 0.5° worse; the centre slip's p90 no more than the box model's ×
   1.1 + 0.05 m; the share longer than 13 m no higher; and on kirk0's 44 reviewed keyframes the
   axis p90 no more than 1° worse, no more keyframes over 45°, the median centre distance no more
   than 0.2 m worse and the median length ratio no more than 0.05 lower.

A candidate that passes is then scored on the fifth segment of the 11 sites that have one, read
for the first time, where it must keep at least half of each target improvement and meet the
second clause (without the reviewed keyframes, which exist only on kirk0). If both pass, the one
with the larger fall in overlap becomes the default; one that fails stays an option, off.
