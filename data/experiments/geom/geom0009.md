# geom0009: the published box's merge guard

**Question.** The box model refuses a frame's spans as extent evidence when the tracker flags the
cluster a probable merge (`TrackedObject.MergeCandidate`). That flag is a growth test: the
cluster's area over the track's history, above 2.5. On kirk0, with the duplicate tracks removed
(l5id0004's `body-older`), truck 1 is one track through its close pass, yet 34 of its 50 frames
there are flagged, because the track grew from a small fragment to the whole truck. Its box never
learns the 10.6 m it then shows, and it leaves drawn 6.4 m long. Does admitting a flagged frame's
spans, always or only up to a rigid vehicle's 12 m, draw the trucks their length without holding
a real merge's size?

**Candidates.** `km-mergein`: a merge-flagged frame's spans enter the extent beliefs like any
other's. `km-mergein12`: they do when the span along the body is 12 m or less. The width belief
stays capped at 2.6 m either way.

**Rule, written before any replay (2026-10-17).** A candidate passes on the tuning set (the first
segment of 23 sites and the two kirk captures, pooled over vehicle rows) when:

1. its target improves on kirk0's 44 reviewed keyframes: the median distance from the drawn centre
   to the reviewed body centre falls by 0.3 m or more and the median ratio of drawn to reviewed
   length rises by 0.05 or more, with the centre's p90 no worse;
2. nothing else gets worse: no return outside; visible turn-rate violations, turning at rest and
   visible jumps each no more than the box model's × 1.1 + 0.1 points; the straight-road error's
   median and p90 no more than 0.5° worse; kirk0's reviewed axis p90 no more than 1° worse and no
   more keyframes over 45°; and, against holding a merge's size, the share of boxes holding five or
   more returns of another cluster, the share overlapping another confirmed track's box by more
   than a tenth, and the share longer than 13 m, each no more than the box model's × 1.1 + 0.1
   points.

A candidate that passes is scored on the fifth segment of the 9 usable sites, where no box measure
has been computed, and must meet the second clause again (without the reviewed keyframes). The one
that passes with the larger centre gain becomes the default, as a `box_model` key with the perf
baselines recaptured; one that fails stays an option, off.

**Result (2026-10-17).** Both candidates failed, on one clause. The target was met by a wide margin
(kirk0's reviewed centre error 2.14 m to 1.20 m at the median, p90 4.16 m to 3.97 m, length ratio
0.62 to 0.84, axis unchanged), and every other clause held, but boxes holding five or more returns
of another cluster rose from 5.95 % to 6.75 % of vehicle rows against a limit of 6.65 %. The 12 m
cap changed nothing. The option stays off. Report: [published box refinement](../../../docs/lidar/operations/published-box-refinement-2026-10.md#geom0009-the-merge-guard).
