# geom0010: held extents that yield to another object

**Question.** geom0009 admitted the spans of frames the tracker flags a probable merge and drew
kirk0's trucks their length (reviewed centre error 2.14 m to 1.20 m), but failed on one clause:
boxes holding five or more returns of another cluster rose from 5.95 % to 6.75 % of vehicle rows,
because after a real merge ends the held length reaches the next object. geom0008 stopped the box
at a neighbour every frame and made it jump. If instead the length and width beliefs are reset to
what the box now sees once another cluster's returns have lain inside the held part of the box
for three frames running, does admitting the merge spans keep the trucks' length without the cost?

**Candidate.** `km-mergein-yield3`: geom0009's `km-mergein` with `YieldFrames` 3. A frame counts
when five or more returns of another cluster lie inside the box, beyond the box's own returns
along or across it, by more than 0.1 m; three consecutive such frames reset the beliefs to the
frame's own spans, the vehicle floor still applying.

**Rule, written before any replay (2026-10-17).** It passes on the tuning set (the first segment
of 23 sites and the two kirk captures, pooled over vehicle rows) when:

1. its target improves on kirk0's 44 reviewed keyframes: the median distance from the drawn centre
   to the reviewed body centre falls by 0.3 m or more and the median ratio of drawn to reviewed
   length rises by 0.05 or more, with the centre's p90 no worse;
2. nothing else gets worse: no return outside; visible turn-rate violations, turning at rest and
   visible jumps each no more than the box model's × 1.1 + 0.1 points; the straight-road error's
   median and p90 no more than 0.5° worse; the centre slip's p90 no more than the box model's ×
   1.1 + 0.05 m; kirk0's reviewed axis p90 no more than 1° worse and no more keyframes over 45°;
   and the shares of boxes holding five or more returns of another cluster, overlapping another
   confirmed track's box by more than a tenth, and longer than 13 m, each no more than the box
   model's × 1.1 + 0.1 points.

If it passes, it is scored on the fifth segment of the 9 usable sites, where no box measure has
been computed, and must meet the second clause again (without the reviewed keyframes). Passing
both, it becomes the default: `admit_merge_spans` and `yield_frames` join the `box_model` block,
with the perf baselines recaptured. Failing either, it stays an option, off.

**Result (2026-10-17).** It failed its target. On kirk0's reviewed keyframes the yield gave back
geom0009's gain (centre p50 2.17 m against the box model's 2.14 m and `km-mergein`'s 1.20 m, length
ratio 0.62), because a truck's other fragments are other clusters inside its held box. On the
corpus it brought the foreign returns down from 6.75 % to 6.35 %. It stays off. Report:
[published box refinement](../../../docs/lidar/operations/published-box-refinement-2026-10.md#geom0010-held-extents-that-yield).
