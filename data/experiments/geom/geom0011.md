# geom0011: centre against extent (W9)

**Question.** D-28 adopted Schäfer et al.'s decomposition as an `S` item: for the reference
vehicles on kirk0, how much of each arm's centre error is explained by its extent error, and how
much remains? The pilot read it by eye (the near-edge arm places truck 1's rear within 0.2 m and
its centre 2.2 m behind, with the body 5.9 m against 10.6); this measures it, for the near-edge
arm, the geometry candidate and the published box, which the following gap now leans on.

**Reading, written before the replays (2026-10-18).** A measurement, no rule. What it decides:
if two thirds or more of the trucks' along-axis centre error is the length's, the following gap's
error is the length's too, and the next lever is upstream (one track per vehicle, the merge
spans, the W3 fix) rather than the centre; if less, the centre's placement on the seen face is
the question and the containment interval is where to look. The cars are reported apart from
the trucks, and truck 2 apart from truck 1, as the W0 record asks.

**What it does not establish.** Anything held out or beyond three vehicles; a rate.
