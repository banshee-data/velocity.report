# geom0013: the hull bound

**Question.** A following gap is read between two bodies' extremes along a path, and passing
clearance between their sides. A hull of the visible returns can only move those reads where the
box face lies outside every return: oblique views and rounded corners. How far is that, on kirk0's
reviewed poses and pairs, and how does it compare with the extent error that dominates the gap?

**Rule, written before the replay (2026-10-27).** Hulls stop here unless, at some aspect bin with
at least 30 observations, the median box-face-to-hull distance along the path exceeds 0.15 m, or
the pilot pairs' hull gaps are closer to the reviewed gaps than the box gaps by 0.10 m or more in
median. If either holds, the report proposes a hull endpoint source to the behaviour plan for
passing clearance; it changes no default.

**What it does not establish.** Anything about the unseen extent, which no visible hull measures;
cyclists and pedestrians, which kirk0's pilot does not hold.
