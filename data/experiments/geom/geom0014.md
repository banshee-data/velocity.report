# geom0014: what the defaults and the solid-body configs draw on kirk0, on main

**Question.** On October 11 the scene-variant tooling replayed 30 s of kirk0 under six tuning
configs on build `0dd608f` and rendered each export. Three observations came out of it, recorded
on a private page and not in the repository: the solid-body shadow drew the defaults' boxes in
every frame; the three arms whose solid body drives the track (A2, alignment, convergence) turned
the box less from frame to frame than the defaults; and the convergence candidate's box width
varied least. Since then main has changed every one of those files: the defaults gained the
published box model (`box_model`, geom0005) and the track guard, and L3 changed. The replay
fixes of perf0002 and l3bg0021 also landed, and the October run was six replays in a row on one
server, the history those fixes concern. Do the three observations hold on main?

The October set's sixth config, `config/tuning.optimised.json`, is not an arm here. It is not a
parameter set under `config/experiments/`, and it still carries the L3 values the defaults have
since left, so a difference from it would not say which change made it.

**Method.** `tools/s2-archive/export-scene-variants.py` against a scratch server built by `make` at
this spec's commit, with a fresh database: kirk0 from 45 s for 30 s, the window the October run
used, under each arm in the order listed, at the published replay settings
(`SCENE_SETTLE=1`, scaled at 0.5, stride 2, export from frame 400). Then the defaults alone, as a
second set, on the same server process. From each export's frames, per arm: the track ids, the
track-frames, the tracks lasting at least 3 s (15 frames), and the moving ones among them (peak
speed at least 3 m/s). Over the moving tracks: the median across tracks of each track's standard
deviation of box length and of width, and the median and 90th percentile of the absolute heading
change between consecutive frames. Track ids are export-local and are not matched across arms.

**Rule, written before the replays.**

1. _The shadow changes nothing._ The shadow arm's exported tracks equal the defaults' in every
   frame (position, extents, heading, speed, class), ignoring export-local ids. Any difference is
   a defect: the shadow's contract is that the tracks are the defaults'.
2. _The repeat equals the first._ The defaults replayed last, after four other configs on the same
   process, export the same tracks as the defaults replayed first, ignoring ids. A difference
   means a replay still carries state from the one before it.
3. _October's direction._ The observation holds if each of A2, alignment and convergence has a
   lower median heading change per frame than the defaults; it does not if any is equal or higher.
   October measured 0.40° for each against 0.69°.
4. The width and length spreads are reported beside October's with no rule: October's differences
   (0.58 m against 0.62 m in width) were too small on 16 to 18 tracks to decide anything.

Nothing is adopted. The arms are scored by their own experiments; this one checks what the
published scene draws under them, which is what the variant renders show a person.

**What it will not establish.** Accuracy. kirk0's labels are not read, and a steady box is not a
correct one. A replay measures what the pipeline drew, not where the vehicles were.
