# geom0015: D2 refreshed on kirk0's frozen split, with a measurement-bias baseline

**Question.** The October D2 re-run scored kirk0 against a version 1 split draft that was never
frozen, under the medoid default, and reported positions only against visible-mask centres. Since
then the split has been frozen (`kirk0-ad8b9438-tuning-r1`, revision 2 on the version 2 pack), the
production position has become the mean of the cluster's returns (l4clus0006), and the pack carries
22 reviewed body poses. Bound to that frozen split, at the current build:

1. Do the replays repeat? Observation repeatability is reported on its own and is not accuracy.
2. How do the three positions compare on track-linked identity: the mean (`a0`), the medoid
   (`medoid`) and D2's OBB centre (`obb-centre`)?
3. How far is each position from the reviewed body centre, by range and aspect, on the reviewed
   straight passes? This is the measurement-bias baseline later geometry work compares against.
   Each arm gives three readings: the measurement the filter consumed (the L4 position), the L5
   online estimate, and the published box centre.

**Arms.** `a0` is the defaults, `config/tuning.defaults.json`: fingerprint `ab0c4035780d`, file
SHA-256 `9ad7377f…1675`, cluster position the mean. `medoid` is
`config/experiments/foreground-refine.json`: fingerprint `42e3a582b803`, file SHA-256
`cb37e11e…2b6a`. It is the defaults without the `l4.dbscan_xy_v1.cluster_position` block, so each
cluster's position is its medoid; the two files differ in that block alone. `obb-centre` is the
defaults replayed with `-measurement-mode obb_centre_v1`. The measurement mode is not a tuning key,
so the arm carries the defaults' fingerprint, and its parameter hash tells it apart.

**The reviewed straight passes.** The frozen split's episode `following-472-530`: truck 1
(`obj_35c661db`, 9 reviewed poses), car 8 (`obj_8c728991`, 7) and truck 2 (`obj_8e829a06`, 6), all
independent `fit:mask_v1` fits in one lane. Read from the physical references before any replay,
each vehicle's reviewed headings span at most 10.6°, so each is a straight pass. The body centres
lie 5.5 to 32.7 m from the sensor at aspects of 10° to 90°. Range bands are under 10, 10 to 20 and
20 m or more. Aspect bins are 0 to 30° (near end on), 30 to 60° and 60 to 90° (near broadside),
from the angle between the line of sight to the reference centre and the reference heading. Range
and aspect are confounded with the vehicle. Car 8 is the only vehicle under 10 m, at 50° to 90°.
The trucks lie 9.8 to 32.7 m away at 10° to 31°. A cell therefore says which vehicle it is about
as much as which view.

**Rule, written before any replay (2026-11-05).** This is a measurement. It changes no default,
whatever it reads: kirk0 is a tuning capture, and nothing here is held out.

1. **Binding.** Every score is from `lidar-ground-truth-eval perframe` given the frozen split
   `kirk0-reference-tuning-r1.json` (split digest `sha256:74ddd659…5617`). The evaluator must accept
   it with every pin holding: label set revision 2232, physical revision 76 and the capture's
   SHA-256 `2864ebde…`. If it refuses, no figure is quoted, and the report records the refusal.
2. **Repeatability, kept apart from accuracy.** Each arm's two replays must write identical
   observation rows and identical estimate rows. `a0` and `obb-centre` must write identical
   observations, since only L5 differs between them. An arm that does not repeat has its accuracy
   figures withheld from the baseline. `medoid` changes the L4 position, so its observations differ
   from `a0`'s by construction. Its rows are compared for cluster membership only.
3. **Identity, on the reviewed whole pack.** HOTA, IDF1, identity switches, fragmentations and
   recall per arm, with the 5th to 95th percentile of each paired delta against `a0` over 2,000
   bootstrap draws. D5 and l4clus0006 stand, with the mean as production and the OBB centre
   opt-in, unless an arm beats `a0` on both HOTA and IDF1 with the whole 5th-to-95th interval of
   each delta above zero. Such an arm becomes a candidate for label0001's held-out read. It is not
   adopted.
4. **The bias baseline.** For each arm and reading: the number of the 22 poses matched within
   3 m; the median centre error; the median signed offset toward the sensor (the prediction less
   the reference centre, projected on the unit vector from the reference centre to the sensor);
   and the median absolute along- and across-axis offsets. Each is given per vehicle, per range band
   and per aspect bin. An unmatched pose is counted and never dropped silently. Predictions, stated
   now and not gates: every arm's measurement and estimate sit on the sensor's side of the
   reference centre on both trucks, at more than the reference bound; the published box centre is
   nearer the reference than the measurement on car 8; and across arms the measurement's offset
   toward the sensor is largest for the medoid and smallest for the OBB centre.
5. **What the baseline is for.** Later experiments on the body's position (geom0012, the near-edge
   and box levers) quote their kirk0 centre errors against this table, at the same split, episode
   and gate. A change that moves the fingerprint re-runs this spec's physical step rather than
   comparing across builds.

**What could mislead.** Twenty-two poses of three vehicles in one lane. Two are trucks whose length
every arm underestimates (W3, W9), so the along-axis error on the trucks is mostly the length's and
is not position bias. The car's rows are the cleanest measure of position bias, and there are
seven of them. The references are fits to the reviewed returns, not surveyed bodies. A 3 m gate
can leave the worst truck poses unmatched, so the matched count goes beside every median. The
cells by range and aspect hold a few poses each and are not rates. Ignoring `obj_788622b5` (a
parked car) is a scoring choice, and the run without the ignore is reported beside it.

**What it does not establish.** Anything held out. A physical accuracy beyond the references' own
bounds. The vertical. Which centre the trail should draw, which is geom0012's question; this spec
supplies its kirk0 baseline.
