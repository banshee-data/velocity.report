# geom0016: the facet attribution gate, restated

**Question.** The facet plan funds E2's constellation arm D only if the along-path error where an
end is visible is mostly edge localisation rather than the extent. F0's predeclared gate read that
as a ratio of end-normal mean absolute residuals, the class-prior stratum over the evidence
stratum, with 1.25 as the threshold. On kirk0 it returned 0.73, "arm D keeps its case", and the
report showed why that reading should not be acted on: the believed extents are short of the
labelled span in 83 % of car and 97 % of truck instants, and removing the half shortfall the
extent forces takes the car and truck end-face medians to −0.02 m. The raw residual mixes the two
terms the gate is meant to separate. Restated on a measure that separates them, with the extent
read from the solid body's extent state (#970) rather than the shipped belief: is the remaining
face placement error large enough, and large against the extent term, for arm D to keep its case?

**The measure.** The near-face evaluator, at schema version 2, reports it on every scored instant
([near-face evaluation](../../../docs/lidar/operations/near-face-evaluation.md)). The labelled
returns' span along the believed heading is a lower bound on the length, given the heading. The
half length shortfall is `max(0, span − believed length)/2`. The end placement residual is the end
normal residual plus that half shortfall: what a box of the believed length leaves once the part
its length forces is removed. The side face takes the width the same way. This is F0 Section 4.1's
post-hoc decomposition, computed by the evaluator and predeclared here.

**Arms.** `control` is F0's control, the shadow solid body with face hysteresis, course-aligned
faces and full members. `face-state` adds `solid_body_face_state`: half-extents in the shadow
filter's state, censored extent evidence, span bounds on the belief, heading noise in the face
variance and anisotropic reference bias, the arm the face-state maths review says to score as one.
Its reported length and width are `2hL` and `2hW` from the state, so the shortfall the gate reads
is the extent state's. Both arms run the defaults; the face-state options are replay experiments,
not tuning keys, so the fingerprints are equal and the parameter hashes tell the arms apart. W2's
floored box, which the backlog row named, is not used: the solid body's extent state is the
belief the face update consumes, and the box is not what the near-face evaluator scores.

**Rule, written before any replay (2026-11-08).** The rule is fixed by this commit. Reading kirk0
does not change it; a restatement is a new commit before the held-out read, and the kirk0 figures
are then not reused for the decision.

1. **Binding.** Every figure is from `lidar-near-face-eval` given a frozen split, accepted with
   every pin holding. If the evaluator refuses a split, no figure from it is quoted, and the report
   records the refusal.
2. **Repeatability.** Each arm's two kirk0 replays must write identical solid-body rows. An arm
   that does not repeat has its figures withheld.
3. **The quantities**, per arm, from the evaluator's `extent_evidence` stratum: `P`, the end
   placement mean absolute (`end_placement.mean_abs_m`); `S`, the half length shortfall mean
   over the same instants (`half_length_shortfall.mean_m`); the raw end normal mean absolute
   beside them; and the extent share `E = S / (S + P)`. The side face's placement and half width
   shortfall are reported the same way and do not decide. The prior stratum's placement, and F0's
   ratio restated on placement (prior over evidence), are reported for continuity and do not
   decide either: F0 showed the two strata hold different vehicles.
4. **The gate**, read on `face-state` on the held-out set only, in this order:
   - `insufficient_evidence` if the evidence stratum has fewer than 100 instants with an end
     residual, or those instants come from fewer than 8 objects (counted from `-instants`), or
     the held-out masks do not exist.
   - `extent_dominates` if `S > P`, that is `E > 0.5`. Arm D is not funded on this evidence;
     the effort routes to extent memory and interval output, the plan's recorded alternative.
   - `placement_immaterial` if `P < 0.10 m`. The remaining placement error is at the face's own
     resolution (range noise, the trimmed extreme, partly seen faces), and no facet arm can show a
     material gain on it. Arm D is not funded.
   - Otherwise `placement_in_play`: edge localisation is a material share of the end error, and
     arm D keeps its case for E2, where H2 and its physical references still decide it.
5. **The screen.** kirk0 is a tuning capture and the capture the shadow work was developed on. Its
   readings for both arms are reported in the same form and are not a decision. Predictions,
   stated now and not gates: on kirk0, `face-state`'s `S` is below `control`'s, because the
   extent state lengthens the extents a second face reaches; and `control`'s end placement medians
   for cars and trucks, read from the instants joined to the split's classes, sit within 0.10 m of
   zero, as F0's post-hoc figures did.
6. **What it changes.** Nothing in the tracker or the defaults. The outcome is a line in the facet
   plan's scorecard, and the report states it with the four quantities, their counts, and the
   control's figures beside them.

**What could mislead.** The span is a lower bound on the extent given the believed heading, so the
half shortfall is a lower bound on the extent's part, and `P` keeps whatever extent error the span
cannot see: the measure leans toward `placement_in_play`, not away from it. A wrong heading
inflates the span across it, which the bus showed on kirk0; the side face is reported, not read,
for that reason. The near-face residual sees only faces the sensor sees, so where only a side is
visible the along-path error is not measured. The held-out window is three minutes of one site,
and its objects are few; the 8-object floor says when it is too few to read. Pedestrians are in
the evaluator's strata and dilute both `P` and `S`; a by-class reading from the instants is
reported beside the gate and does not decide it.

**What it does not establish.** The physical along-path endpoint error, which needs physical
references and is H2's measure. Whether arm D would reach H2's 20 % reduction. Whether the extent
state is a better estimator than the shipped belief, which the state plan's checklist scores
against geom0015's box rows.
