# geom0017: the published box takes only measured length from the extent state

**Question.** `box_extent_solid_body` (#984) takes the published box's length and width from the
solid body's joint extent state once a face or a bound has moved each dimension and its sigma is
at most half the class prior's. In the November benchmark (l5id0019, PR #994) it moved no truck
length on kirk0's reviewed poses, but widened car 8's box from about 1.8 m to 2.3 to 2.6 m
against a 1.73 m reference, and moved car 8's median centre error from 0.04 to 0.29 m. A sensor
beside the road sees one side of a passing vehicle. The far side of the width is then the class
prior and the span bounds, and the bounds taken across a heading that has not converged only
ever raise it. If the box takes from the state only a dimension the state has measured, does it
keep a0's geometry and identity on kirk0, and is #984's width effect gone?

**The change.** `BoxModelConfig.BoxExtentMeasuredOnly`, replay experiment
`box_extent_measured_length`, which qualifies `box_extent_solid_body` and is refused without it.
Under it the box takes the length only once both end faces have updated `hL`, from opposite
sides, or a span bound has moved it, and the width only once both side faces have updated `hW`.
The sigma bound still applies. Otherwise the box keeps its own `windowBelief` for that dimension.
"From opposite sides" means the two faces' last site-frame normals lie more than 135° apart, so
a heading that turns by a half turn and renames the face the sensor keeps seeing does not count
as a pair. The extent state's existing counter (`updates`) does not say which face moved a
dimension. The state now records that per face beside it, and nothing the state computes reads
the record.

**Arms.** `a0` is the defaults. `box-extent` is l5id0019's arm of that name, as merged in #984, and
checks that this build reproduces it. `measured-length` adds the qualifier. All three run the
defaults; the options are replay experiments, so the fingerprints are equal and the parameter
hashes tell the arms apart.

**Rule, written before any replay (2026-11-09).** This commit fixes the rule, and reading kirk0
does not change it.

1. **Anchor.** `a0` must reproduce l5id0019's published-box rows on the 22 poses: 22 matched,
   median centre error 0.190 m, car 8 0.04 m. `box-extent` must reproduce its l5id0019 rows (median
   0.281 m, car 8 0.29 m). If either fails, no decision is read and the report says which.
2. **Repeatability.** Each arm's two replays must write identical tracking baselines. An arm that
   does not repeat has its figures withheld.
3. **The gates**, on `measured-length`, all three required to pass:
   - **G1, geometry.** The published box's median centre error over the matched reviewed poses is
     not worse than `a0`'s (at most 0.190 m to three decimals), with all 22 matched.
   - **G2, car 8.** Car 8's median centre error is within 0.05 m of `a0`'s 0.04 m (at most
     0.09 m, to two decimals).
   - **G3, identity.** The arm's paired-bootstrap point HOTA and IDF1 on the labelled objects lie
     inside `a0`'s 5th-to-95th intervals from the same bootstrap (l5id0019: HOTA 0.4111 to 0.5415,
     IDF1 0.5107 to 0.6232), and its identity switches inside `a0`'s (4 to 242).
4. **Outcome.** All three pass: `measured-length` replaces `box-extent` as the published-box arm
   worth carrying to label0003's held-out window once tracking fix 3 lengthens the extent state.
   Any fails: the gate that failed is named, and `box-extent` and the qualifier both stay off.
   Nothing is adopted and no default changes either way.
5. **Reported, not decided.** Per vehicle: matched poses, median centre error, median offset
   toward the sensor, published length and width, against the references (truck 1 10.63 × 2.91 m,
   car 8 4.26 × 1.73 m, truck 2 9.63 × 2.84 m). Box IoU and yaw. Full-comparison HOTA, IDF1,
   switches and fragmentations, and the paired deltas against `a0` with l5id0019's R1 reading.
   Brief tracks and spurious clusters a frame (`lidar_noise_screen.py`), and moving tracks lost,
   shortened and added against `a0` (`lidar_flip_cost.py --match footprint`). The box counters:
   frames that took the state, took its width, or kept a one-sided dimension.
6. **Predictions, stated now and not gates.** Car 8's published width returns to `a0`'s 1.78 to
   1.90 m, and no truck's published length moves by 0.5 m. In l5id0019 the frame's span floor
   exceeded the state's truck lengths, so the state's length has nothing to carry until fix 3.

**What could mislead.** kirk0 is one tuning capture. Its 22 poses are three vehicles, two of them
trucks whose length the box already reads from the span, so the arm is read almost entirely on
car 8. The L3 track guard carries the box into the next frames' clusters, so a box change can
move identity. Identity on 19 scored objects has wide intervals, and G3 is a non-inferiority
bound, not a gain. The reconstructed pack's points differ from the published pack's. The
physical references are re-pinned in scratch to its digest, as l5id0019 scored them.

**What it does not establish.** Anything held out or off kirk0. Whether the measured length helps
once the extent state accumulates length (fix 3). Whether an end-on span, which shows the whole
width, should count as measuring it, which needs the width bounds gated on the heading first.
