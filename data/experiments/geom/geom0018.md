# geom0018: extent bound accrual on kirk0

**Question.** The November benchmark (l5id0019, PR #994) bisected a 1.64 m loss in truck 2's
solid-body length on kirk0 to #989 (`fc9d8979`): 6.24 m before, 4.60 m after, against a 9.63 m
reference, while car 8 improved from 0.89 to 0.36 m. #989 replaced the moment-matched span-bound
truncation with a projection that holds a half-extent at the current frame's bound and leaves P
alone. Its ranked fix 3 asks that the extent state build length across frames again and take no
width bound off an unsettled heading. `solid_body_extent_accrual` is that fix
(`SolidBodyOptions.ExtentBoundAccrual`): bounds only along a believed axis, the course standing
in for an unsettled face axis; each half-extent held at or above the largest bound three admitted
frames reached; a merge candidate one body wide giving its length, and only its length, to that
record. Does it restore truck 2's length without giving back car 8's, and what does it do to the
published box under `box_extent_solid_body`?

**Arms.** `a0` is the defaults, for the published-box comparison. `face-state` is the shadow solid
body with all five face-state options: main's extent state since #989, and l5id0019's
`face-state` arm. `accrual` adds the option. `box-extent` is l5id0019's `box-extent` arm, the
published box's length and width from the extent state; `box-accrual` adds the option to it. The
face-state options and the accrual are replay experiments, so every arm runs the defaults'
fingerprint and the parameter hashes tell them apart.

**What was read before this rule.** Two label-free probe replays of the `face-state` arm on kirk0,
at this branch's base and at #989's parent (`50c7790b`), with a scratch trace of truck 2's extent
state per frame (truck 2 is the track `seq-000049` in l5id0019's scoring). No scored replay of any
arm with the option has run. The trace says where the 6.24 m came from, and the predictions below
are made from it:

- At #989's parent the bounds truck 2 received before its reviewed poses were 3.4 to 4.0 m long
  and 2.6 to 3.3 m across, taken at under 2 m/s along a face axis 40 to 50 degrees from its
  course (orientation variance about 1.2 rad²). The moment-matched truncation of those bounds
  raised the half-length from 2.20 to 3.14 m in five frames: a 6.3 m length from spans of at
  most 3.95 m, the overshoot #989 removed. The 6.24 m on the reviewed poses is that overshoot.
- On truck 2's six reviewed poses (73.8 to 75.6 s) no bound is admitted at this base: the face
  axis is 12 to 23 degrees from the course. The span along the course is 3.4 to 3.6 m until
  75.0 s, then 8.2 to 8.8 m with the track flagged a merge candidate (the cluster's area against
  the running box), which refuses it. The first admitted bound, 8.96 m at 77.5 s, takes the
  half-length to 4.48 m.

**Rule, written before any scored replay (2026-11-09).** The rule is fixed by this commit. Every
figure is from the score step's tools at one build of this branch, the build stamped by the
Makefile's LDFLAGS; the reviewed poses are geom0015's 22, samples 472 to 530 (truck 1 nine, car 8
seven, truck 2 six), references truck 1 10.63 × 2.91 m, car 8 4.26 × 1.73 m, truck 2
9.63 × 2.84 m.

- **R0, binding.** `face-state` must reproduce l5id0019's solid-body rows for the three vehicles
  (truck 2 4.60 m, car 8 3.90 m, truck 1 9.73 m, medians over the poses, to 0.01 m), and `a0`
  its box rows (22 of 22 matched, median 0.190 m). If either does not, no figure is quoted as an
  answer and the report says why. `accrual`'s tracking baseline must equal `face-state`'s byte for
  byte: the option is in the shadow.
- **R1, truck 2's length.** `accrual`'s solid-body length, median over truck 2's six poses, is at
  least 6.24 m, and its absolute error against 9.63 m is below #989's 5.03 m.
- **R2, car 8's length.** `accrual`'s solid-body absolute length error, median over car 8's seven
  poses, is no more than `face-state`'s plus 0.005 m.
- **R3, the floor.** `accrual`'s count of solid-body rows with either dimension at the half-extent
  floor (length or width at or under 0.2001 m) is no more than `face-state`'s.
- **R4, the published box.** `box-accrual`'s box rows on the 22 poses against `a0`'s: no fewer
  matched; median centre error no more than 0.05 m above `a0`'s overall and no more than 0.25 m
  above it for any one vehicle; median absolute truck length error no more than 0.5 m above
  (l5id0019's R3 thresholds read as non-inferiority).
- **Verdict.** The fix holds on kirk0 when R1 to R4 all hold. A failing R1 is reported as the
  brief's target not met, with the per-pose lengths and the reason, and is not restated. Nothing is
  adopted and no default changes, whatever the result: kirk0 is a tuning capture.
- **Reported, not gates.** Per-pose solid-body lengths and widths of the three vehicles under each
  arm; truck 2's solid-body length after the reviewed poses (label-free, from the rows); the
  solid-body widths, which l5id0019 found wide (car 8 2.45 m, truck 1 3.82 m); `box-extent`'s box
  rows beside `box-accrual`'s; and the count of frames whose bound came from the course.

**Predictions, stated now and not gates.** From the trace: R1 fails. On poses 491 to 504 no
believed-axis span exceeds what the state already holds (about 4.5 to 4.8 m), so the length there
is unchanged; on pose 509 the record holds three merge-candidate length spans of 8.2 to 8.7 m, and
the length there rises to about 8.2 m. The median of six moves from 4.60 to about 4.7 m. R2 and R3
hold. Widths fall where they were raised off an unsettled heading; by how much is not predicted.

**First read (2026-11-09, the option at `016ebacc`).** R0 held: `face-state` reproduced
l5id0019's solid-body rows (truck 2 4.60 m, car 8 3.90 m, truck 1 9.73 m), `a0` its box rows,
and `accrual`'s tracking baseline equalled `face-state`'s. The option then refused every bound off
an axis not believed within 5 degrees. R1 failed (truck 2 4.58 m), R2 failed (car 8's length error
0.83 m against 0.36 m) and R3 failed (113 rows at the floor against 14, 106 of them widths). R4
held (median 0.227 m against `a0`'s 0.190 m, car 8 0.23 m against 0.04 m). Refusing the width
bounds let the faces carry widths to the floor, and refusing the length bounds shortened truck 1
and car 8. The prediction for pose 509 was wrong: the course's sigma was 5.0 to 5.5 degrees there,
just outside the 5-degree belief. That read is final for that version and is not restated.

**Second read: the rule restated before its replays (2026-11-09).** The option at `181459be`
widens a bound's window of axes with the axis's uncertainty instead of refusing the bound: twice
the standard deviation of the face axis or, where smaller, the course, from 10 degrees to a
quarter-turn. At 10 degrees it is main's computation exactly. The second read replays `accrual`
and `box-accrual` at that build, beside `a0` and `face-state` at the same build, and applies R0 to
R4 and the verdict exactly as written above. Nothing in R0 to R4 changes. Predictions, not gates:
R1 fails again, truck 2 within 0.1 m of 4.60 m on the median of its six poses, since no window
gives its reviewed poses a span longer than the state holds; R3 holds, since every frame keeps a
width bound; car 8's and truck 1's widths fall from 2.45 and 3.82 m; R2 is not predicted.

**What could mislead.** One tuning capture, three reviewed vehicles, six truck 2 poses within two
seconds. The merge flag is an area ratio, so the lateral test is the only guard on the merge
candidate's length; kirk0 has no labelled merge to test it on. The reconstructed pack's points are
decoded by the current parser. The record keeps no frame identities and cannot be revised (Section
9.2.3), which no figure here can show.

**What it does not establish.** Anything held out, any other site, a merge reference, or whether
the extent state is a better estimator than the box's own belief.

**Answer (2026-11-09).** The rule fails in both reads, and nothing is adopted. The first version
failed R1, R2 and R3. The second version, at `f6ce2843`, holds R0 and R2. It fails R1: truck 2
reads 4.83 m against 6.24 m, though its last two poses read 8.01 and 8.56 m. It fails R3 (16 floor
rows against 14) and R4 (median 0.280 m against `a0`'s 0.190 m, where main's `box-extent` reads
0.281 m). The trace shows the 6.24 m target was #989's overshoot, so no honest lower bound
reaches it on these poses. See the
[report](../../../docs/lidar/operations/extent-bound-accrual-kirk0-2026-11.md).
