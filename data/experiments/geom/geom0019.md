# geom0019: a body-frame occupancy profile as the extent estimator

**Question.** The extent state is the weakest link of the solid body. On kirk0's 22 reviewed
poses its centre sits a median 1.03 m from the reference against the published box's 0.19 m;
truck 2's believed length is 4.60 m against 9.63 m, and the bound-accrual option moved it to
4.83 m and failed three of its four clauses (geom0018). Every form tried so far summarises a
frame as one number, a span or a bound, and then argues about how to combine the numbers. Schäfer
et al. 2026 keep the evidence instead: a one-dimensional grid per dimension, log-odds per cell,
with lock-on, scored against RTK truth at a dimension RMSE of 0.46 m. D-28 adopted it as a shadow
arm and it was never built. It is also the smallest form of an accumulated body-local shape: if
occupancy along two axes does not beat a bound, a two-dimensional map or a surface model has no
case yet; if it does, the same grid in two dimensions is the next step and this spec's
withheld-frame score is its measure.

**Arms.** `a0` (the published box, the bar to beat); `face-state` and `accrual` as geom0018 left
them; `profile`, the new shadow. No arm changes a track.

**Cases.** kirk0 for the reviewed poses: tuning data, three vehicles, two of them trucks. The
first five-minute segments of ashbury-downey and haight-divisadero for the label-free clauses;
both have been read for geometry by l5id0019, so they are a screen.

**Rule, written before the option exists (2026-11-10).**

- **P1, long bodies.** Over truck 2's six poses the profile's median absolute length error is
  under the published box's (`a0`: length 6.59 m against 9.63 m, an error of 3.04 m) by 0.5 m
  or more, and over truck 1's nine poses it is no worse than the box's by more than 0.25 m.
- **P2, cars.** Over car 8's seven poses the profile's median absolute length error is at most
  0.365 m and its width is under 2.0 m at the median (reference 1.73 m; the extent state reads
  2.44 m).
- **P3, steadiness, label-free.** On each placement segment, over confirmed vehicle tracks of
  3 s or more, the median within-track spread of the profile's length after its first second
  (p90 less p10) is no more than the box's, and the share of tracks whose reported length falls
  by more than 0.5 m after lock-on is under 5 %. A rigid body does not shorten.
- **P4, withheld frames, label-free.** With every fifth observed frame withheld from the
  profile's update, the share of the withheld frame's returns that fall inside the profile's
  extent placed at the tracked pose, grown by 0.25 m, is at least the box's own share on the
  same frames less two points, while the profile's area is no more than 1.15 times the box's at
  the median. Containment bought with area does not pass.
- **Verdict.** The profile is a candidate when P1 to P4 hold. It then replaces `accrual` as the
  extent arm in geom0016's restated attribution gate, and a two-dimensional body-frame grid is
  specified as its successor with P4 as its measure. A fail on P1 with P3 and P4 holding says
  the limit is observation (a body seen from one end), not the estimator, and the next step is a
  capture with a long vehicle seen through its whole pass (W7), not another estimator.

**Reported, not decided.** Each end's observed or censored mark against the references; the
solid body's centre error when its extents are read from the profile; lock-on time; the
profile's cost per frame against the solid body's; the count of frames refused by the heading
admission.

**What could mislead.** Truck 2's six poses lie within two seconds and decide P1. The references
are fits to the sensor's own returns, not surveyed. Free cells need the ray geometry, and a ray
that passes over a low bonnet reads as free where a body is: the height band the grid reads must
be stated in the report. A wrong heading smears occupancy across the axis and reads as width,
which is why admission is gated on the heading belief; P2's width clause will show it. A merge of
two vehicles writes occupancy that the grid, like the accrual record, cannot take back; kirk0
has no cluster holding two reviewed objects, so that case is untested here.
