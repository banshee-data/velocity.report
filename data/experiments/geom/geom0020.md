# geom0020: the occupancy profile, anchored to the body

**Question.** geom0019's profile failed all four clauses, for two reasons its report traced and
neither of them about keeping free space. Its grid's origin was the solid body's centre, which
moves whenever the extent state does, so a shortening of the extent state was written as
occupancy: truck 2's profile jumped from about 9.6 m to 12.5 m at 77.4 s, and the P3 falls on the
placements are the same smear being trimmed. And its admission read the solid body's own heading,
whose spread on kirk0 is 33 to 44 degrees at the median, so the profile was admitted on one of
22 reviewed poses. The published box's heading, by contrast, sits 1.2 degrees from reviewed yaw at
the median (geom0015). This spec asks whether the same estimator, with its grid held to the body
and its axis taken from the box, measures length.

**Arms.** `a0`, `face-state` and `profile` as geom0019 ran them; `anchored`, with the two changes
and nothing else. geom0019's constants are not retuned.

**Cases.** kirk0 (tuning data, three reviewed vehicles, already read by geom0019) and the first
five-minute segments of ashbury-downey and haight-divisadero (read by geom0019 and l5id0019). It
is a screen on data the profile has seen.

**Rule, written before the option exists (2026-11-10).** geom0019's clauses, unchanged except P4,
whose bar was the box's own containment, which the box holds by construction.

- **P0, admitted at all.** The anchored profile reports an extent on at least 4 of truck 2's six
  poses, 5 of truck 1's nine and 4 of car 8's seven. Below that, P1 and P2 are reported as not
  read and the verdict is that admission, not the estimator, still limits it.
- **P1, long bodies.** Over truck 2's poses the anchored profile's median absolute length error is
  under the published box's (3.04 m) by 0.5 m or more, and over truck 1's no worse than the box's
  by more than 0.25 m.
- **P2, cars.** Over car 8's poses the median absolute length error is at most 0.365 m and the
  median width under 2.0 m.
- **P3, steadiness, label-free.** On each placement segment, over confirmed vehicle tracks of
  3 s or more, the median within-track spread of the profile's length after its first second
  (p90 less p10) is no more than the box's, and the share of tracks whose length falls by more
  than 0.5 m after lock-on is under 5 %.
- **P4, withheld frames, label-free, restated.** With every fifth admitted frame withheld, the
  share of the withheld frame's returns inside the anchored profile's extent at the tracked pose,
  grown by 0.25 m, is at least the solid body's own extent's share on the same frames, grown the
  same, while the profile's area is no more than 1.15 times the solid body's at the median.
- **Verdict.** A candidate when P0 to P4 hold; it then replaces the extent state as the extent arm
  in geom0016's restated gate, and a two-dimensional body-frame grid is specified as its
  successor. A fail names its clause. Nothing is adopted.

**Reported, not decided.** The share of admitted frames per arm on each case; each end's observed
or censored mark; truck 2's profile length across 70 to 80 s; the box's own P4 share beside the
solid body's.

**What could mislead.** Everything here is tuning data the profile has already read, and the two
changes were chosen after reading geom0019's failure; a pass is a reason to spend held-out data,
not a result. The box's heading is itself smoothed, so a profile admitted on it inherits its lag
through a turn. Truck 2's six poses lie within two seconds.
