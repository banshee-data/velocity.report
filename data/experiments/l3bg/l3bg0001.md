# l3bg0001: zero-code L3 arms on the labelled kirk captures

**Question.** Does narrowing L3's acceptance window (region overrides off, then a narrower base
window, then no neighbour confirmation) recover the road-user returns the shipped grid absorbs,
and at what cost? The review's Z0 step.

**Answer.** The region overrides are the lever: off, kirk0 recall goes from 59 % to 94 %, at 39
times the unlabelled clusters. No arm passes Stage A. The A2 to A4 arms (closeness 1.5, a flat
window, no neighbour confirmation) ran from scratch configs before parameter sets were required,
so only A0 and A1 are listed as arms here; the report has all five.
