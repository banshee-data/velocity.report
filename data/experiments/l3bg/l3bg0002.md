# l3bg0002: E4 support filter with the overrides off

**Question.** Does a range-image support filter (E4) remove the flood the overrides-off arm
produces, without losing the recovered road users?

**Answer.** It removes the single-cell speckle (a fifth of the unlabelled clusters) at under a
point of recall; the rest of the flood is coherent patches it cannot separate from small
objects. Report Section 8.
