# l3bg0003: which region-override tercile absorbs road users

**Question.** Is it the volatile tercile's ×8 window or the quiet terciles' ×3 and ×4 windows that
costs recall, and does a height band floor at road height remove the road returns a narrowed L3
lets through?

**Answer.** The volatile window: volatile-only recalls 0.613 against the shipped 0.587, and
quiet-only is the overrides-off arm. The floor removes every road-surface cluster for at most 0.2
points of recall. Report Section 3.
