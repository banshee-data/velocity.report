# l3bg0004: the kirk0 recall frontier with the overrides on and off

**Question.** Does switching the region overrides off move kirk0's recall and false-foreground
trade-off, or only move along it?

**Answer.** Along it. In none of 518 configs does turning the overrides off raise recall without
raising the spurious clusters, and at every budget the best overrides-on config matches or beats
the best overrides-off one. Report Section 4.
