# l3bg0007: record the region map, and test the volatile-lane mechanism

**Question.** The attribution report infers that a lane lands in the volatile tercile because
traffic passed while the grid settled. Is that what happens, and does the same tercile hold the
vegetation the overrides-off arm floods with?

**Method.** Persist the region map, replay the shipped grid on both kirk captures, and draw each
cell's tercile against where the labelled road users and the flood's clusters are.

**Success.** Either most labelled road-user cells are volatile and most flood cells are too (the
mechanism holds, and a robust settling spread is the next arm), or they are not (the report's
reading is wrong and is corrected).
