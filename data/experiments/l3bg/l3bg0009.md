# l3bg0009: Stage B for the overrides-on retune

**Question.** The review's decision rule adopts an L3 change only after a held-out score. Does
CM or CMR keep its kirk gains on a labelled window nobody tuned on, with the dwell policy in place
and the Pi's frame times within budget?

**Dependencies.** The held-out window's labels (label0001), the dwell policy (l3bg0008) and the
Pi timing (perf0001). The replay itself is short; the experiment waits on people and hardware.
