# l3bg0010: the region map's two levers on kirk

**Question.** The region map found that the volatile tercile's ×8 window holds back the
overrides-off flood but also takes the road users that pass in front of variable surfaces, and
that lanes are there for those surfaces and for region merging, not for traffic during settling.
It named two levers. Does applying the overrides by each cell's own tercile instead of its merged
region's, or bounding the volatile window in metres instead of in proportion to range, recover
road users without letting the flood through?

**Method.** Two default-off replay options, then six arms on both kirk captures: the shipped
grid, the overrides off (the flood at this commit), each lever alone (the cap at 1 m and 2 m), and
the cell tercile with the 1 m cap. Score as the attribution report did: object-frame recall by
class and unlabelled clusters a frame against kirk0's ground truth, covered road users on kirk1,
the reviewed-pack per-frame evaluator, and W3.

**Success.** A lever that moves kirk0's road-user recall well above the shipped 0.587 while its
unlabelled clusters a frame stay near the shipped 3.4, and kirk1's coverage above 38 of 49, goes
to the corpus screen (l3bg0012) with CMR and the dwell policy. If neither does, the flood and the
lanes share cells as well as regions, and the report says which surfaces.
