# l3bg0011: the dwell policy's cost on labelled road users

**Question.** On Lombard–Laguna the dwell policy removed every stationary track over 60 s and kept
1,003 of CMR's 1,045 moving tracks, but it absorbed 16 tracks classed pedestrian, and that capture
has no labels. On the two kirk captures, which do, what does it cost: which labelled road users
does it absorb, and how much object-frame recall, by class, does it take from the shipped L3 and
from CMR?

**Method.** Replay both kirk captures under the shipped defaults and CMR, each with and without
`dwell_policy`, and score them as the attribution report scored its arms. Match every absorbed
track to the labelled objects by position and time.

**Success.** A number for the cost: absorbed labelled road users by class, and the recall
difference per class, on two captures. Either it is small enough that Stage B (l3bg0009) runs with
the policy on, or the policy needs a class-aware or longer T before it does.

**Answer (October 16, 2026).** On kirk it costs no moving road user: nothing is absorbed under the
shipped L3 on kirk0, one or two bird and dynamic tracks on kirk1, and recall by class is equal or
up. Its one absorption under CMR on kirk0 is the parked car the reviewed pack labels in every
frame, which accounts for the whole per-frame drop (HOTA 0.533 to 0.494). Neither window holds a
person standing for 20 s, so the pedestrian cost stays open:
[report](../../../docs/lidar/operations/l3-dwell-policy-labelled-cost-2026-10.md).
