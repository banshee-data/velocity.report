# l3bg0013: was the Lombard–Laguna scene change a sensor pose step?

**Question.** At 33:20.6 into the Lombard–Laguna placement 45 tracks start within 0.2 s, and on
the replay every L3 arm jumps to 8.6 to 13.8 clusters a frame for the rest of the capture. The
scene-change proposal's first hypothesis is a physical nudge of the sensor; its second, a fault in
the background model's state. Does the raw geometry show a rigid pose change at the event?

**Method.** From the raw capture alone, the median range of each static cell (ring and azimuth
bin) over 10 s before and 10 s after the event, turned into points, and a robust six-degree rigid
fit between them, with the residual before and after the fit by azimuth sector and ring. The same
fit on at least ten quiet pairs elsewhere in the placement is the null. The arm is the shipped
configuration only in name: nothing here runs the pipeline.

**Success.** Either the event's transform lies well outside the quiet pairs' and removes most of
the static disagreement (the proposal's H1, so the sprint builds a pose response), or it is
within the null while the disagreement is local (H2 or H3, so the sprint looks for a model-state
or occlusion cause instead).
