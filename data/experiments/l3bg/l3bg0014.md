# l3bg0014: unrecorded knocks to the sensor across the corpus placements

**Question.** l3bg0013 found two unrecorded pose changes and a pickup in the Lombard–Laguna
placement, each of which an L3 replay reads as a scene change. Do the other placements hold any?
Every L3 experiment on the corpus replays them, and a knock inside a scored window would be read as
the background model's failure.

**Method.** `lidar-pose-step` on each placement's raw captures: in each static part of the site
index, a 10 s reference window 5 s in, compared with a 10 s window every 30 s to the part's end. A
knock shows as a step in that series of transforms; the threshold, 0.03° or 0.3 cm between
consecutive windows, is about three times the largest quiet pair at Lombard–Laguna. Lombard–Laguna
itself is the positive control. Label-free; nothing runs the pipeline.

**Success.** A list of placements with steps, their times and sizes, and whether any falls inside
the first or second five-minute segment the corpus screens score. Or a clean bill for the other 22.

**Answer (October 16, 2026).** Seven of the 23 placements hold a persistent pose change of 0.03°
to 0.27°, none recorded, and six corpus cases are scored across one: in the first segment
marina-webster-beach, embarcadero-bryant and van-ness-sacramento, in the second
haight-divisadero, marina-webster-beach and van-ness-sacramento. The site index's static parts
catch the large changes and miss the small:
[report](../../../docs/lidar/operations/l3-corpus-pose-sweep-2026-10.md).
