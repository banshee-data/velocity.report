# l3bg0016: CMR and the dwell policy on the corpus's second segment

**Question.** l3bg0012 screens the shipped L3, CMR and CMR with the dwell policy on the first
five-minute segment of 23 corpus sites. Does what it finds hold on the second segment, which no
first-segment screen has read and which the held-out geometry replication used for the same
purpose?

**Method.** The same three arms, screen and parameters as l3bg0012, and CMR with the dwell policy
at 40 s, added once l3bg0015 recommended it for Stage B, and the same with the support filter,
the candidate l3bg0018 found, on the second segment of the
22 sites that have one (ashbury-downey has a single capture; embarcadero-folsom stays held out).
Started once l3bg0012's replays end, so the two do not share the LiDAR volume's bandwidth. One
report covers both.

**Success.** The per-site and pooled directions of l3bg0012 (moving tracks, stationary tracks,
re-tracks after absorption) repeat on this segment, or the report says where they do not.
