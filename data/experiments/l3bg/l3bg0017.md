# l3bg0017: the dwell policy's drift rule at 40 s

**Question.** At 40 s the dwell policy keeps CMR's moving tracks and clears the long stationary
ones over Lombard–Laguna, but the longest stationary track is 76 s at both 40 and 60 s: the clock
restarts whenever a track's estimated position wanders more than 1 m from where its stationary run
began, which kirk0's parked car does after 34.7 s without moving. Does a 2 m tolerance, or drift
measured from the run's running median position, let the clock finish on the objects that sit
still without absorbing the ones that creep?

**Method.** CMR with the policy at 40 s under each drift rule over the whole placement, replayed
and screened as l3bg0015, against l3bg0015's 40 s arm with the 1 m first-estimate rule.

**Success.** A drift rule that brings stationary tracks over 60 s and the longest stationary
lifetime down, while moving tracks stay at CMR's and the re-track rate stays at its control's; or
the finding that the 76 s tracks are objects that do creep, so the 1 m rule stands.
