# l3bg0018: CMR with the cell tercile and the support filter

**Question.** On top of CMR the cell tercile raised kirk0's pedestrian recall from 0.76 to 0.88
but tripled the unlabelled clusters (0.52 to 1.55 a frame), at level HOTA. The support filter
(E4) removed about a fifth of the overrides-off flood, the single-cell speckle, at under a point of
recall. Does it keep the cell tercile's pedestrians while taking back its false positives?

**Method.** CMR, CMR with the support filter, CMR with the cell tercile and CMR with both, on both
kirk captures, scored as l3bg0010.

**Success.** CMR with both keeps pedestrian recall near 0.88 with unlabelled clusters near CMR's
0.52 and HOTA above CMR's, which would make it a Stage B arm; or it does not, and the levers
report says the cell tercile stays an option.

**Answer (October 16, 2026).** No. With CMR and the cell tercile the support filter removes 0.07
of 1.55 unlabelled clusters a frame and a third of the pedestrian gain (0.88 to 0.82), so the
cell tercile's false positives are not single-cell speckle. On CMR alone the filter lifts HOTA from
0.533 to 0.559 and IDF1 from 0.593 to 0.638 at six points of pedestrian recall:
[report](../../../docs/lidar/operations/l3-region-levers-kirk-2026-10.md#follow-up-the-support-filter-on-top-l3bg0018).
