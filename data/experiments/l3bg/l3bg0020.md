# l3bg0020: why a replay depends on the server's history

**Question.** perf0002 found that a replay of one capture records different tracks depending on
what the server replayed before it, and that the difference starts within the first second of
capture time. Its lane logs then showed two L3 mechanisms, both visible in every lane:

1. **The convergence state `ResetGrid` leaves in memory.** `BackgroundGrid.prevSpreads`,
   `prevRegionIDs`, `settlingCheckCounter`, `settlingAnnounced` and `LastObservedNoiseRel` are not
   cleared between replays. On a new process the first convergence check has no previous spreads
   to compare, so the spread delta is zero, and settling completes "on convergence" at frame 59
   (5.9 s). On a reused process the check compares against the previous capture's spreads
   (`spread_delta 0.71`), then settles to a real delta of 0.004 to 0.005 a check, which never meets
   the 0.001 threshold, and settling runs to the 30 s ceiling (300 frames). Every first replay on a
   lane logged `variance samples: 59`; every later one that identified regions logged `300`.
2. **The region store.** When settling completes, `persistRegionsOnSettleLocked` writes the region
   map and the grid cells to `lidar_bg_regions` and `lidar_bg_snapshot`, keyed by the replay's
   source path. Ten frames into any later replay of the same capture against the same database,
   `tryRestoreRegionsFromStoreLocked` restores both and marks settling complete, so that replay
   runs the earlier replay's regions and background from frame 10, whatever its own tuning. Every
   repeat of a capture on a lane logged `Restored 50 regions from snapshot ... by source_path`.

Read that way, the sweep's outcomes per site are four, not many: a new process identifying its
own regions; a reused process identifying them at the ceiling; a restore of the first kind of
snapshot; and a restore of the second (or of one made under the substituted L3). Does each
mechanism, alone, move a replay off the fresh result, and does the store pre-empt the memory?

**Arms.** The four server histories above, each one analysis replay of each case under the
defaults. `fresh` is the reference and repeats perf0002's identical pair. `store-only` is made by
replaying the case on a fresh process, stopping the server, and starting a new process on the
same database. `memory-only` replays the other site first on an empty database. `both` replays the
other site and then the case on a database already holding the case's snapshot.

**Rule, written before the replays (2026-10-18).** Each prediction below is checked on both
cases, by the row-for-row comparison and by the server's debug log:

1. `fresh` repeats perf0002's fresh result for the case (embarcadero-bay 858 confirmed tracks,
   ashbury-downey 382) and settles on convergence at about frame 59.
2. `store-only` differs from `fresh`, logs a restore by source path at about frame 10 with no
   settling lines, and matches the sweep's restore-of-a-fresh-snapshot outcome (embarcadero-bay
   876 tracks, ashbury-downey 385).
3. `memory-only` differs from `fresh`, logs `not converged` lines and `Settling complete ... after
30.0s`, and matches the sweep's reused-process outcome (embarcadero-bay 774, ashbury-downey
   313).
4. `both` matches `store-only`: the restore at frame 10 pre-empts settling.

A mechanism is confirmed as a cause when its arm differs from `fresh` with its predicted log
signature. A prediction that fails names a third mechanism, and l3bg0021 waits for it. Whether the
two are the whole of it is l3bg0021's determinism step, not this experiment.

**What could mislead.** The restore falls back to matching the grid's scene signature when no
snapshot matches the path, so a `memory-only` arm could restore a snapshot of the other site if
the signatures collide; the log says which path was taken. The store is the server's database:
each arm's database is named in the run notes and deleted or kept as the arm requires.
