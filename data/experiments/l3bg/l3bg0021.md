# l3bg0021: a replay that starts from nothing

**Question.** l3bg0020 names two L3 states that make a replay depend on the server's history: the
convergence state `ResetGrid` leaves in memory, and the region snapshot a settled replay persists
and the next replay of that capture restores. If both are cleared, does any sequence of replays on
one server reproduce, row for row, a replay on a new process? And which semantics should the
first convergence check have, now that it has to be chosen rather than fall out of an
uninitialised slice?

**The change.** Three parts, each a Go change and none a tuning key, so the defaults fingerprint
and the perf baselines do not move:

- **(a) The reset.** `ResetGrid` clears the five fields the convergence evaluation and its
  diagnostics keep on the grid.
- **(b) The store.** A PCAP replay runs from its own packets: it does not read a region snapshot
  by source path or grid hash at frame 10, and does not write one when it settles, unless the
  start request asks with `restore_regions`. Live input keeps both, which is what the store is
  for: a sensor restarted on a scene it knows skips settling. `settle_before_recording` keeps its
  explicit reload of the grid it just settled.
- **(c) The first check.** Today a new process converges at its first check because the spread
  delta against no previous spreads is zero. After (a) every replay would do the same, which is
  deterministic but not convergence. The two semantics are arms: `honest` treats an undefined
  delta as not converged, so a replay settles on real convergence or at the 30 s ceiling;
  `first-check` keeps today's new-process behaviour, written down: the first check after the
  frame minimum passes on coverage and confidence alone.

**Rule, written before the replays (2026-10-18).**

1. **Determinism**, required of both arms: on one server, never restarted, with the old code's
   snapshots for both captures already in its database, every analysis replay of a capture in the
   interleaved sequence is identical row for row to every other replay of it and to one on a new
   process, and the 0.5x replay is identical to them too. The debug log
   shows no restore and no persist for any replay. An arm that fails this has not cleared the
   state, and the report says which replay differed and from what frame.
2. **Which semantics**, decided on kirk0 and kirk1 against the per-frame ground truth: `honest`
   is adopted unless its HOTA is more than 0.01 below `first-check`'s on either capture; then
   `first-check` is adopted and l3bg0022 asks why the honest criterion is not met.
3. (a) and (b) are adopted when clause 1 holds for the adopted arm. The live server's semantics
   are a decision entry, not this rule's: the change is to a deployed device's start-up.

**What could mislead.** The old snapshots in the database are the test: a database without them
would pass clause 1 by accident. kirk0's labels were reviewed on tracks from a new process, so
`first-check` is the semantics those labels saw; a HOTA difference between the arms is a
difference in settling time (6 s against 30 s) as well as in criterion, and the report separates
the two by the frame at which each arm's regions were identified.

## Answer (2026-10-19)

A replay now starts from nothing. With `ResetGrid` clearing the convergence state, the region
store kept out of PCAP replays (a settling pass persists its own), and, beyond the spec, a replay's
frames ending only on their azimuth wrap, every replay of an interleaved sequence on one server
that was never restarted, on a database holding both captures' old snapshots, is identical row
for row to a new process, under both first-check semantics. `honest` scores HOTA 0.363 on kirk0
against 0.376 for `first-check`, more than 0.01 below, so `first-check` stays; the live server's
start-up is therefore unchanged. The `restore_regions` option is left out. Report:
[replay determinism](../../../docs/lidar/operations/replay-determinism-2026-10.md).
