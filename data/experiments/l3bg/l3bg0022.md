# l3bg0022: does the background converge by its own criterion?

**Question.** With the convergence state reset honestly (l3bg0021), perf0002's logs say the
spread delta settles to 0.004 to 0.005 a check and never reaches `settling_max_spread_delta`
0.001, so every replay waits out the 30 s ceiling and the criterion decides nothing. Is 0.001 a
threshold no scene meets, or do some scenes converge below it given time? What threshold would
let the scenes that have converged stop waiting, and does stopping earlier cost anything on the
labelled captures?

**Method.** Every first segment of the corpus replayed once with each convergence evaluation
logged until settling completes. From the 23 trajectories: the delta at which each scene's
trajectory flattens, the share of scenes that would have converged before 30 s at 0.001, 0.005 and
0.01, and whether a delta relative to the mean spread separates settled scenes from unsettled ones
better than an absolute one. The thresholds are then scored on kirk0 and kirk1.

**Rule, written before the replays (2026-10-18).** A threshold is proposed as the default when at
least 18 of the 23 scenes converge before the ceiling under it, no scene converges before the
50-frame minimum, and kirk0's and kirk1's HOTA under it are within 0.01 of the ceiling's. If no
absolute threshold meets that, the report says what the trajectories look like and proposes the
relative form as a further spec rather than a change. The criterion is tuning
(`settling_max_spread_delta`), so a change moves the defaults fingerprint and recaptures the perf
baselines in the same PR.

**How the rule is read, fixed before any replay (2026-11-03).** The rule above stands as written;
this says how each clause is measured on arrow-worker, where the run is made.

- **Trajectories.** `lidar-state-estimation-baseline -settling-trajectory` (new: each convergence
  evaluation's elapsed time, coverage, spread delta, region stability and mean confidence, written
  per case and checked equal on the repeat) with `settling_honest`, on the first segment of the 23
  sites listed, 120 s warm-up, 10 s scored, `-require-settled=false`, from
  [`l3-settling-trajectory.json`](../../../config/experiments/l3-settling-trajectory.json): the
  defaults with the ceiling raised to 120 s, so a scene's trajectory continues past 30 s. A check
  is the same computation whatever the ceiling, so every check before 30 s is the one the
  defaults' ceiling would make; the checks after it only answer the question's "given time". The ceiling is 120 s rather than longer because arrow-worker,
  shared with another lane, replays these segments at about real time with the repeat run; a
  smoke replay of one site at 240 s, before this change, is not read.
- **Converges before the ceiling under a threshold T**: some check before 30 s elapsed is measured
  (honest) and meets coverage 0.8, region stability 0.95 and mean confidence 10 (the defaults'
  other three criteria, unchanged in every arm) with spread delta at most T. Read for T = 0.001,
  0.005 and 0.01 from the same trajectory; the arms' kirk replays check that each arm settles
  where its trajectory says.
- **Converges before the 50-frame minimum.** Convergence is not evaluated before the minimum, so
  read literally the clause cannot fail. It is read as what it guards against, a threshold so
  loose it ends warm-up at the first moment it is allowed: a scene fails it when its first
  converged check under T is the first measured check after the minimum.
- **HOTA within 0.01 of the ceiling's**: kirk0 whole, 6 s warm-up, `lidar-ground-truth-eval
perframe` against the reviewed pack ad8b9438 (version 2), split `kirk0-tuning`, ignoring the
  parked car `obj_788622b5`, each arm against `ceiling`. Read as a cost bound: an arm passes when
  its HOTA is no more than 0.01 below the ceiling's; the report also gives the two-sided reading.
- **kirk1** has no per-frame ground truth (l3bg0021), so its HOTA cannot be computed. Its clause is
  read on the stand-in l3bg0021 used: of kirk1's 49 labelled road users (reference 60a4774c), the
  arm covers no fewer than the ceiling does (a track overlapping 30 % of the reference's span), on
  the 270 s trim at 6 s warm-up. The report says the HOTA clause was not measurable on kirk1.
- **No default changes in this run.** The owner asked that no default change without their say:
  a threshold that passes is recommended in the report and left off, so this PR moves no
  fingerprint and recaptures no baseline.

**Answer (November 3, 2026).** No absolute threshold passes. Under 0.001 no site converges before
30 s, or by 120 s; under 0.005, 3 of 23, all at the first measured check; under 0.01, 15, 7 of them
at the first measured check. The spread delta holds each site's own level (0.003 to 0.05) and
tracks traffic rather than falling. On kirk, 0.01 settles both captures at 6.9 s and costs nothing
(kirk0 HOTA 0.3936 to 0.4269; kirk1 41 to 49 of 49 covered). The delta relative to the mean spread
separates better and is proposed as [l3bg0030](l3bg0030.md). See the
[report](../../../docs/lidar/operations/settling-convergence-corpus-2026-11.md).
