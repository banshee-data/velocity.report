# l3bg0022: does the background converge by its own criterion?

**Question.** With the convergence state reset honestly (l3bg0021), perf0002's logs say the
spread delta settles to 0.004 to 0.005 a check and never reaches `settling_max_spread_delta`
0.001, so every replay waits out the 30 s ceiling and the criterion decides nothing. Is 0.001 a
threshold no scene meets, or do some scenes converge below it given time? What threshold would
let the scenes that have converged stop waiting, and does stopping earlier cost anything on the
labelled captures?

**Method.** Every first segment of the corpus replayed once with each convergence evaluation
logged until settling completes. From the 23 trajectories: the delta at which each scene's
trajectory flattens, the share of scenes that would have converged before 30 s at 0.001, 0.005 and
0.01, and whether a delta relative to the mean spread separates settled scenes from unsettled ones
better than an absolute one. The thresholds are then scored on kirk0 and kirk1.

**Rule, written before the replays (2026-10-18).** A threshold is proposed as the default when at
least 18 of the 23 scenes converge before the ceiling under it, no scene converges before the
50-frame minimum, and kirk0's and kirk1's HOTA under it are within 0.01 of the ceiling's. If no
absolute threshold meets that, the report says what the trajectories look like and proposes the
relative form as a further spec rather than a change. The criterion is tuning
(`settling_max_spread_delta`), so a change moves the defaults fingerprint and recaptures the perf
baselines in the same PR.
