# l3bg0024: where a vehicle's lower body goes

**Question.** The published track's `z` is the cluster's lowest return, and with range that
return climbs up the body. On the Columbus–Broadway scene (0.5.1-pre45), against a road taken as
the tenth percentile of the settled background in 2 m cells, moving vehicles' lowest return sits
a median 0.43 m above the road within 10 m of the sensor, 1.62 m at 20–30 m and 2.25 m at 50–60
m, while their top holds at about 3 m and their drawn height falls from 1.5 m to 0.4 m. kirk0
replayed through lidar-box-eval with `box_ground` (2026-10-18, development evidence) gives the
same shape against the fitted surface: 0.56 m within 10 m, 1.49 m at 10–20 m, 1.85 m at 20–30 m,
2.09 m at 30–40 m. The lower body leaves the foreground with range.

The beam-wise review derived an absorbed band from the L3 closeness test: both the band and the
vehicle subtend angles in proportion to 1/r, so the band takes a fixed height, about 0.45 m on a
settled cell and 1.1 m on one held at the confidence floor, at every range. A fixed band does not
climb with range. So either the band is wider at range than the derivation allows (cells drained
to the floor by traffic, the review's mechanism 2), or something else takes the lower body: a
nearer object occluding it (parked cars between the sensor and the far lane), returns that never
come back from dark lower panels and tyres at grazing incidence, or the L4 clip.

Which it is decides the remedy. Absorption is L3's and a window change can return the lower body;
occlusion and dropout are the scene's and the sensor's, which no L3 change can return, and the
remedy for them is the box stood on the surface with a held height (ground0004), which this spec
does not test.

**Measures.** Per confirmed vehicle-sized track frame, by 10 m range bin, per site and pooled:

1. The lowest foreground return's height above the blended surface: p10, p50, p90.
2. For the rings that reach the body below that return, the share of their returns in the box's
   azimuth span that were absorbed, occluded, dropped or clipped, as the implement step defines.
3. Under `closeness-2` against `pre-consolidation`: the change in (1) beyond 20 m, and the
   screen's spurious clusters per frame and confirmed vehicle tracks.
4. The same for `defaults`, the L3 shipped since l3bg0025: closeness 2 with a safety margin of
   0.3 m, which adds 0.15 m to the window at every range, and neighbour confirmation 5. It is
   read beside the other two and decides nothing in the reading below.

The arms were revised on 2026-10-19, before any replay. The spec was written against defaults of
closeness 3, and l3bg0025 then moved the defaults to closeness 2, so `a0` became
`pre-consolidation`, the L3 it was written against, and `defaults` was added. The reading is
unchanged.

**Reading, written before any replay (2026-10-18).**

- If absorption is the largest share beyond 20 m on most sites, the band is wider at range than
  the derivation allows, and `closeness-2` must lower the lowest return's p50 above the surface
  beyond 20 m by 0.3 m or more without raising spurious clusters per frame beyond l3bg0023's
  bound; if it does, the window's shape (flat against proportional to range, the review's claim
  C1) is the next spec.
- If occlusion or dropout is the largest share, no L3 window returns the lower body, and the
  base of a published box belongs to the surface: ground0004's `box-ground` arm is then the
  remedy, and this spec records why.
- A site where clipping is more than a tenth of the share has a floor above its road, which is
  ground0001's departure table and ground0002's clip.

**Definitions fixed with the instrument (2026-10-26), before the replays.**

- _A track frame_ is a scored frame of a confirmed track observed in it (no misses), moving at
  2 m/s or more, whose published box is 3 m long or more, binned by the box centre's horizontal
  range in 10 m bins (the last from 70 m on); "beyond 20 m" pools every frame from 20 m.
- _A ring-slot_ is an azimuth slot of the L3 grid (0.2°) whose ray from the sensor enters the
  box's footprint, on a ring whose beam meets the footprint's near face, where the ray enters it,
  above the blended surface there and below the track's lowest foreground return: the height at
  which the body should have returned something. It is read from the slot's nearest return.
- _The classes._ The spec's four, from the slot's nearest return: **absorbed**, background between
  the near face less the L3 closeness window and the far side plus it; **occluded**, background
  nearer than the near face by more than the window; **dropout**, no return; **clipped**,
  foreground below L4's height-band floor. Two more are counted rather than forced into those:
  **behind**, background beyond the far side by more than the window (the beam passed under or
  beside the body), and **foreground**, foreground above the floor (noise, or another cluster).
  The window is `ClosenessThresholdMetres` with the arm's global L3 parameters and a spread of 0,
  the settled cell's window that the review's derivation uses, at the return's range; region
  overrides and a cell's own spread are not read. The classes are read from L3's own mask, before
  the track guard publishes the returns it holds inside a tracked box as foreground; the slots the
  guard so publishes are counted beside the classes as `guard_held`.
- _The reading's "largest share"_ compares the spec's four classes only; `behind` and `foreground`
  are reported beside them and decide nothing.
- _The shares_ are of ring-slots, per bin, per site and pooled over sites by summing slots.
- _l3bg0023's bound._ l3bg0023 states no bound on spurious clusters. Its corpus tolerances are
  applied to them instead: `closeness-2`'s spurious clusters a frame within 10 % of
  `pre-consolidation`'s pooled, and no site more than 25 % above. Spurious clusters and vehicle
  tracks are l4clus0004's label-free readings of l3bg0023's screen.
- The replays are 200 s after a 70 s warm-up on the first segments, `-variants none` (the
  published box only), with an evidence database for the screen; kirk0 is replayed whole after a
  20 s warm-up.

**What could mislead.** The surface is the sensor's own returns: a site whose fit sits below the
road counts every return as higher than it is, which ground0001's table checks. A ring reaching
the footprint's near edge may strike the road in front of the body, not the body, so the block
counts rings between the near and far edges' heights only. A parked vehicle held as background is
absorbed by design; confirmed moving tracks only are counted for the shares.

**What it does not establish.** A recall gain: the lower body is a geometry question here, not a
detection one. Anything about pedestrians, whose lower legs the review puts in the band at every
range. Anything held out.
