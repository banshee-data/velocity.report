# l3bg0025: the best L3 currently known, for the defaults

**Question.** l3bg0023 found that the defaults' L3 loses about half the vehicle tracks the
once-substituted replay L3 finds, and that the substituted L3 brings five times the spurious
clusters on kirk0. The decision is to run one L3 everywhere and make it the best currently known.
CMR (closeness 2.0, post-settle update 0.005, minimum cluster size 8, height-band floor −2.0) is
the retune built to take back closeness 2's noise, and it has never been scored beside the
substituted L3 on one build. Which of the substituted L3, CMR, and CMR with the substituted L3's
safety margin and neighbour confirmation (the two keys that held its identity switches down)
should the defaults carry?

**Rule, written before the replays (2026-10-19).**

1. **Eligible** on the labels: an arm whose kirk0 HOTA and IDF1 are each within 0.02 of the highest
   of the three, whose kirk0 car recall is within 3 points of the highest, and which covers kirk1's
   references within 2 of the most.
2. **Chosen:** among the eligible, the one with the fewest kirk0 spurious clusters a frame; within
   0.05 of each other, the one with fewer keys changed against today's defaults.
3. **Confirmed on the corpus,** unless it is `replay-l3`, whose first-segment screen l3bg0023
   holds: on the first segment of the same 23 sites the chosen arm must keep pooled vehicle-sized
   tracks and vehicle track life p50 within 10 % of `replay-l3`'s, with no more stationary tracks
   over 60 s. If it does not, the next eligible arm by clause 2 is taken, down to `replay-l3`.
4. The chosen arm's keys become the defaults, with the parameter sets, profiles, characterisation
   tests, coverage survey and perf baselines brought to them in the same change.

**What could mislead.** kirk is one site, and its split is the tuning split; CMR was chosen on
kirk0's sweep, which favours it. The corpus confirmation is label-free. Live timing on the Pi is
not measured here; the change doubles clusters a frame against today's defaults whichever arm is
chosen.

## Answer (2026-10-19)

The substituted replay L3 (closeness 2.0, margin 0.3 m, neighbour confirmation 5) is the only
eligible arm: HOTA 0.542 and IDF1 0.656 on kirk0, against CMR's 0.533 and 0.593 and CMR with the
margin and neighbour keys' 0.536 and 0.621. The CMR arms have a third of its spurious clusters
(0.52 against 1.64 a frame) but nearly twice its identity switches. Its corpus screen is
l3bg0023's, so the corpus step did not run. The defaults now carry it. Report:
[one L3 for every replay](../../../docs/lidar/operations/replay-l3-consolidation-2026-10.md#l3bg0025-the-best-l3-currently-known).
