# l3bg0026: a boundary vote on the L3 grid

**Question.** The L3 decision is one cell at a time: a return is foreground when it falls outside
its cell's acceptance window and enough same-ring neighbours agree. The window is model-imposed
(gap analysis B7, B8) and reaches about 5 m at 20 m under the region overrides, so the lowest
returns on a body at range fall inside it and are absorbed as background: under CMR, 13 % of the
returns in kirk0's 20 to 35 m gaps between a truck's fragments were L3 background
([Stage B return fate](../../../docs/lidar/operations/w3-return-fate-stage-b-kirk0-2026-10.md)).
Every widening of the window that would admit them costs settling and spurious clusters across the
corpus, and no narrowing has cost anything measured. The jump-convolution pass in
LiDAR-Processing-V2 decides the ground-to-object boundary differently: only the cells on the
dilated edge of the object are reconsidered, each by a vote of its ring-and-bin neighbours weighted
by the 3-D distance between the returns, and the vote can go either way
([review](../../../docs/lidar/operations/lidar-processing-v2-segmentation-review.md#boundary-refinement-the-jump-convolution-pass)).
On our grid, does that vote recover absorbed body returns at the edge without admitting the road
beside the contact patch, and what does it cost a frame?

**What is already known.** The support filter (`support_filter`, the beam-wise review's E4) is
the one neighbour rule on the grid today: a count over 5 rings by 21 bins with a range tolerance,
able only to turn foreground into background. It is the `support` arm so the two rules are read
side by side and together (`both`). Range-adaptive ε at L4 did not join the 20 to 35 m fragments
([l4clus0001](../l4clus/l4clus0001.md)), and a lower core threshold added fragments, not bodies
([l4clus0003](../l4clus/l4clus0003.md)); the 72 % of gap returns that were DBSCAN noise are L4's
and this experiment cannot reach them. The arms differ only in replay experiments; the tuning
file is the defaults' in every arm, so no parameter set is added.

**Rule, written before the replays (2026-10-23).**

1. **Retained** if on kirk0 the `vote` arm's W3 retention in 20 to 35 m is at least five points
   above `a0`'s with fragmented instants in that band no higher; its spurious clusters a frame are
   no more than 1.1 × `a0`'s at every site of the screen; its per-frame HOTA is no lower than
   `a0`'s less 0.005; and its frame-time p99 is no more than 1.1 × `a0`'s.
2. **Pulling the road in** if retention rises and spurious clusters a frame exceed 1.1 × `a0`'s
   at three or more sites, or fragmented instants rise with retention. The direction counts say
   which way the flips went. The next spec then restricts the vote to the background-to-foreground
   direction, above the surface clip, and this one closes with that as its answer.
3. **Nothing at the edge** if retention moves by under two points and the background-to-foreground
   flips are under 1 % of foreground returns. The absorbed returns are then not within a metre of
   confirmed foreground on neighbouring rings, and the W3 rule for dropped returns needs a
   different mechanism; the report says what the flipped returns were.
4. `both` is read against `support`: a `both` that keeps `vote`'s retention and `support`'s
   spurious-cluster count is the pairing to carry forward; one that loses either is not.

A retained arm is not a default. It moves the L3 fingerprint, needs recaptured perf baselines and
reopens G-GEO-1; a frozen labelled holdout and the Pi's frame budget, with p95, p99 and overruns,
come before any default change.

**What could mislead.** kirk is one site and its split is the tuning split. The two reviewed
trucks are the only mid-range fragment cases with point masks, so a retention gain there is a
screen, not a population. The vote reads the labels before the pass, so it cannot propagate more
than one window from a decided cell; a body whose absorbed band is wider than two rings is out of
its reach by construction, and the report should say how wide the absorbed bands were. Frame-time
is a Mac figure.

**What it does not establish.** Any change to the background model or its window; the ground
boundary under a vehicle, which is L4's surface clip (ground0002); recall of distant objects, which
the corpus has no labels for; and whether a different kernel scale or window would serve better,
since neither is swept.
