# l3bg0027: the track guard's held returns to L4

**Question.** On 23 corpus sites, beyond 20 m, two thirds of the ring-slots that should have met a
moving vehicle below its lowest foreground return came back as background at the body's own range,
and 58 % of them were returns the track guard already publishes as foreground inside the tracked
box ([l3bg0024](l3bg0024.md)). A client sees the lower body; the cluster, and so the box's base,
does not. Narrowing L3's window returns 0.41 m of it at 1.44 × the spurious clusters. Does handing
the guard's held returns to L4 return the lower body to the cluster without that cost?

**Why the held returns.** They are inside the box of a track that was moving and confirmed the
frame before, so they are the one part of the background L3 already has reason to doubt, and no
new geometry decides them. The edge-follow part of the guard's foreground needs the frame's own
clusters and cannot reach L4 in the same frame; the block's split says how much of the 58 % that
is. l3bg0026's boundary vote is the L3-side alternative for the same returns, by 3-D distance to
their neighbours; this arm is the cheaper test of whether the returns are worth having.

**Rule, proposed with the spec (to be fixed on approval, before any replay).**

1. **The target.** `held` lowers the lowest return's p50 above the surface beyond 20 m by 0.3 m or
   more against `defaults`, median over the 23 sites, and does so at 15 or more of them.
2. **Non-inferiority, every bound at once.** Spurious clusters a frame within 1.1 × `defaults`'s
   pooled and 1.25 × at every site; vehicle tracks within 10 % pooled and 25 % at every site;
   duplicate pairs no more than 2 per hundred vehicle tracks above `defaults`'s; on kirk0, the
   lowest return's p50 beyond 20 m lowered and no new duplicate pair.
3. **Held out.** A candidate is read once on the second segment of 22 sites (`d2-second-segment`)
   and must keep half its p50 gain there under the same bounds.
4. **Then.** A candidate that passes is still not a default: the Pi's frame time with held returns
   in L4's input is a perf spec, and the box's base against kirk0's physical references is
   ground0004's comparison to make.

**What could mislead.** A held return is inside a box drawn the frame before, so at speed the box
lags the body and some held returns are the road or a neighbour behind it: the block's `behind`
and `foreground` classes, and the duplicate pairs, read that. A track that gains its lower body
grows taller, which moves its class (l6cls) and its box's height: the screen's vehicle tracks and
kirk0 read the first, ground0004 the second.

**What it does not establish.** Recall, pedestrians, or anything about a track's first frame,
which has no held box.
