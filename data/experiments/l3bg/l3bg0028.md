# l3bg0028: border classes on the L3 grid

**Question.** A fixed sensor knows its rays. Assigning each cell of the ring-by-azimuth grid
directional obstacle, shadow and veil traits, as PCL's range-image border extractor does, supplies
candidates for three separate questions: which extent ends have physical or occlusion support,
whether a predicted footprint is hidden or seen through, and whether withholding silhouette
returns helps. The traits alone establish none of those physical labels. [OQ-12](../../../docs/lidar/operations/pcl-return-fate-border-contract.md)
reuses the publication tap for diagnostics while separating lower-body fates and earlier replay
interventions. On kirk0, does withholding veil returns narrow clusters without losing reviewed
returns, and does footprint evidence keep true tracks through occlusion and end ghosts sooner?

**Rule, written before the replays (2026-10-27).** `veil` passes if W3 retention of reviewed
returns falls by no more than one point in any range band and the reviewed masks' extent ends are
labelled physical or shadow correctly at 85 % or more of ends a person can judge. `coast` passes
if fragments per reviewed vehicle fall by 10 % or more against `a0`, identity switches do not
rise, HOTA and IDF1 are each within 0.01 of `a0`, and the full-callback p99 rises by no more than
2 ms on the M1. A pass on kirk0 is a tuning screen: it proposes the evidence for the v0.5.6
occlusion continuity acceptance and changes no default.

**What it does not establish.** Held-out behaviour, which label0001 owns; Pi cost, which the
v0.5.9 field gates own; whether the labels are right where nobody reviewed the ends.
