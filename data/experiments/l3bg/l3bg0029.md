# l3bg0029: foreground refinement

**Question.** The full frames of run 95f63441 show objects part foreground and part background, and
background painted foreground as objects pass. Is that the classifier, and do rules that are not a
window depth fix it? The [levers report](../../../docs/lidar/operations/l3-region-levers-kirk-2026-10.md)
found no window depth separates the flood from road users, so this asks about other evidence: a
return beyond the learned surface cannot be an object in front of it; a return in front of its
surface that connects to foreground is that object; a connected patch new to its cells is an
object although no window rejected it.

**Measures.** Labels on kirk0 cover only the objects a reviewer selected, so false positives
against them count real unlabelled road users. The rule therefore reads recall and identity from
the labels and precision from label-free evidence. All label-free measures run against an oracle
built per capture with no L3 state: each cell's persistent surfaces over the capture, a surface
being a range the cell returns from in at least 8 % of frames. Structure paint is published
foreground on a surface present in at least half the frames, which leaves out a vehicle that stood
for a while: that is an object, and the oracle cannot say otherwise. Coherent transient recall is
the share of returns in front of every persistent surface, with three such neighbours, that L3
keeps. Tracks are confirmed tracks: moving if they travel at least 5 m in at least 1 s, stationary if under 2 m.
Medoid jitter is the RMS residual of a measured medoid from a quadratic fit to its ten neighbours.

**Rule, written before the held-out read.** The refine arm is compared with the defaults on the five
held-out placements, as the mean of per-site ratios.

- **Target:** coherent transient recall up by at least 0.15, absolute; moving-track seconds up by
  at least 20 %; structure paint per frame down by at least 30 %. At least four of the five sites
  must improve on moving-track seconds and on structure paint.
- **Non-inferiority:** stationary tracks at most +10 %; unassociated clusters a frame at most
  +15 %; median and p90 medoid jitter no higher.
- **Labels, kirk0:** scored with the parked car `obj_788622b5` left out (`-ignore-objects`): it is
  labelled in all 773 frames, and a background model that is right keeps it out, as the W3 report
  said. ID switches no higher, false negatives at most +10 %, W3 fragmented instants no higher,
  identity recall no lower than 0.02 under the defaults' and pedestrian retention no lower than
  0.10 under them. HOTA, MOTA and false positives are reported and not gated, because the pack's
  partial coverage makes them fall when real unlabelled objects are found; the report shows that
  attribution.
- **Cost:** mean replay wall time at most +10 % on the Mac.

**Adoption.** A pass does not move the defaults by itself: judging the labelled precision needs a
complete label set, or the decision to accept the motion evidence, and a Pi needs timing it
(perf0001). The report recommends; the parameter set and the replay option stay off by default.

**Answer (November 1, 2026).** Every quality clause passes on the held-out placements: recall +0.29,
moving-track time ×1.97, structure paint ×0.12, stationary tracks ×0.68, medoid jitter ×0.87 and
p90 ×0.81. kirk0 ID switches fall from 440 to 117. The replay cost clause fails, at +5 to +20 %
against a bound of +10 %. The defaults stay as they are; adoption needs a decision, Pi timing and a
perf baseline recapture:
[report](../../../docs/lidar/operations/l3-foreground-refinement-2026-10.md).
