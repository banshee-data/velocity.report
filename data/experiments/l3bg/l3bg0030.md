# l3bg0030: a convergence criterion relative to the scene's spread

**Question.** l3bg0022 found the absolute spread delta measures traffic, not convergence: it holds
each site's own level, from 0.003 to 0.05, and no absolute threshold lets most scenes stop before
the 30 s ceiling without stopping them at the first moment allowed. The delta relative to the mean
spread of the observed cells varies far less by site (medians 0.047 to 0.084). On the first
segments l3bg0022 read, a ratio of 0.075 had 19 of 23 sites converge before 30 s, none at the first
measured check, at a median of 16.9 s. That ratio was chosen after looking. Does it hold on
segments not read, and what does it cost on labelled captures?

**Rule, fixed by the
[November 5 review](../../../docs/lidar/operations/experiment-review-2026-11-05.md) before any
held-out segment is read (2026-11-05).** The ratio is 0.075, read off l3bg0022's
first-segment trajectories (19 of 23 sites before 30 s, none at the first measured check, median
16.9 s); it is not tuned again. The held-out set is the second segment of the 22 sites, which no
settling measure has read. `ratio` is proposed as the default criterion when all of these hold:

1. 18 or more of the 22 held-out sites converge before the 30 s ceiling under the ratio, and none
   at the first measured check (l3bg0022's reading of the 50-frame clause);
2. on kirk0, HOTA under `ratio` is no more than 0.01 below the `ceiling` arm's, and kirk1 covers no
   fewer of its 49 labelled road users;
3. the downstream cost is reported and does not gate: at each held-out site, spurious clusters a
   frame in the 30 s after the criterion settles, against the same site's 60 to 120 s after it.
   A site whose first 30 s run 1.5 × or more above its steady state is named: the criterion stopped
   on a quiet moment, not a settled background, and the report says how many such sites there are.

A pass is a recommendation; the owner decides the default, which moves the fingerprint and
recaptures the perf baselines in the same PR. The region-stability criterion reads 1.0 at every
warm-up check and is not part of this rule; dropping or redefining it is engineering the review
lists separately.

**What could mislead.** The second segments follow the first in time at most sites, so their
traffic resembles it; this is held out for settling, not for a scene the sensor has never seen.
The spurious-cluster read is label-free and counts fragments of movers as well as background
paint.

**kirk0 read (2026-11-05, cloud run, not the answer).** The option is built
(`settling_relative_0075`). kirk0's ratio of spread delta to mean spread never falls under 0.075
before the ceiling (lowest 0.076, median 0.104), so the arm settles at 30 s, as `ceiling` does, and
scores the same (HOTA 0.3921): clause 2 holds. The held-out corpus clauses are unread. See the
[report](../../../docs/lidar/operations/cloud-run-kirk0-labelled-2026-11.md#l3bg0030-on-kirk0).
