# l4clus0002: the DBSCAN input cap under CMR

**Question.** geom0004 found that once CMR keeps a near truck's returns, the largest remaining
loss is L4's input cap: above 8,000 foreground points a frame, DBSCAN sees a uniform random
subsample, and the cap drops 17% of the trucks' reviewed returns and half the returns lying in
the gaps between CMR's fragments inside 20 m. Does raising the cap join those fragments, and at
what cost in frame time?

**Method.** CMR with the cap at 8,000 (as shipped), 16,000 and 100,000 (above any kirk frame's
foreground), on both kirk captures, scored as l3bg0011 scored its arms, and the fate of every
reviewed return on kirk0. Frame times come from `-campaign-metrics` on the Mac with other lanes
running, so they are read as relative only.

**Success.** Fragmentation inside 20 m and on the trucks falls toward the share the cap explains,
with recall and spurious clusters level, or it does not and the cap is not what splits them. The
frame-time cost is stated against perf0001's Pi budget as the open question it is.
