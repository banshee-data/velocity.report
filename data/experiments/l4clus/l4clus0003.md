# l4clus0003: CMR's minimum cluster size against the mid-range fragments

**Question.** CMR's minimum cluster size of 8 was chosen to curb spurious clusters, and the
return-fate report found it is where the 20 to 35 m fragments now break. Zero code: the two
parameter sets exist (CR at the default minimum, CMR at 8). On the two labelled kirk captures,
does the default minimum join the mid-range fragments, and at what cost in unlabelled clusters?

**Rule, written before the replays (2026-10-18).** The minimum of 8 stays in the Stage B arms
unless, on both kirk captures, fragmented instants at 20 to 35 m fall by five points or more
under `cr` with the unlabelled clusters a frame no more than 1.5 × `cmr`'s and HOTA no lower than
`cmr`'s less 0.01. If it passes, l3bg0009 gains `cr` as an arm; it changes no default itself,
and the fragments that remain are read against l3bg0019's fate.

**What it does not establish.** Anything on the corpus, where the spurious-cluster cost was
measured label-free; the Pi's frame time, which perf0001 owns.
