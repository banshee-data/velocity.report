# l4clus0004: the subsampled frames, with the threshold rescaled

**Question.** Above 8,000 foreground points a frame, DBSCAN clusters a uniform subsample while
`MinPts` stays at 5, so every neighbourhood's expected count falls by the kept fraction f and the
busiest frames cluster at an effective threshold of 5/f (gap analysis D6). The rescaled threshold
(`density_cap`, round(MinPts·f) floored at 2) is built and unmeasured. The maths decision review
(E-06) chose to measure it before any voxel stage. How many corpus frames exceed the cap, and on
those sites does rescaling recover what removing the cap recovers, without the frame-time tail
that removing it costs?

**What is already known.** On kirk0 under CMR, removing the cap recovered 10 points of W3
retention but moved recall by 0.002 and HOTA by 0.001, and took the frame-time p99 from 48 ms to
129 ms ([l4clus0002](l4clus0002.md)). kirk1 never reaches the cap. So `nocap` is the bound on
what `density` can recover, and kirk alone is expected to show almost nothing.

**Rule, written before the replays (2026-10-22).**

1. **Immaterial at the shipped cap** if under `a0` no site has 1 % or more of its frames over the
   cap, or if `nocap`'s change in confirmed vehicle tracks is inside the corpus's run-to-run
   band at every site that does. D6 then closes at the shipped cap, and no voxel stage is built
   for it.
2. **Otherwise `density` is a candidate** if, across the sites with 1 % or more frames over the
   cap, its median change in confirmed vehicle tracks is at least half of `nocap`'s, its spurious
   clusters a frame stay within 1.1 × `a0`'s and its frame p99 within 1.1 × `a0`'s at every site,
   and kirk0's per-frame HOTA is no lower than `a0`'s less 0.005.
3. **If `nocap` gains and `density` does not,** the loss is the dropped points, not the
   threshold, and the voxel stage of clustering maths §3 is the next question, with its own spec.

A candidate is not a default: `ScaleMinPtsWhenSubsampled` ships only after a held-out score. The
arms differ only in the replay experiment `density_cap` and in
`l4.dbscan_xy_v1.foreground_max_input_points` (8,000 and 100,000).

**Readings fixed before the replays (2026-10-26).** The screen and the rule's terms, as the lane
reads them:

- _Over the cap_ is `a0`'s first-run `dbscan_input.json`: the share of scored frames whose DBSCAN
  input exceeded `foreground_max_input_points`, and the median and smallest kept fraction among
  them. A site is _capped_ when the share is 1 % or more.
- _Confirmed vehicle tracks_ are the screen's (`screen_consol.py`, l3bg0023's) vehicle-sized
  tracks: lasting 2 s or more, with the p90 of their associated clusters' longer box side 3.0 m or
  more.
- _Spurious clusters a frame_, on the corpus with no labels, are the clusters that no lasting
  track (2 s or more) ever takes as its measurement, a frame, over the frames with observations:
  the label-free counterpart of kirk0's unlabelled clusters.
- _The run-to-run band._ The replays are deterministic (the tool's repeat check is byte-equal), so
  there is no run-to-run spread to measure; the band is a change of one vehicle track or 1 % of
  `a0`'s count at the site, whichever is larger.
- _Frame p99_ is the repeat run's `frame_timing.json`, which writes no evidence; a site's three
  arms run back to back, on arrow-worker beside its other services, so the timing is that host's
  and not the Pi's.
- Clause 2's kirk0 condition is read only if clause 2 is reached.

**What it does not establish.** Recall of distant objects by range band, which the corpus has no
labels for; any Pi frame time; and whether a different cap would serve better.
