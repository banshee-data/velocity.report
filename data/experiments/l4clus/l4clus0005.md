# l4clus0005: the joining rule, not the density

**Question.** l4clus0003 lowered DBSCAN's core threshold and made more fragments, not fewer. PCL
separates connectivity from density: a radius graph joins what any chain of neighbours connects, and
a conditional radius graph joins only where a stated condition holds. Do the 20 to 50 m fragments on
kirk0 have a path between them at the current 0.8 m radius, and does a vertical-overlap condition
keep that path from bridging two vehicles?

**Rule, written before the replays (2026-10-27).** l4clus0003's thresholds apply: an arm passes if
fragmented instants at 20 to 35 m fall by five percentage points or more against `a0`, reviewed
cross-object merges do not rise, unlabelled clusters a frame are no more than 1.5 times `a0`'s, and
HOTA and IDF1 are each within 0.01 of `a0`. A pass proposes the arm for the v0.5.4 W3 fix's
candidates; it changes no default.

**What it does not establish.** The corpus or a held-out window; side-by-side vehicles, of which
kirk0 has too few; Pi cost.
