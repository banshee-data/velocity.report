# l4clus0005: sampling and the joining rule

**Question.** l4clus0003 lowered DBSCAN's core threshold and made more fragments, not fewer.
A radius graph joins any chain of neighbours; the conditional graph restricts its edges by
vertical support. Does either connect kirk0's 20 to 50 m fragments at 0.8 m without joining
separate vehicles, and does the answer change when the cap no longer removes connecting samples?

**Design amendment, before execution (October 28, 2026).**
[OQ-05](../../../docs/lidar/operations/pcl-joining-cap-controls.md) adds the uncapped DBSCAN
control and both graph counterparts, using the existing 100,000-point parameter set. That is
an effective no-cap treatment only after verifying all input counts. The original three arms
remain. This spec is still proposed; neither the oracle nor the replay selectors is implemented.

**Rule, amended before the replays.** Every candidate must meet the original l4clus0003 limits
against `a0`: fragmented instants at 20 to 35 m fall by at least five percentage points, reviewed
cross-object merges do not rise, unlabelled clusters per frame are at most 1.5 times `a0`, and
HOTA and IDF1 each fall by no more than 0.01. An uncapped graph must also meet those same limits
against `nocap`, so removing samples and changing connectivity do not share credit. Report both
comparisons even if one fails. `nocap` alone is a control, not a new default candidate. A passing
graph is proposed for the v0.5.4 W3 fix's candidates and changes no default.

The one-third oracle gate uses 20 to 50 m; the five-point replay rule uses 20 to 35 m, as before.
Do not pool the bands, change denominators after seeing outcomes, or use label-free track counts
as a substitute for reviewed merges. Report frame p99 and cap exposure, including warm-up, as
cost/context; this amendment adds no unsupported timing acceptance threshold.

**Partition scope (OQ-13).** The [merged-detection disposition](../../../docs/lidar/operations/pcl-merged-detection-partition-disposition.md)
drops a separate PCL splitter. These six arms still test joining and its cross-object merge harm;
conditional components are not certified physical objects. Point-level repair of one merged
parent belongs to the observation-revision plan with exclusive child membership, positive merge
cases, and single-body false-split controls. No arm, parameter, or numerical rule changes here.

**What it does not establish.** A corpus or held-out result; side-by-side vehicles, of which
kirk0 has too few; Pi cost. If kirk0 has no relevant capped scored frames, report that the
sampling interaction is untested there. A failed fixed-radius oracle rules out only these
four graph/cap combinations on this frozen input, not all L4 alternatives or evidence of motion.
