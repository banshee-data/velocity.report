# l4clus0006: the mean of a cluster's returns as its position

**Question.** With foreground refined, [what remains of medoid jitter](../../../docs/lidar/operations/l3-foreground-refinement-2026-10.md#what-remains)
is mostly composition: the medoid is one return, so it steps between returns as the visible surface
changes. Task 3.2 chose the medoid because the mean of a non-convex cluster can fall off the cloud
and [D2](../../../docs/lidar/operations/d2-medoid-obb-current-solver-2026-10.md) kept it against the
OBB centre, both on masks that cut vehicles in pieces. On whole bodies, does the mean hold identity
as well and move less?

**Measures.** As l3bg0029, on the arm's own reported position. Track state jitter is the same
statistic on the filter's output, and speed jitter the median change in speed between frames.

**Rule, written before the held-out read.** The refine-mean arm against the refine arm on the four
held-out captures, as the mean of per-capture ratios.

- **Target:** median position jitter down by at least 20 %, p90 no higher, at least three of the four
  captures lower on the median.
- **Non-inferiority:** moving-track seconds at most −5 %; stationary tracks and broken links at most
  +10 %; track state jitter and speed jitter no higher.
- **Labels, kirk0, parked car left out:** ID switches no higher, identity recall no lower, false
  negatives at most +5 %. Precision is read as in l3bg0029.
- **Cost:** none expected: the mean is already computed for the medoid.

**Adoption.** A pass adds the key to the parameter set, not to the defaults: the candidate there is
already waiting on the decisions l3bg0029 names.

**Answer (November 1, 2026).** No, by one clause. The target passes: position jitter −36 %, p90 −18 %,
four captures of four, and kirk0 ID switches fall from 117 to 87. Broken links rise 11.6 % against a
bound of 10 %, or from 67 to 69 in counts. The key stays out of the parameter set and the arm stays
its own file:
[report](../../../docs/lidar/operations/l4-cluster-mean-centre-2026-11.md).
