# l4clus0007: the mean centre on the defaults

**Question.** [l4clus0006](l4clus0006.md) found the mean of a cluster's returns a large gain on
refined foreground, and on development captures a large one on the defaults too. The mean needs
nothing else from that work and costs nothing to compute. Does it hold up on the defaults alone,
on captures not read for it?

**Rule, written before the held-out read.** The defaults-mean arm against the defaults on three
placements, as the mean of per-capture ratios, measures as l3bg0029.

- **Target:** median position jitter down by at least 15 %, all three captures lower; filtered track
  position jitter and speed jitter down by at least 5 %.
- **Non-inferiority:** moving-track seconds at most −5 %; stationary tracks and broken links at most
  +10 %; p90 position jitter no higher.
- **Labels, kirk0, parked car left out:** ID switches lower, HOTA no lower, false positives and false
  negatives each at most +3 %. Both arms find the same objects, so here HOTA is a fair reading.

**Adoption.** A pass recommends the default. It moves the config fingerprint, so it needs a perf
baseline recapture, and the medoid's identity as `medoid_v0` needs its own decision.
