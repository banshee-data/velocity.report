# Motion-supported fragment joining

The owner requested the W3 cloud development item. Earlier fixed-radius graph,
minimum-density and held-return arms did not qualify. This is a different lever:
use a recent, confirmed moving body's predicted footprint to support joining
otherwise disconnected accepted components. No broader radius or minimum-point
retune, and no recovery of rejected/noise returns.

## Rule before replay

The candidate starts off. On kirk0's existing reviewed tuning population,
fragmented instants at 20–35 m must fall by at least five percentage points,
with no increase in reviewed cross-object merges, no reduction in retained
reviewed membership, HOTA and IDF1 no lower than baseline minus 0.005, and no
increase in false negatives. Report all range bands and the label-free noise
screen beside the score, including brief tracks, clutter and observed vehicle
frames. Zero target fragmentation exposure is insufficient evidence.

A candidate must refuse ambiguous ownership, stale/unconfirmed/static priors,
missing complete membership, non-finite geometry and groups failing the existing
shape filters. Source ordinals and all accepted members must survive joining.
No default promotion: this cloud screen cannot replace the corpus, frozen
held-out merge/identity references or target-hardware timing. If labels remain
unavailable, report synthetic correctness and unlabelled replay observations
separately, with the W3 gate explicitly insufficient.

## Frozen inputs

Baseline tuning SHA-256 `9ad7377fa5d356ba5f9d8756d48721708071da3295702350c835110b38b41675`.
The implementation pins one candidate file before replay: maximum joining gap
2 m and footprint margin 0.15 m, recent-support age 0.2 s, minimum speed 2 m/s,
maximum position standard deviation 0.75 m. No changes after reading the score.
Kirk0 SHA-256 `2864ebde38e736b496d33361e9bcdc9246aa5147459ec48aee0f8f11f1f58b9a`.

The motion prior additionally requires at least eight observations, observed
support on the preceding frame, a classified rigid vehicle, length at least
3 m and width 0.5–4 m. Full CV position covariance is projected to the common
capture time and its largest standard deviation is bounded. All retained
processed members must lie in one footprint; partial intersection with any
second eligible footprint makes ownership ambiguous. Trial groups failing
existing size/aspect filters revert to their original components.

## Cloud checkpoint

The corrected build produced 39 joins on kirk0, reproduced both baselines and
joining counts, and reduced the approximate spurious-cluster proxy by 8.94 %.
The [report](../../../docs/lidar/operations/w3-motion-fragment-joining-cloud-2026-11.md)
records observed vehicle frames and the unchanged duplicate-pair count beside it.
Reviewed retention, fragmentation and merge scoring remain unavailable; the
required rule is insufficient evidence. The spec remains approved with scoring
open, and no default changes. This is a development screen, not corpus or
physical qualification.
