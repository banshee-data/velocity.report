# l4clus0010: truck membership, fragmentation and the extent state

**Question.** [l4clus0008](l4clus0008.md)'s motion-supported join takes kirk0's 20 to 35 m
fragmented instants from 6.5 % to 5.7 % and its IDF1 gain keeps its sign over the bootstrap. The
[F0 report](../../../docs/lidar/operations/facet-f0-attribution-kirk0-report.md) found the solid
body's believed truck length short of the labelled span in 97 % of truck instants, and the
[October W3 baseline](../../../docs/lidar/operations/w3-retention-fragmentation-kirk0-2026-10.md)
split 59 % of truck instants. #970's extent state now carries the half-extents as states, but
refuses every span bound on a merge candidate and on a disbelieved axis. Does joining a truck's
fragments reach the extent state, and if not, where does the evidence stop?

**Arms.** Every arm runs `solid_body` and `solid_body_face_state` (all five face-state options,
the extent state among them), so the extent the readings judge is the one #970 built.
`a0` is the defaults; `join` is l4clus0008's registered set. An arm `fix` is added after the
implementation, before its replay, without changing this rule: the parameter set and replay
options the gap below needs.

**Measures.** On kirk0's reviewed labels, the pack rebuilt by `lidar-pack-rebuild` as in the
[cloud labelled run](../../../docs/lidar/operations/cloud-run-kirk0-labelled-2026-11.md), split
drafted with `-from-seconds 6 -split kirk0-tuning`, `obj_788622b5` ignored:

1. **Identity.** `perframe` on the tracked estimates: HOTA, IDF1, identity switches and
   fragmentations, with `-bootstrap 2000 -bootstrap-seed 20261108`, each arm against `a0`.
2. **Truck fragmentation.** `retention` by class: of truck instants reaching a cluster, the share
   in two or more clusters; the 20 to 35 m band beside it.
3. **Truck extent.** `perframe -physical-reference` on the solid-body rows: for the two reviewed
   trucks (`obj_35c661db`, `obj_8e829a06`), the believed length's signed error against the
   reference length, median over their scored instants, and the share of instants below the
   reference interval.

**Rule, committed before any replay (2026-11-08).** A measurement that chooses a fix, then a
screen of the fix.

- **J1, identity non-inferiority.** An arm is non-inferior to `a0` when the paired bootstrap's
  5th percentile of the HOTA and IDF1 deltas is at or above −0.02 and its 95th percentile of the
  switch delta is at or below +10. A gain is claimed only where the 5th to 95th interval keeps its
  sign.
- **J2, truck fragmentation.** An arm reaches the trucks when its fragmented truck share is at
  least 5 percentage points below `a0`'s.
- **J3, truck extent.** An arm carries extent evidence when the median absolute truck length
  error is at least 0.5 m below `a0`'s.

Which gap `join` names:

- J2 holds and J3 fails: the joined cluster reaches the tracker but its span does not reach the
  extent state. Fix (a): admit a joined cluster's span as length evidence on a merge candidate.
- J2 fails: the join does not reach the trucks' fragments. Fix (b) if the trucks' published box
  length, the footprint the join reads, is more than 1 m under the solid body's believed length
  on most of the trucks' scored frames; fix (c), track-conditioned membership, otherwise.
- J2 and J3 both hold: no gap is named by this capture; fix (a) is implemented as the narrowest
  and read as a control.

**The fix arm** passes this screen when it is J1 non-inferior to `a0`, meets the clause its gap
names (J2 for (b) and (c), J3 for (a)) against `a0`, adds no reviewed cross-object merge, keeps
pooled retention at or above `a0`'s less 0.005, and when the build that carries it, with the option
off, reproduces `a0`'s baseline recording byte for byte. A pass makes it a candidate for the corpus
read; no default changes on kirk0 alone.

**The fix arm, added before its replay (2026-11-08).** `join` read J2 false (fragmented truck
instants 21.2 % to 19.4 %) and the published box more than 1 m under the believed length on 394 of
874 truck instants (45 %), not most, so the rule names fix (c). `fix` is `join` with
`fragment_join_track_membership` (commit `9bf60fb`): each footprint widened by two standard
deviations of its projected position along and across the body. It is judged on J1, J2 against
`a0`, merges, retention and the off-path identity, as written above.

**What could mislead.** kirk0 is a tuning capture; its two trucks carry most of the identity
switches and all of the truck instants, so J2 and J3 are two objects' figures. The pack is
reconstructed: its points are decoded by the current parser, so retention runs with its floor
lowered. The physical references score only the instants a reference keyframe covers. A joined
cluster changes the background's feedback through the track guard, so not every changed figure is
the join's own.

**What it does not establish.** Anything held out or on the corpus; Pi cost; whether the extent
state is right for cars.
