# l4clus0011: track-conditioned fragment joining off kirk0

**Question.** Track-conditioned joining (`fragment_join_track_membership` on the motion join's
parameter set) is the one November candidate that gained a labelled figure on kirk0 at no noise
cost: truck fragmented instants 198 to 145 of 936 (l4clus0010), and in l5id0019's screen IDF1
0.4728 to 0.4769 with no noise cost on any of five cases. Every case it has read is quiet, and
kirk0 holds no two vehicles side by side, so the case every report names as its risk, a footprint
that joins two vehicles, is unread. l5id0024's lot drew two placements with 73 to 196 moving
tracks a segment. This spec reads the join there, with a merge read written now.

**Arms.** `a0`; `join`, the motion join alone; `join-membership`, with track-conditioned
membership.

**Cases.** kirk0 as the anchor. The first two five-minute segments of columbus-north-point and
lombard-broderick, read by l5id0024 for the likelihood birth gate and by no joining arm. A screen:
the label-free identity and noise budget on the corpus is spent.

**Rule, written before any replay (2026-11-10).** Read for `join-membership`; `join` is reported
as its control and decides nothing.

- **M1, the anchor.** On kirk0, against `a0`, the paired bootstrap's 5th percentiles of the HOTA
  and IDF1 deltas are above −0.005 and the 95th percentile of the switch change is +10 or less.
- **M2, noise.** On each segment under l5id0019's R4, moving by l5id0024's peak test: it passes
  on three of the four segments at least, and on none loses a lasting moving track by footprint.
- **M3, counts.** On each segment the moving count moves by 5 % at most after l5id0024's join
  credit, and p85 by 0.5 m/s at most.
- **M4, merges.** Inferred merges per segment are no more than `a0`'s floor plus one, and no more
  than two over the four segments in excess of the floor.
- **Outcome.** A pass on M1 to M4 names `join-membership` for label0003's window, with label0001's
  masks on any adjacent pair as the merge reference that decides it. A fail names its clause.
  Nothing is adopted.

**Reported, not decided.** Joins per frame; spurious clusters a frame; duplicate pair-frames; each
inferred merge's frames, widths and speeds; the old median count beside the peak count.

**What could mislead.** An inferred merge is a footprint read, not truth: a vehicle passing close
beside a parked one, or a duplicate pair the overlap test misses, reads as a merge, which is why
`a0`'s floor is read the same way. The peak test and join credit were written after l5id0023 and
were read once on these segments by l5id0024, for another candidate.

**Answer (2026-11-10).** `join-membership` passes M1 (HOTA 5th percentile −0.0002, IDF1 +0.0004,
switch 95th percentile 0; HOTA 0.4323, IDF1 0.4769, 90 switches, 42 fragmentations, l5id0019's
figures) and M2 (noise costs only at lombard-broderick's second segment, where duplicate pairs per
hundred vehicle tracks go from 11.4 to 11.6 over the same 19 pairs; no lasting mover lost). It fails
M3 at lombard-broderick's first segment, where the credited peak count moves +6.6 %: 14 joins are
credited 15 while 15 of `a0`'s moving vehicles are carried in a second moving track. It fails M4:
inferred merges 17, 18, 26 and 12 against a floor of 17, 15, 18 and 9, 14 in excess over the four
segments. Of its nine pairs the floor does not hold, seven are one-frame contacts at closest
approach and one, drawn by both join arms at lombard-broderick's second segment, is a 3.6 m box over
a slow vehicle and a passing one. The control `join` passes M1 to M3 and would fail M4 by 5. Not
named for label0003's window; nothing is adopted. See the
[report](../../../docs/lidar/operations/fragment-join-membership-lot-2026-11.md).
