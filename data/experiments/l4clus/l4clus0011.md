# l4clus0011: track-conditioned joining on six more placements, with a merge reference

**Question.** `fragment_join_track_membership` (#986, l4clus0010's fix (c)) widens each motion
footprint the join tests membership against by two standard deviations of the body's projected
position, and passed [l4clus0010](l4clus0010.md)'s screen on kirk0. In the November benchmark
(l5id0019, on the open PR #994: five cases on kirk0 and two placements) it lowered spurious clusters a frame at every case, −6.2 % pooled, with no noise cost,
and its IDF1 gain on kirk0 kept its sign. The open risk is the wrong join: two road users merged
into one cluster. kirk0's trucks never come within reach of another body, so it cannot test that.
On six placements it has not read, does the drop in spurious clusters hold, and does the widened
footprint make more wrong joins than the join alone?

**Arms.** `a0` is the defaults. `join` is l4clus0008's registered set,
[motion-fragment-join.json](../../../config/experiments/motion-fragment-join.json). `membership` is
the same set with `fragment_join_track_membership`. Every arm also runs `solid_body` and
`solid_body_face_state` as a shadow, so that the solid body's believed length can be read after
each join. l5id0019 found that shadow byte-identical to `a0` on kirk0's tracks and unchanged in
every label-free figure at its four segments; guard G0 below checks it here.

**Cases.** The first five-minute segment (0 to 300 s from the first packet) of six placements in
`banshee-data/sf-street-speeds`: the next six in the dataset's listing order after the two that
l5id0019 read, skipping embarcadero-folsom, the held-out site, which is neither downloaded nor
read. In order: `20260901T1327_80858-0a4_pierce-haight`, `20260901T1359_80858-0b4_fulton-divisadero`,
`20260901T1433_80858-0cc_california-leon-baker`, `20260901T1510_80858-0c4_broadway-gough`,
`20260901T1546_80858-0bc_laguna-eddy` and `20260902T0938_80858-0d4_lombard-broderick`. One placement
is held at a time. Each segment is cut by a pcapng window filter, because `pcap-split
--start-seconds --duration-seconds` writes the whole capture. Each cut's UDP 2369 packet count must
equal `pcap-split --dry-run`'s for the same window (guard G1). These are d2 first segments, which
l5id0012 has already read for its flip cost, so they are not held out for the noise measures.

**Measures.** Each arm on each site, with the benchmark's tools and options:

1. **Spurious clusters a frame** (`lidar_noise_screen.py` on the evidence).
2. **Noise and counts**, as l5id0019's R4 read them: brief tracks and near-static tracks
   (`lidar_noise_screen.py`); clutter and its classes (`lidar_clutter_origin.py`); duplicate pairs,
   per hundred vehicle tracks, and pair-frames (`lidar-box-eval`'s summary); moving tracks, lost,
   shortened and added (`lidar_flip_cost.py --match footprint` against `a0`); the moving count and
   its p85 and p98 (`lidar_figure_sensitivity.py` against `a0`).
3. **The merge reference** (the two join arms), from a record of every join. A join is one output
   cluster built from two or more DBSCAN components. Its owner is the motion footprint holding all
   its components. Each confirmed track's predicted box is its published box projected to the
   frame at its velocity, with the join's 0.15 m margin, not widened. A track whose state is more
   than 1 s old has no predicted box. Three counts:
   - **M1, oversize joins.** The joined cluster's oriented length or width exceeds the plausible
     extent for the owner's class at the join: car 7.0 m long or 3.0 m wide; truck, and the
     unspecified vehicle class, 12.0 m or 3.0 m; bus 19.0 m or 3.2 m.
   - **M2, two-track joins.** The components are attributed to two or more distinct confirmed
     tracks. A component is attributed to the track whose predicted box holds the largest share of
     its returns, when that share is at least a half.
   - **M3, solid-body length jumps.** The owner's solid-body believed length at its first row at
     or after the join's frame, within 0.2 s, exceeds its last row before that frame by more than
     2.0 m.

   Also reported, outside the rule: joins and frames with joins; the union of M1 to M3; each count
   per thousand joins; the share of each join's returns outside the owner's predicted box (what
   the widening admitted); the published box length's change on the same rows; and solid-body rows
   whose length is not positive (l4clus0010's latent defect).

**Reading rule, written before any replay (2026-11-09).** A screen on label-free data. Nothing is
adopted and no default changes, whatever the result.

- **G0, the shadow.** On the first site the `membership` arm is replayed again without the solid
  body. Its track observation rows must equal the shadowed run's. If they do not, M3 is reported
  as unreadable and the screen is read on S1, S2 and M1 and M2 alone.
- **G1, the cuts.** A segment whose UDP 2369 count differs from `pcap-split --dry-run`'s is cut
  again; a site that cannot be cut or fetched is reported missing.
- **S1, the target.** `membership`'s spurious clusters a frame is lower than `a0`'s at 5 or more
  of the 6 sites.
- **S2, noise.** At every site, against `a0`, `membership`'s brief tracks rise by no more than 3
  and its duplicate pairs per hundred vehicle tracks do not rise (l5id0019's R4 bounds).
- **S3, merges.** Pooled over the six sites, each of `membership`'s M1, M2 and M3 counts is no
  higher than `join`'s.
- **Passes the screen** when S1, S2 and S3 all hold. The counts clause of R4 (moving count by more
  than 5 %, p85 by more than 0.5 m/s) is reported, not gating, as R6 read it.
- **Missing sites.** If fewer than six sites are read, the screen is incomplete and says so. S1
  is then read as all but one of the sites read, provisionally.

**What could mislead.** No labels: M1 to M3 are geometry and identity signs of a wrong join, not
merges confirmed by a person. M1 flags a long vehicle the classifier calls a car, and misses two
cars nose to tail inside 7 m. M2 misses a road user no confirmed track holds. M3 also fires on a
real truck whose length the join first made visible. Each segment carries tens of moving tracks,
so the 5 % count bound is one or two tracks. The L3 track guard carries any L4 change into L3, so
a join's effect is not only its own layer's.

**What it does not establish.** Merges confirmed against labels, anything held out
(embarcadero-folsom is not read), any site beyond these six, live behaviour or Pi cost. A corpus
read is a screen, not a decision.

**Answer (2026-11-09).** All six sites read; G0 and G1 hold. `membership` does not pass the
screen. S1 holds: spurious clusters a frame fell against `a0` at 6 of 6 sites, by 3.0 % to 9.6 %,
7.3 % pooled against `join`'s 3.6 %. S2 fails: duplicate pairs per hundred vehicle tracks rose at
pierce-haight (one new pair, owned by no join) and laguna-eddy (no new pair; one fewer vehicle
track). S3 fails: pooled M1 37 against 17 and M2 14 against 4, with M3 0 against 2. Every M1 flag
in both arms sits within 0.5 m of the owner's own box, a long body under the car limit. M2
carries the finding. All 4 of `join`'s attach a young unclassed track; 5 of `membership`'s 14 join
a second classed track, two of them pedestrian-classed, and 127 of its 370 joins take returns
outside the owner's unwidened box. No default changes. The next test is the option with a
refusal for components another confirmed track claims. See the
[report](../../../docs/lidar/operations/w3-join-membership-corpus-merge-reference-2026-11.md).
