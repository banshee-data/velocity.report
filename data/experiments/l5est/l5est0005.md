# l5est0005: G-EST-1's acceleration test on the face-referenced measurement

**Question.** G-EST-1 (state plan Section 7.3) moves the tracker from constant velocity (CV) to
constant acceleration (CA) only if CV's NIS over-dispersion is correlated with acceleration rather
than with range, aspect or point count. On the medoid, the centre slides along a long body as it
enters and leaves view, and a CV filter reads that slide as acceleration it cannot follow. So the
test is likely to pass for the wrong reason there (maths review 2026-11-08, Section 4.3).
figure0002's trucks, whose filter speed ran about 12 % low on kirk0, fit either cause. Is the
over-dispersion acceleration-specific once the measurement is face-referenced?

**Arms.** Both from `config/tuning.defaults.json`.

- `a0`: the shipped tracked filter, reading its medoid residuals (source `tracked_filter`).
- `face_state`: the same replay with the shadow solid body under every face-state option
  (`solid_body`, `solid_body_face_state`: extents in the state, censored extent evidence, span
  bounds, heading face noise, anisotropic reference bias). It reads the solid body's near-edge fix
  residuals (source `solid_body_faces`). The shadow changes no track. The `face_state` replay's
  `tracking_baseline.json` must equal `a0`'s byte for byte, and its `tracked_filter` section
  must equal `a0`'s. Otherwise the run is void.

**Method.** One kirk0 replay per arm, the first run collecting per track and frame (state plan
Section 7.3; [motion models maths](../../maths/motion-models-maths.md#10-g-est-1-analyses)).

- **Residuals.** `a0`: the live innovation against the tracked prior, `S = P⁻ + R·I`, and the live
  NIS with two degrees of freedom. `face_state`: each near-edge fix's joint face innovations
  against the solid body's prior, rank-aware, and its NIS with the fix's rank as degrees of
  freedom. A rank-one fix gives only its normal component, attributed to the course axis its normal
  lies nearer. The solid body's medoid updates, lapses and coasts are not face-referenced and are
  not read.
- **Population.** Confirmed chains with at least 10 observations whose smoothed speed reaches
  2 m/s (moving tracks), and their residuals in the scoring window after the 6 s warm-up.
  Criterion 2 also needs a course (smoothed speed of at least 0.5 m/s), a smoothed acceleration
  and the associated cluster's point count.
- **Smoothed acceleration**, the same estimator on each arm's own observations. The offline CV
  filter is smoothed over the whole chain (RTS), and the smoothed velocity is differenced at the
  nearest observations at or beyond ±0.5 s, at most 1.5 s apart, then projected on the smoothed
  course. It is centred, so it has no filter lag. Within about 1.5 s of a chain's end it is
  attenuated toward zero.

**Rule, written before any replay (2026-11-08).** A screen. kirk0 is tuning data with about 20
reviewed objects, and G-EST-1's criterion 1 asks for residuals from at least 5,000 confirmed
moving tracks, which kirk0 cannot supply; the report gives the count. Whatever this reads, no
default changes and no CA ships. A pass proposes the corpus read and nothing more.

1. **Criterion 2, per arm.** The partial correlation `r` of NIS per degree of freedom with
   |longitudinal acceleration|, controlling for range, aspect, point count and dimension. It comes
   with its 95 % Fisher-z interval, as G-EST-1 defines it. Samples within a track are not
   independent, so the report also gives a 95 % interval over 2,000 seeded resamples of whole
   tracks. Over-dispersion is read as **acceleration-specific** for an arm only when both
   intervals lie wholly above zero. If the Fisher interval excludes zero and the track interval
   does not, the reading is "not shown at this sample". An interval wholly below zero is an
   anti-correlation and is no case for CA.
2. **Criterion 3, per arm.** The share of moving tracks with at least three consecutive
   same-sign longitudinal residuals beyond one sigma, against G-EST-1's 5 %. It is reported as
   context. It carries no decision on its own, because the medoid's slide also makes runs.
3. **Criterion 4, per arm.** Offline CA with the coast rule against offline CV over the arm's
   observations: p99 |along| one-step innovation at most 0.8 of CV's, and p99 |across| at most
   1.05 of CV's.
4. **Criterion 5, per arm.** Reacquisition error medians and p95 over synthetic gaps of 0.5, 1.0
   and 2.5 s (the longest permitted coast at 10 Hz, `max_misses_confirmed` 25). CV, CA with the
   coast rule and the IMM are read against CV; CA without the coast rule is reported. Results are
   split into all gaps and gaps starting while decelerating. A row with fewer than 20 cases is
   reported and not read. CA with the coast rule, or the IMM, holds criterion 5 if its median and
   p95 do not exceed CV's in any read row.
5. **What the screen says.** The CA increment is supported only if `face_state`'s criterion 2
   reads acceleration-specific (clause 1), and its criterion 4 passes, and criterion 5 holds for
   CA with the coast rule or the IMM. Short of that, the screen says CA is not supported on
   kirk0's evidence. If `a0` reads acceleration-specific and `face_state` does not, the medoid's
   result is attributed to the measurement, as Section 4.3 predicts. The cross-read supports that
   attribution when it is null: `a0`'s NIS against `face_state`'s acceleration, same track and
   time.

**Secondary reads, reported and not ruled on.** Criterion 2 per dimension (where both ranks
occur), on the interior (samples at least 1.5 s from both ends of their chain, beyond the
smoother's attenuation) and on ranks (partial Spearman). Each arm's NIS by dimension: count,
mean per degree of freedom, and share above the χ² 95 % point.

**What could mislead.**

- For `a0` the acceleration comes from the medoid track, so the slide can enter both the NIS and
  the acceleration, inflating `r`. The cross-read is there for that.
- `face_state` reads only the frames where faces fixed the body, a subset of `a0`'s, and mixes
  ranks one and two.
- The solid body's NIS may be miscalibrated overall (the extent prior, the heading term). Its
  mean per degree of freedom is reported so a correlation is not read without its level.
- Pearson's `r` on NIS is carried by the tail; the rank read guards it.
- One truck can decide a sample of twenty objects.

**What it does not establish.** Anything off kirk0; criterion 1; whether a CA model would improve
the published speeds; any physical acceleration (the label is a smoothed estimate from the
pipeline's own measurement, not a reference).
