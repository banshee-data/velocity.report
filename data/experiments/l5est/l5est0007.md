# l5est0007: G-EST-1, restated and read on the corpus

**Question.** G-EST-1 asks whether CV's innovations are over-dispersed in a way acceleration
explains. l5est0005 passed its screen on kirk0 as declared, with three qualifications that
matter more than the pass: 43 moving tracks against the 5,000 criterion 1 asks; NIS
under-dispersed (0.43 per dimension on the medoid, 0.24 to 0.33 on faces) where the gap analysis
measured moving-track NIS of 1.7 to 3.2 at the sites in October; and a face-referenced CV p99
along the track of 4.06 m against the medoid's 1.27 m, so CA's 0.615 ratio on faces is a model
absorbing a measurement tail. This spec reads the same report on enough tracks, with the
criterion the screen's report asks for, and reads the one zero-code alternative to a new model
beside it: the velocity process noise at 0.2, 0.4 and 0.8.

**Arms.** `a0` and `face_state` as l5est0005 ran them (their tracks are byte-equal; they differ
in which residual is read). `vel-x2` and `vel-x4` from their checked-in parameter sets. The
offline CA, coast and IMM estimators run inside `-gest1-report` at their prototype defaults on
every arm's record; none is live.

**Cases.** The first five-minute segment of the 23 tuning and screen sites. They have been read
for identity and noise; no report has read them for filter consistency, so this is their first
read for that measure family and it is a measurement, not a held-out score.

**It is a measurement. What each reading decides, written before any replay (2026-11-10).**

- **M0, enough tracks.** Criterion 1 is met when the pooled arms hold 5,000 confirmed moving
  tracks with residuals. Under it, every reading below is reported with its count and decides
  nothing; the second segments are then named for the same read.
- **M1, is there over-dispersion to explain.** Restated criterion 2, first half: the pooled
  one-step along-track z RMS under `a0` is above 1, or more than 5 % of along-track z values lie
  beyond 1.96. **If neither holds**, CV is not over-dispersed along the track on the corpus, the
  premise of G-EST-1 is not met, CA and the IMM stay offline, and the next work is the
  measurement noise (G-UNC-1), since the filter is then under-confident, not lagging.
- **M2, is it acceleration.** If M1 holds: the partial correlation of along-track z squared with
  the smoothed acceleration magnitude, controlling for range, aspect and point count, has a
  whole-track bootstrap 5th percentile above 0.1 on the medoid residuals **and** on the one-step
  face residuals. **If it holds on the medoid only**, the excess is the reference point sliding
  on the body and the work is the measurement, not the model.
- **M3, does a looser CV do it.** If M1 and M2 hold: `vel-x2` or `vel-x4` passes when its
  along-track z RMS is within 0.9 to 1.1, its across-track z RMS is no further from 1 than
  `a0`'s, and its jerk share above 15 m/s3 is no more than 1.5 times `a0`'s. **If one passes**,
  the candidate for the trucks' speed shortfall is a process-noise value, l5est0004 carries it
  and CA stays offline. **If neither passes** and the offline IMM's along-track p99 ratio is
  0.8 or under with an across-track ratio of 1.05 or under on the medoid residuals, G-EST-1
  criterion 4 is met on the corpus and a live CV/CA IMM is specified as the next increment.
- **M4, the strata.** Whatever M1 reads, the stratum table is reported: a stratum whose
  along-track z RMS is over 1.5 while the pool's is under 1 (young tracks, long bodies, far
  range) names where the filter is wrong, and that stratum is the next spec's population.

**Reported, not decided.** Criteria 3 and 5 as l5est0005 reported them; each estimator's jerk
checks; the faces' metre-scale tail by face kind and aspect; per-site figures with the worst
leave-one-out line for M1 and M2; the count of tracks under 1 s old and their share of the tail.

**What could mislead.** The acceleration is the pipeline's own smoothed estimate, so M2
correlates a residual with a function of the same residuals; the partial correlation and the
bootstrap bound that, they do not remove it. NIS reads the filter's consistency with itself, not
its distance from a vehicle: an R that is too large hides lag. The jerk threshold is Punzo's for
passenger-car trajectories at 10 Hz and is a plausibility check, not truth. First segments
include each site's settling. No speed reference exists off kirk0; nothing here says a speed is
right.

**Answer (2026-11-10): M0 is not met, so the read decides nothing.** The 23 first segments hold
4,117 confirmed moving tracks with residuals under `a0`, against 5,000; the second segments are
named for the same read. Reported with that count: M1's figures hold (one-step along z RMS 1.073,
track bootstrap 1.057 to 1.090, 7.0 % beyond 1.96); M2's hold on both measurements (bootstrap
5th percentile 0.177 on the medoid, 0.118 on the one-step faces, worst leave-one-out 0.170 and
0.106); M3 passes neither arm (`vel-x2` along 0.987 but across 0.355 against `a0`'s 0.392;
`vel-x4` across 0.325 and live jerk share 19.2 % against 15.5 %), and the IMM's along ratio is
0.977, so criterion 4 is not met; M4's trigger is not met because the pool is over 1, but
residuals under 1 s into their track read 2.52 and carry half the tail, and after the first
second the medoid reads 0.85. The excess is track start, not acceleration. kirk0's tracked
figures reproduce exactly; its face figures moved with a change on main after l5est0005. Report:
[gest1-corpus-read-2026-11](../../../docs/lidar/operations/gest1-corpus-read-2026-11.md).
