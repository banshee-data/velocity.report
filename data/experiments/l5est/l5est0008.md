# l5est0008: G-EST-1 on the second segments

**Question.** l5est0007 read G-EST-1's restated criteria on the corpus's 23 first segments and
fell short of criterion 1's count (4,117 confirmed moving tracks against 5,000), so by its own
M0 its readings decide nothing and the second segments are named for the same read. Its post-hoc
split also suggests a different answer from the one G-EST-1 asks about: the medoid's along-track
over-dispersion (one-step z RMS 1.073) sits in each track's first second (RMS 2.52, 51.6 % of the
tail), and after it the medoid reads 0.85, with a partial correlation with acceleration of 0.26
after the first second and −0.02 inside it. That split was found after reading; this spec states
it as a rule and reads it on segments no filter-consistency measure has seen.

**Arms.** The defaults, read through one `face_state` replay per site, which writes both the
tracked-filter (medoid) and the face-referenced residuals.

**Cases.** The second segment of the 22 sites of `d2-second-segment`. They have been read for
identity and noise and, today, for settling (l3bg0030); never for filter consistency, so they are
held out for this family.

**Readings, written before any replay (2026-11-10).**

- **N0, enough tracks.** l5est0007's M0 on the pool of the first and second segments under the
  defaults: 5,000 confirmed moving tracks with residuals. Below it every reading is reported with
  its count and decides nothing.
- **N1, the first second, held out.** On the second segments alone: the medoid's one-step
  along-track z RMS over residuals at track age 1 s or less is above 1.5, and over residuals after
  1 s it is at most 1.0, each with a whole-track bootstrap 5th to 95th interval that does not
  cross the bound. **If both hold**, the along-track excess is a track-start effect: G-EST-1's
  premise is not met after the first second, CA and the IMM stay offline, and initialisation
  (the class-range velocity prior the November 10 review names) is the next increment, ahead of
  any motion model. **If the after-1 s RMS is above 1.0**, there is a residual excess for a motion
  model to explain and N2 is read.
- **N2, acceleration after the first second.** Over residuals after 1 s only, the partial
  correlation of along-track z squared with the smoothed acceleration magnitude, controlling for
  range, aspect and point count, has a whole-track bootstrap 5th percentile above 0.1 on the medoid
  and on the one-step faces. **If it holds** with N1's after-1 s RMS above 1.0, G-EST-1 criteria 1
  and 2 are met on the pool and the next spec is CA with the coast rule on the corpus, under
  criteria 4 and 5. **If not**, no motion model follows.
- **N3, the pool.** l5est0007's M1, M2 and M4 recomputed on the pooled 45 segments, reported.
  They decide only through N0.

**Reported, not decided.** Per-site N1 and N2 with the worst leave-one-out; the IMM's along and
across ratios after 1 s; the face-referenced figures beside the medoid's; the jerk and turning
shares.

**What could mislead.** The 1 s boundary and the 1.5 and 1.0 bounds come from l5est0007's
post-hoc read of the first segments; the second segments follow the first in time at most sites,
so their traffic resembles them. Track age is the filter's, so a fragment reborn mid-pass counts
as young. The acceleration is the pipeline's own smoothed estimate.
