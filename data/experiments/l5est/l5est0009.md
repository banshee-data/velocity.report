# l5est0009: an honest velocity prior at birth

**Question.** l5est0007 found the CV filter's along-track over-dispersion on the corpus (one-step
z RMS 1.073 on the medoid) concentrated in each track's first second, which reads 2.52 and holds
51.6 % of the tail; after the first second the medoid reads 0.85. The shipped tracker starts every
track at zero velocity with a variance of 1 m²/s², so a vehicle at 6.5 m/s is 6.5 sigma from its
prior and takes about a second to converge (maths review §4.2). Two-point initialisation, the
other fix, invents speed from reflections and sliding reference points (l5est0006). This spec reads
the simpler one: leave the velocity at zero and make its variance honest.

**Arms.** `a0`; `prior`, the defaults with the birth velocity variance at (7.5 m/s)².

**Cases.** kirk0 (tuning, labelled) for identity. The first segments of six sites drawn by lot;
l5est0007 read them for consistency under `a0`, so this is a screen of the candidate, not a held-out
read. l5est0008 reads the second segments under `a0` today and is not read by this spec.

**Rule, written before the option exists (2026-11-10).**

- **V1, the first second.** Pooled over the six segments, the medoid's one-step along-track z RMS
  at track age 1 s or less falls by 30 % or more from `a0`'s, with the whole-track bootstrap's 95th
  percentile of the candidate's figure under `a0`'s point figure.
- **V2, after the first second.** The same figure after 1 s rises by no more than 0.05, and the
  across-track figure after 1 s moves by no more than 0.05.
- **V3, no invented speed.** On each segment's scored rows, the candidate's p98 speed is no more
  than 0.5 m/s above `a0`'s, and it writes no row over 15 m/s on a segment where `a0` writes none.
- **V4, identity.** On kirk0, against `a0`, the paired bootstrap's 5th percentiles of the HOTA and
  IDF1 deltas are above −0.005 and the 95th percentile of the switch change is +10 or less.
- **V5, noise.** On the six segments under l5id0019's R4 (moving by l5id0024's peak test), noise
  costs on no more than two.
- **Outcome.** A pass on V1 to V5 names `prior` for the corpus's second segments and label0003's
  spans; it is not adopted on a screen. A fail names its clause.

**Reported, not decided.** Confirmation time and the share of tracks confirmed; the count and p85
with the peak test; criterion 2's partial correlation after 1 s; kirk0's fragmentations.

**What could mislead.** A wide prior also widens the young track's gate, so a newborn can take a
neighbour's cluster: V4 and V5 are there for that. The 7.5 m/s figure is a stated urban prior, not
a fitted one, and a site with faster traffic needs a wider one. The first second was named as the
place to look after l5est0007 read these segments; V1 is a test of a candidate there, not of the
finding.
