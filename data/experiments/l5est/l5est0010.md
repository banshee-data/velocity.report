# l5est0010: the honest birth prior, gated as shipped

**Question.** l5est0009 set a newborn track's velocity variance to (7.5 m/s) squared and the
first second's along-track over-dispersion went (z RMS 2.244 to 0.802 at age under 1 s, pooled
over six lot-drawn segments), but it failed on speed, identity and noise: the wide variance also
widened the newborn's gate, so fragments and sparse clutter confirmed and kept the speed of their
first pairing. Its report separates the two: the gain is in the update, the cost in association.
This spec keeps the wide variance for the update and gates a tentative track as the shipped init
would.

**Arms.** `a0`; `gated`, the defaults with `velocity_prior_init_7p5_gated`. `a0`'s figures on the
six segments may be taken from l5est0009's run where its summaries are kept and its build's `a0`
reproduces byte for byte (checked on kirk0 and one segment); otherwise `a0` is replayed.

**Cases.** kirk0 and the same six first segments as l5est0009 (its lot). Both are now tuning data
for initialisation: a screen, and a pass names the arm for unread segments, not for adoption.

**Rule, written before the option exists (2026-11-10).** l5est0009's V1 to V5, unchanged, read for
`gated` against `a0`:

- **V1, the first second.** Pooled over the six segments, the medoid's one-step along-track z RMS
  at track age 1 s or less falls by 30 % or more from `a0`'s, with the whole-track bootstrap's 95th
  percentile of the candidate's figure under `a0`'s point figure.
- **V2, after the first second.** The same figure after 1 s rises by no more than 0.05, and the
  across-track figure after 1 s moves by no more than 0.05.
- **V3, no invented speed.** On each segment's scored rows, the candidate's p98 speed is no more
  than 0.5 m/s above `a0`'s, and it writes no row over 15 m/s on a segment where `a0` writes none.
- **V4, identity.** On kirk0, against `a0`, the paired bootstrap's 5th percentiles of the HOTA and
  IDF1 deltas are above −0.005 and the 95th percentile of the switch change is +10 or less.
- **V5, noise.** On the six segments under l5id0019's R4 (moving by l5id0024's peak test), noise
  costs on no more than two.
- **Falsifier (l5est0009's).** If duplicate pairs per hundred vehicle tracks still rise on more
  than two of the six segments, the cost is in the update, not the gate.
- **Outcome.** A pass names `gated` for the second segments and label0003's spans. A fail names
  its clause. Nothing is adopted.

**What could mislead.** The six segments and kirk0 have now been read twice for this question, and
the form was chosen after l5est0009's failure: a pass says the gate was the cause on this data, not
that the arm is ready. Gating with a covariance the state does not hold is a deliberate
inconsistency for a tentative track's first frames; the report states how many frames it applies.
