# l5est0011: the gated birth prior, refused in a confirmed track's shadow

**Question.** l5est0010 kept l5est0009's honest birth variance in the update and gated a newborn as
shipped: the first second's along-track excess still went (z RMS 2.244 to 0.787), the brief tracks
and clutter l5est0009 added mostly went, but invented speed (34 and 32 new rows over 15 m/s at
columbus-broadway and franklin-mcallister) and duplicates (up on 6 of 6) stayed, so the cost is in
the update. l5est0006 traced the same invented speeds under two-point initialisation to thin
clusters on a nearer car's bearing at a multiple of its range, and its shadow test declines them.
This spec gives those births the shipped prior and every other birth the wide one.

**Arms.** `a0`; `shadow`. `a0` is taken from l5est0010's run where it reproduces byte for byte on
kirk0 and one segment.

**Cases.** kirk0 and l5est0009's six lot-drawn first segments. Now read three times for this
question; a screen.

**Rule, written before the option exists (2026-11-10).** l5est0009's V1 to V5 and l5est0010's
falsifier, unchanged, read for `shadow` against `a0`:

- **V1.** Pooled first-second medoid along-track z RMS falls by 30 % or more from `a0`'s, with the
  candidate's bootstrap 95th percentile under `a0`'s point figure.
- **V2.** After 1 s, along-track rises by no more than 0.05 and across-track moves by no more than
  0.05.
- **V3.** On each segment, p98 no more than 0.5 m/s above `a0`'s and no row over 15 m/s where `a0`
  writes none.
- **V4.** On kirk0, HOTA and IDF1 delta 5th percentiles above −0.005 and the switch change's 95th
  percentile +10 or less.
- **V5.** Noise costs under R4 (peak test) on no more than two of the six segments.
- **Falsifier.** If V3's new fast rows remain at both columbus-broadway and franklin-mcallister,
  the invented speed is not a shadow birth and the shadow test is not the lever.
- **Outcome.** A pass names `shadow` for the second segments and label0003's spans. Nothing is
  adopted.

**What could mislead.** The six segments, kirk0 and the shadow test have all been read for this
question, and the form was chosen after l5est0010; a pass is a reason to read unread segments. The
shadow test reads the published box, which for a near car spans tens of degrees, so real births
behind a nearer body also take the shipped prior (they then behave as under `a0`).
