# l5id0001: one vehicle, one track

**Question.** The published box refinement found two confirmed tracks on one vehicle behind every
box defect it could not fix: on kirk0, truck 1's close pass is taken by two tracks in alternate
frames, each frame flagged a merge, and five pairs of vehicle-sized tracks (seven of 21 tracks)
stay within 2 m of each other for five frames or more; on the corpus a tenth of vehicle rows
overlap another confirmed track's box under the legacy box. Two tracks on one vehicle count it
twice. The tracker already carries default-off identity options that decide who may take a
cluster: the reacquisition guard, contested rejoins (a track that missed the last frame may not
take a cluster a live track explains about as well), the likelihood cost that removes a coasting
track's discount, and tentative priority. Does any of them hold one track per vehicle without
losing the vehicles?

**Measure.** A duplicate pair is two confirmed tracks whose published boxes, each 3 m or longer,
share more than half the smaller box's area on five or more scored frames: two vehicles cannot
overlap. Counted by `lidar-box-eval` on every arm's own replay, with the box model of the defaults.

**Rule, written before any candidate's replay (2026-10-17).** Only a0's kirk0 figures above were
seen. A candidate passes on the tuning set when:

1. its target improves: pooled over the first segment of 23 sites and the two kirk captures, the
   duplicate pairs per hundred vehicle tracks fall by a third or more against a0;
2. it loses nothing on kirk0's tuning split, scored by the per-frame evaluator against the
   reviewed pack: matches (true positives) at least a0's × 0.98, HOTA and IDF1 each no lower
   than a0's less 0.005, identity switches no more than a0's, fragmentations no more than a0's
   × 1.1 + 1;
3. it keeps the vehicles on the corpus: vehicle-sized scored frames at least a0's × 0.95 (a
   duplicate's frames are among them, so some fall is expected).

A candidate that passes is scored on the fifth segment of the 11 sites that have one, read for
the first time, where its duplicate pairs per hundred vehicle tracks must fall by at least half
as much and the third clause hold. A candidate that passes both is promising, not adopted: the
backlog asks for held-out per-frame identity comparisons before an identity option ships, and
kirk0's frozen held-out split is not read here.

**Result (2026-10-17).** No candidate passed. Duplicate pairs per hundred vehicle tracks: a0
15.5, the guard 13.2, contested rejoins 12.5, contested rejoins with the likelihood cost 6.9,
tentative priority 13.4. Only the likelihood arm met the target, and it lost 8.7 % of vehicle
frames and kirk0's HOTA (0.376 to 0.358, through association accuracy). Tentative priority met
every reviewed clause (HOTA 0.385, IDF1 0.390, 117 identity switches against 124) and missed the
target. Report: [one vehicle, two tracks](../../../docs/lidar/operations/l5-duplicate-tracks-2026-10.md).
