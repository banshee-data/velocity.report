# l5id0002: ending a coasting track that lies on a live one

**Question.** In l5id0001 no identity option held one track per vehicle without a cost: contested
rejoins with the likelihood cost halved the duplicate pairs but lost vehicle frames and kirk0's
association accuracy; tentative priority kept the reviewed score and cut duplicates by a seventh.
On kirk0, 121 of the 188 overlapping frames of its duplicate pairs have one track coasting on top
of the live one that took its vehicle, for up to its 15-frame miss budget. Two road users cannot
occupy the same ground, and an occluded one is behind its occluder, never inside it. Does ending a
coasting track whose drawn box shares more than half the smaller box with a live confirmed
track's, on the first or the third such frame of its coast, remove the duplicates without losing
road users?

**Rule, written before any of its replays (2026-10-17).** l5id0001's figures for a0 and its four
arms were seen; nothing of these arms was. A candidate passes on the tuning set when:

1. its target improves: pooled over the first segment of 23 sites and the two kirk captures, the
   duplicate pairs per hundred vehicle tracks fall by a third or more against a0;
2. it loses nothing on kirk0's tuning split, by the per-frame evaluator against the reviewed
   pack: matches at least a0's × 0.98, HOTA and IDF1 each no lower than a0's less 0.005,
   identity switches no more than a0's, fragmentations no more than a0's × 1.1 + 1;
3. it keeps the vehicles: vehicle-sized scored frames outside duplicate pairs (vehicle frames
   less pair-frames) at least a0's × 0.98. (l5id0001 counted every vehicle frame, a duplicate's
   included, which charged a candidate for the duplicates it removed.)

A candidate that passes is scored on the fifth segment of the 11 sites that have one, read for
the first time, where its duplicate pairs per hundred vehicle tracks must fall by at least half as
much and the third clause hold. One that passes both is promising, not adopted: an identity
option needs a held-out per-frame identity comparison before it ships, kirk0 has no held-out
split, and the labelled held-out window is label0001's.
