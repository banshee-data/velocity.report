# l5id0003: the overlap end, kept to the vehicles it observes

**Question.** In l5id0002 the overlap end removed nearly every duplicate pair and improved kirk0's
reviewed identity figures, but failed a clause that counted coasting frames, which is what it
removes. Does it keep the vehicles when only the frames in which a confirmed track holds a
vehicle-sized cluster are counted?

**Rule, written before any figure under the new measure (2026-10-17).** l5id0002's figures were
seen: duplicate pairs, vehicle frames with coasting, and the kirk0 per-frame scores. The observed
frames were not, on any arm. A candidate passes on the tuning set (the first segment of 23 sites
and the two kirk captures) when:

1. its duplicate pairs per hundred vehicle tracks fall by a third or more against a0;
2. its kirk0 per-frame score meets l5id0002's second clause (that score is l5id0002's: the
   tracker is unchanged, only the scorer counts more);
3. its observed vehicle frames (vehicle-sized rows in which the track holds a cluster, of tracks
   drawn vehicle-sized on five or more frames) are at least a0's × 0.98.

A candidate that passes is scored, with a0, on the fifth segments, read for the first time, where
its duplicate pairs per hundred vehicle tracks must fall by at least half as much as on the tuning
set and its observed vehicle frames stay at least a0's × 0.98. The held-out result decides. One
that passes both is promising, not adopted: an identity option needs a held-out per-frame identity
comparison before it ships, kirk0 has no held-out split, and the labelled held-out window is
label0001's.

**Result (2026-10-17).** No candidate passed. On the tuning set all three did (observed vehicle
frames 99.8 %, 98.1 % and 105.3 % of a0's). On the fifth segment of 9 sites (Embarcadero at
Broadway's is shorter than the warm-up too) the duplicates fell as far (15.1 to 0.51, 8.7 and 0.54
pairs per hundred vehicle tracks), but the observed vehicle frames fell to 93.0 %, 97.1 % and
96.3 % of a0's, most of it on Fulton at Divisadero, where the rule ends real cars in queues whose
floored boxes overlap. Report: [one vehicle, two tracks](../../../docs/lidar/operations/l5-duplicate-tracks-2026-10.md#l5id0003-kept-to-the-vehicles-it-observes).
