# l5id0004: the overlap end on the body's returns, keeping the older track

**Question.** l5id0003's overlap end removed the duplicates and improved kirk0's identity figures,
but held out it ended real cars in queues: it compared drawn boxes, which the box model extends by
its floor and held extents into the next car. And it ended the coasting track whatever its age, so
the survivor was sometimes the younger, with the shorter memory. Does ending a coasting track only
when its predicted position lies within a live confirmed track's observed returns (the cluster's
extent along the live track's heading, grown by a quarter metre) keep the cars, and does ending the
younger of the two keep the better identity?

**Rule, written before any of its replays (2026-10-17).** l5id0001-0003's figures were seen. A
candidate passes on the tuning set (the first segment of 23 sites and the two kirk captures) when:

1. its duplicate pairs per hundred vehicle tracks fall by a third or more against a0;
2. on kirk0's tuning split, by the per-frame evaluator against the reviewed pack: matches at least
   a0's × 0.98, HOTA and IDF1 each no lower than a0's less 0.005, identity switches no more than
   a0's, fragmentations no more than a0's × 1.1 + 1;
3. its observed vehicle frames are at least a0's × 0.98.

A candidate that passes is scored, with a0, on the second segment of 22 sites, which no identity
measure has read, where its duplicate pairs per hundred vehicle tracks must fall by at least half
as much as on the tuning set and its observed vehicle frames stay at least a0's × 0.98. The
held-out result decides. If both pass, the one with more observed vehicle frames held out is the
candidate. One that passes is promising, not adopted: an identity option needs a held-out
per-frame identity comparison before it ships, and the labelled held-out window is label0001's.
