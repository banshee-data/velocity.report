# l5id0005: replicating l5id0004 on the third segments

**Question.** l5id0004's `body-older` (the overlap end on the body's returns, keeping the older
track) passed on the tuning set and on the second segment of 22 sites. Does it hold on a third
set of captures that no identity measure has read?

**Rule, written before the replays (2026-10-17).** On the third segment of the 22 sites, against
a0, `body-older` replicates when its duplicate pairs per hundred vehicle tracks fall by at least
half its tuning-set fall (15.5 to 1.3, so by 7.1 or more) and its observed vehicle frames are at
least a0's × 0.98. It changes no default either way: adoption still waits on label0001.
