# l5id0006: the surviving track's box, and tentative priority with the overlap end

**Question.** l5id0004's overlap end keeps the older track and ends the younger, and with it
kirk0's reviewed axis p90 rises from 8.4° to 13.4°: a surviving track whose box has not converged
restarts its box. The duplicate tracks report asks for two things on top of it: give the
surviving track's box the ended track's heading and extent beliefs, and read tentative priority
again with the overlap end, since alone it improved every reviewed figure and with the box-overlap
end took fragmentations to 31. Does either keep what `body-older` won while giving back the
axis, and what does one track per vehicle do to the count of vehicles in a 50 m zone, the figure
the fragment-stitching papers report?

**Rule, written before the replays (2026-10-18).** Against `body-older` on the tuning set (the
first segment of 23 sites and the two kirk captures):

1. `body-older-inherit` passes when kirk0's reviewed axis p90 falls to within 1° of the defaults'
   8.4° with the reviewed centre median no worse than `body-older`'s, duplicate pairs per hundred
   vehicle tracks no higher than `body-older`'s plus 0.5, observed vehicle frames at least
   `body-older`'s × 0.99, and on kirk0's tuning split HOTA and IDF1 each no lower than
   `body-older`'s less 0.005 and identity switches no more;
2. `body-older-tentative` passes when its fragmentations are lower than `body-older`'s with the
   same clauses on duplicates, observed vehicle frames, HOTA, IDF1 and switches.

A candidate that passes is read, with `a0` and `body-older`, on the fourth segment of 20 sites,
where its duplicates must stay within 0.5 per hundred of `body-older`'s and its observed vehicle
frames at least `body-older`'s × 0.99. Neither changes a default: the overlap end itself still
waits on label0001's window. The 50 m count error is reported for every arm and rules nothing.

**What it does not establish.** Anything on the held-out labelled window; the count error's value
off kirk0, where there are no reviewed objects to count against.
