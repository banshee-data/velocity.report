# l5id0010: fragment links on the held-out window

**Question.** Does the linker carried from l5id0009 keep its precision on data no part of it was
tuned or drawn on, and when counting, per-vehicle speed and following read chains instead of
tracks, does anything change that should not?

**Method.** The held-out window from label0001, with label0002's following records. The linker's
arm and parameters are frozen before the window's map is drawn, and the map is drawn without
opening the window's labels or tracks.

**Rule, written before the replays (2026-10-21).**

1. `carried` passes when its vehicle link precision on the held-out window is at least 0.98, its
   HOTA and IDF1 are no lower than `a0`'s less 0.005, its identity switches no more, and its
   observed vehicle seconds equal `a0`'s exactly.
2. With chains: the following metric's eligible observed time is unchanged against `a0` to the
   frame (links add identity, not observation); no following record pairs a chain with itself;
   per-vehicle max speeds are each the maximum of an observed sample of one of the chain's tracks.
3. The 50 m zone count error against the window's reviewed objects is reported for both arms.
4. Rule 1 is read only when the window holds at least 30 true vehicle links. Below that this
   spec is answered as unreadable, persistence (P5) is not decided, and the report says how long
   a window would carry the rule at l5id0007's link rate.

Every ratio is reported with its counts and a Clopper–Pearson 95% interval.

A pass is the evidence for a `D-NN` on persisting links (plan phase P5). It does not adopt a
live linker, which needs its own timing experiment on the Pi.

**What could mislead.** One held-out window at one site; a pass bounds what a busier site may
show, it does not guarantee it. Following records change if their leader decision used a track
that is now part of a chain; the report lists every such record.

**What it does not establish.** Live or fixed-lag behaviour; any site but the held-out one.
