# l5id0011: the likelihood cost with containment as an input

**Question.** The association cost is bare squared Mahalanobis distance, and a coasting track's
covariance is inflated on purpose, so a coasting track outbids a fresh one for the same cluster
(gap analysis S3). The likelihood cost d² + ln|S| charges a vague track for its vagueness. In
[l5id0001](l5id0001.md) it was the only arm to halve duplicate tracks on kirk0, but it lost 8.7 %
of vehicle frames and kirk0's HOTA. The maths decision review (E-05) sequenced it after W2, so
that the box's containment could say which clusters it was right to refuse. W2's containment is
scored ([geom0005](../geom/geom0005.md)). With containment as an input, does the likelihood cost
keep its duplicate gain without losing the vehicles?

**Rule, written before any replay (2026-10-22).** No arm's figures have been seen. The thresholds
are l5id0001's, so the two answers compare. A candidate (`lik`, `claim`, `lik-claim`) passes on
tuning when:

1. pooled over the 23 first segments and the two kirk captures, its duplicate pairs per hundred
   vehicle tracks fall by a third or more against `a0`;
2. on kirk0's tuning split, scored by the per-frame evaluator against the reviewed pack, its
   matches are at least `a0`'s × 0.98, HOTA and IDF1 each no lower than `a0`'s less 0.005,
   identity switches no more than `a0`'s, and fragmentations no more than `a0`'s × 1.1 + 1;
3. on the corpus, its vehicle-sized scored frames are at least `a0`'s × 0.95.

A candidate that passes is scored once on label0001's frozen held-out window, where clause 2 must
hold again. Passing both makes it promotable, not adopted. Retiring `occlusion_cov_inflation`
is then a spec of its own, as E-05 requires: it waits for this comparison.

If tentative priority ([l5id0006](l5id0006.md)) is adopted before this runs, every arm carries it,
and the rule is unchanged.

**What it does not establish.** Physical accuracy of any track, which G-GEO-1 owns; identity at
sites the held-out window does not resemble; or anything about coasting longer than the shipped
`max_misses`.
