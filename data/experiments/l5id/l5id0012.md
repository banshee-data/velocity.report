# l5id0012: the overlap end with tentative priority as the default

**Question.** l5id0006 found the overlap end with tentative priority (`overlap_end_1`,
`overlap_end_body`, `overlap_end_older` and `tentative_priority`, the arm `body-older-tentative`)
takes kirk0's identity switches from 87 to 5 and lowers duplicates at every site read, and left it
off because an identity option was to wait for label0001's labelled held-out window, which does not
exist yet. Its cost was measured only as brief tracks (+61 % at the median site) and clutter
(+32 %). Before anyone decides to make it the default without label0001, what would the flip cost a
user across the corpus: real tracks lost or carried in pieces, spurious tracks added, the vehicle
count, the speeds a report gives, duplicates, and kirk0's and kirk1's identity figures?

**Arms.** `a0`, the defaults, and `overlap-end`, the four options together. No new code: the
options exist, default off.

**Measures** ([`lidar_flip_cost.py`](../../../scripts/lidar_flip_cost.py), definitions fixed in the
script before any replay). A moving track lasts 2 s or more, moves 5 m or more first to last and
has a median speed of 1 m/s or more: what a traffic count reads. An `a0` moving track is lost when
under half its rows have an `overlap-end` row within 2 m at the same frame, and shortened when it is
covered but no single `overlap-end` track covers half of it. Added tracks are the converse. Clutter
is brief (under 1 s) and near-static tracks together, as
[`lidar_noise_screen.py`](../../../scripts/lidar_noise_screen.py) defines them. Speeds are each
matched pair's p85 row speed, `overlap-end` less `a0`. Duplicates are `lidar-box-eval`'s pairs per
hundred vehicle tracks.

**Rule, written before the replays (2026-11-03).** Pooled over a set's sites unless a site count is
given, `overlap-end` against `a0`. The flip is recommended when every clause holds on the first
segment of the 23 sites (the tuning set, read before by l5id0006 for duplicates, brief tracks and
clutter, never for the others) and again on the held-out set:

1. **Lost real tracks:** at most 1 % of `a0`'s moving tracks, and no site loses more than 5 % of its
   own (or 2 tracks, whichever is more).
2. **Shortened real tracks:** at most 2 % of `a0`'s moving tracks.
3. **Spurious tracks:** clutter added (`overlap-end`'s less `a0`'s) at most 5 per hundred of `a0`'s
   moving tracks, and added moving tracks at most 2 per hundred.
4. **Counts:** moving tracks no more than `a0`'s × 1.02 (ending a duplicate may lower the count;
   clause 1 bounds a fall that is a lost vehicle), at 20 or more of the sites.
5. **Speeds:** over matched pairs, the median absolute p85 difference at most 0.1 m/s and the 95th
   percentile at most 1.0 m/s; the mean difference within ±0.2 m/s.
6. **Duplicates:** pairs per hundred vehicle tracks fall at 20 or more of the 23 sites (at 19 or
   more of the held-out 22).
7. **kirk** (tuning only): on kirk0's tuning split by the per-frame evaluator, HOTA and IDF1 no
   lower than `a0`'s less 0.005 and identity switches no more than `a0`'s; on kirk1, covered road
   users of its 49 no fewer than `a0`'s less 1.

The held-out set is the third five-minute capture of the 22 sites that have one and are not
label0001's, read here for the first time for any measure, with clauses 1 to 6. It is read whatever
the tuning set's result, since the question is a cost account rather than a choice among candidates;
the verdict is the conjunction of both sets. Each clause's figure is reported with the site range and
a worst leave-one-out line.

**Scope changes before any figure was read (2026-11-03).** The bounds and sets above are as first
written. Six replays, two of them interrupted, were made and set aside unread on the way: a cut to 20 s warm-up and
60 s scored with 11 held-out sites and no kirk1 (commits `54395c48`, `a6b72302`), made because an
evidence database tripled replay time on arrow-worker, was refused at 20 s as unsettled, ran once at
35 s, and was undone when replays without a database proved fast enough for the scope as written.
What remains of those changes: the corpus and held-out replays write no evidence database, and the
scorer reads `lidar-box-eval -rows` (each scored frame of each confirmed track), where a track's
duration is its span within the scored window, the same for both arms; its test checks the two
sources agree.

**Not a default change.** The owner decides the flip: a pass is a recommendation in the report and
the options stay off in this PR. The flip would also move the defaults' fingerprint, which this run
does not touch.

**What it does not establish.** Whether any lost, added or merged track at the 22 unlabelled sites
was right: lost and added are measured against `a0`, not truth. Live timing (the overlap end cost
about 40 % more replay time in l5id0006, on another host). label0001's labelled identity
comparison, which this does not replace.
