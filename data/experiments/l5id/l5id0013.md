# l5id0013: the overlap end's cost, rescored with a body-aware match

**Question.** l5id0012 found the overlap end with tentative priority fails its lost-track clause
(10.0 % and 10.2 % of `a0`'s moving tracks against a bound of 1 %), and a diagnostic written
afterwards found 212 of 251 and 224 of 267 lost tracks had another `a0` track within 6 m on half
their frames: the surviving half of a duplicate pair on a long vehicle, further than the scorer's
2 m point match reaches. The same run raised brief tracks from 61 to 580 and 90 to 816, and
nobody has looked at what they are. The rows are archived for both arms and both sets. Rescored
with a match that knows the body, how many real moving tracks does the flip lose and shorten, what
are the brief tracks it leaves, and where does its speed tail come from?

**Measures, fixed here before any row is read again.** The rows are `lidar-box-eval -rows`
(every scored frame of every confirmed track: `t_ns`, `x`, `y`, `psi_deg`, `length`, `width`,
`speed`, `class`). l5id0012's definitions of brief, lasting, near-static, moving, lost, shortened
and added stand, with one change to coverage:

- **Covered (footprint).** A row of A is covered by B when some B row at the same frame has a drawn
  footprint (its `psi_deg`, `length` and `width`, each extent grown by 0.25 m, as
  `overlap_end_body` grows a live body) that contains the A row's centre, or whose footprint overlaps the A row's
  footprint by half the smaller's area or more. Coverage, best single-track coverage, lost,
  shortened and added then follow l5id0012's definitions with this test in place of the 2 m point.
- **Re-birth.** A brief B track born within 1.0 s after a B track ended by the overlap end on the
  same body (its first row's centre inside the survivor's grown footprint at that frame), whose
  rows are inside a surviving lasting B track's grown footprint on half or more of its frames.
- **Split.** A brief B track that is not a re-birth, concurrent with a lasting moving B track and
  inside its grown footprint on half or more of its frames.
- **Other.** Any brief B track that is neither.
- **Speed tail by age.** Each matched pair's p85 difference (B less A), binned by the B track's age
  at the pair's first common frame: under 1 s, 1 to 3 s, 3 s or more.

**Rule, written before any row is read again (2026-11-05).** This is a rescoring: it decides how
l5id0014 is shaped, not a default.

1. If, with the footprint match, lost real tracks are at most 1 % of `a0`'s moving tracks pooled
   on both sets and shortened at most 2 %, l5id0012's clauses 1 and 2 are recorded as failures of
   the measure, l5id0014 reads them with the footprint match, and the overlap end's remaining
   measured costs are clause 3 (clutter) and clause 5 (the speed tail).
2. If lost real tracks stay over 1 % on either set, the remaining lost tracks are listed per site
   with span, length, class and nearest B track, the worst site is named for a person to look at
   in the visualiser, and the overlap end stays where l5id0012 left it; l5id0014 still runs, with
   the footprint match, since the birth gate removes different tracks.
3. If 80 % or more of B's brief tracks, pooled, are re-births or splits on a surviving body,
   l5id0014's birth gate is the lever and its spec stands. If under 80 %, the brief tracks are new
   tracks the option creates elsewhere, l5id0014 is amended before its replays to say what it
   removes, and l6cls0004 reads the remainder.
4. The speed tail is reported by age bin and decides nothing; l5id0014's speed clause is read on
   its own rows.

Every figure is given pooled and by site with a worst leave-one-out line. No replay is made; the
rows are l5id0012's, at the commit and fingerprint its report records.

**What could mislead.** A footprint grown by 0.25 m on a bus can cover a motorcycle alongside; the
split class absorbs a real second road user travelling inside another's footprint, which the
rows cannot tell from a fragment. A re-birth within 1.0 s is a timing prior, not an identity.
The rows hold confirmed tracks only, so a tentative track the overlap end never let confirm is
invisible here, which is what l5id0014 is for.

**What it does not establish.** Whether any lost track was a real vehicle: lost is against `a0`,
not truth, and the 1 % bound is a non-inferiority bound on a proxy. label0003's spans are the
first truth that can count them. Nothing about live timing.
