# l5id0014: a birth gate on the live body

**Question.** The overlap end ends a duplicate after it has coasted onto a live body, and when it
ends the younger of the two it leaves the body's cluster unowned for a frame, so a new tentative
track is born on the body, confirms in four frames and is ended in turn: the brief tracks that
rose from 61 to 580 in l5id0012, if l5id0016 finds they are re-births. A gate at birth would stop
that: a tentative track whose predicted position lies inside a live confirmed track's observed
returns is not allowed to confirm, and dies if it stays there. Does the birth gate, with the
overlap end and tentative priority, remove the duplicates without leaving the brief tracks, and
without losing the queued cars the drawn-box overlap end lost in l5id0003?

**Arms.** `a0`; `overlap-end`, l5id0012's four options; `birth-gate`, the same with
`birth_gate_body`. The gate reuses the on-body test of `overlap_end_body` and is refused without
it.

**Rule, written before any replay (2026-11-05).** l5id0012's seven clauses, pooled over the first
segment of the 23 sites unless a site count is given, `birth-gate` against `a0`, with clauses 1
and 2 read by l5id0016's footprint match. The sites are a screen: identity, noise and counts have
read every corpus segment, so no corpus read is held out here (the review's held-out ledger).

1. **Lost real tracks:** at most 1 % of `a0`'s moving tracks, and no site over 5 % (or 2 tracks).
2. **Shortened:** at most 2 %.
3. **Spurious tracks:** clutter added at most 5 per hundred of `a0`'s moving tracks, and added
   moving tracks at most 2 per hundred. Brief tracks are reported beside `overlap-end`'s: the gate
   is meant to remove them, and a brief count above `overlap-end`'s half fails this clause whatever
   the clutter figure says.
4. **Counts:** moving tracks no more than `a0`'s × 1.02 at 20 or more sites.
5. **Speeds:** over matched pairs, median |Δp85| at most 0.1 m/s, p95 at most 1.0 m/s, mean within
   ±0.2 m/s.
6. **Duplicates:** pairs per hundred vehicle tracks fall at 20 or more of the 23 sites, and are
   within 0.5 per hundred of `overlap-end`'s pooled: the gate must not give back what the overlap
   end won.
7. **kirk:** kirk0 HOTA and IDF1 no lower than `a0`'s less 0.005 and identity switches no more
   than `overlap-end`'s plus 5; kirk1 covered road users no fewer than `a0`'s less 1. A kirk0
   HOTA or IDF1 difference under 0.01 is read as not distinguishable until l5id0015 gives the
   interval.

**Held out.** The candidate's held-out clause is label0003's spans on label0001's window: no
labelled road user's span uncovered that `a0` covers (30 % overlap), moving count within `a0`'s
× 1.02, and duplicates lower. A pass on the sites and on the spans makes `birth-gate` the
candidate for label0001's per-frame identity comparison. Nothing here changes a default.

**What could mislead.** Two cars in a queue whose clusters touch: the second car's first frames
can lie inside the first's grown extent, so a real vehicle's track is born late or not at all.
Clause 1 by footprint and the kirk1 coverage read that. A body that splits into two clusters, each
with a track, is not a duplicate the gate can see at birth if neither cluster lies inside the
other; the duplicates clause reads whether such pairs remain.

**What it does not establish.** Any identity figure off kirk0 before label0001; live timing (the
overlap end cost about 40 % more replay time on a Mac in l5id0006; the gate's cost is reported
and does not gate); whether a lost track at an unlabelled site was real.
