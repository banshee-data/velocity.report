# l5id0015: the width of kirk0's identity figures

**Question.** Every identity clause this autumn has read kirk0's HOTA, IDF1 and switch count as
exact: no lower than `a0` less 0.005, within 0.01, no more switches. The split holds 21 reviewed
road users, two of them trucks that carry most of the fragmentation, and the `a0` figure itself
differs between reports (HOTA 0.4293 with 87 switches in the noise and splits run, 0.4283 with 90
in l5id0012) for no recorded cause. A replay is byte-deterministic, so the uncertainty is in the
sample. How wide does a bootstrap over the reviewed objects make these figures, and which of the
round's claimed deltas stand inside it?

**Measures.** For each arm, 2,000 resamples of the split's reviewed objects with replacement; on
each, HOTA, IDF1, identity switches and fragmentations recomputed over the resampled objects'
frames; the 5th, 50th and 95th percentiles. For each candidate against `a0`, the same resamples
applied to both (paired), and the percentiles of the delta.

**Rule (2026-11-05).** This is a measurement; it decides how later clauses are written, not a
default.

1. The `a0` interval's half-width (95th less 5th, halved) for HOTA, IDF1 and switches is recorded
   in `data/experiments/l5id/README.md` as the kirk0 anchor, with the commit and tool sha, and
   every later l5id spec states its kirk0 bounds against it.
2. A round delta stands when the paired delta's 5th percentile is on the same side of zero as its
   median. The deltas read: `overlap-end`'s switches (87 to 5) and HOTA (+0.0074), the radius
   graph's HOTA (+0.0044) and its 20 to 35 m fragmented share (6.5 to 4.5 %, read as a count of
   fragmented instants in the band).
3. If the `a0` HOTA half-width is 0.01 or more, every clause in the open l5id specs written at
   0.005 or 0.01 is listed in the report as not distinguishable as written, for the owner to
   reset; no clause is changed by this spec.

**The `a0` anchor.** The report records `a0`'s HOTA, IDF1, switches and fragmentations at this
commit, and whether they equal either of the two November figures. If neither, the cause is found
and recorded before the write step closes; the review's F22 asks for this check in every later
run.

**What could mislead.** Objects are not independent: the two trucks share an episode, and a
resample that draws both twice doubles their fragmentation. The interval is a sampling interval
over the objects a reviewer chose, not over road users at large. Fragmentations and switches are
counts, so their resampled values are not comparable to the full split's absolute figures; the
report gives them as rates per object as well.

**What it does not establish.** Anything off kirk0; the width on label0001's window, which is its
own read once the window exists; whether a delta that stands is right, which only labels say.

**Answer (2026-11-05).** On kirk0's 19 scored objects, the defaults' labelled-object HOTA runs from
0.413 to 0.540 and their switches from 4 to 242 (5th to 95th percentile, 2,000 draws); the
half-widths, 0.063 HOTA, 0.057 IDF1 and 119 switches, are recorded in the area README as the kirk0
anchor. Of the round's deltas only the overlap end's switches keep their sign; its HOTA and the
radius graph's do not. The HOTA half-width is over 0.01, so the open l5id clauses at 0.005 are
listed in the report as not distinguishable on kirk0. The pack was reconstructed in the cloud. See
the [report](../../../docs/lidar/operations/cloud-run-kirk0-labelled-2026-11.md#l5id0015-how-wide-kirk0s-figures-are).
