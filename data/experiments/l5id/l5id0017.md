# l5id0017: the overlap end and the velocity noise together

**Question.** On kirk0 the overlap end with tentative priority takes identity switches from 90 to
5, and doubling the velocity process noise takes them from 90 to 43. Do the two remove the same
errors (the duplicates a coasting track leaves) or different ones, so that a corpus read of
l5est0004 should carry the combination as an arm?

**Rule, written before either replay (2026-11-05).** A screen; it adopts nothing. Against
`overlap-end`, by the paired bootstrap:

1. **Different errors.** The combination's HOTA or IDF1 gain keeps its sign (5th and 95th
   percentiles above zero), with switches no more than `overlap-end`'s plus 3 and brief tracks no
   more than its plus 3: l5est0004 then gains an `overlap-end-vel-x2` arm before its corpus read.
2. **The same errors.** Neither gain keeps its sign: l5est0004 runs as written, and the report says
   the velocity noise's kirk0 gain lies in what the overlap end already removes.

**What it does not establish.** Anything off kirk0.
