# l5id0018: guard containment refusals with global assignment cardinality

The local claim keeps a bidder in every row but can leave two rows competing for their only
remaining column. The counterexample in #934 reduces a two-pair assignment to one. This question
changes only that failure mode; it does not retune covariance, body geometry or birth thresholds.

## Rule written before replay

The new optional guard is off by default. It tentatively applies all claim refusals, compares
maximum matching cardinality before and after, and restores the whole original matrix if the
cardinality fell. Restored edges do not count as accepted claim refusals; a separate counter
records fallback events. The original unguarded arms remain available unchanged.

1. **Engineering acceptance:** preserve maximum matching size for rectangular, sparse, conflicting
   and partially gated matrices, including the published two-by-two counterexample. Never admit
   an originally forbidden edge or change a surviving edge's original cost. Tests cover accepted
   refusals, fallback, no-refusal paths and repeatability. Both replay runs must agree exactly.
2. **Development screen:** compare each guarded arm with its paired unguarded claim at the same
   source revision. Report duplicate vehicle pairs and their rate, observed vehicle frames,
   births, accepted refusals and guard fallbacks. A useful local candidate must cut duplicate
   rate by at least one third against `a0`, retain at least 95 % of its observed vehicle frames,
   lose no more than 0.01 HOTA, and add no identity switches or fragmentations against `a0`.
   Also report guarded-minus-unguarded changes without treating fewer births as proof of cause.
3. **No default adoption:** kirk0 is already development data. The reconstructed pack has exact
   return identities but different decoded coordinates from the published points file. No
   independent labelled held-out window is available here, and the corpus has already been read
   for identity/noise measures. Local success cannot satisfy W2 containment, physical accuracy,
   multi-site non-inferiority, or Pi timing. A local failure leaves the candidate off as well.

Wall-time comparisons on this shared cloud host are descriptive only. The fallback preserves
assignment count, not assignment identity, physical correctness, or a global no-birth guarantee
across the rest of the tracker lifecycle. There is no held-out read in this screen.

## Answer

The engineering safeguard passed. Both guarded candidates failed the local screen and remain
off. Guarded claim used 19 whole-matrix fallbacks and changed births from 158 to 148, switches
from 129 to 78 and duplicate pairs from 21 to 16. With likelihood, 31 fallbacks accompanied
births from 152 to 143 and pairs from 22 to 18. Guarded duplicate rates remain above the
control's, and HOTA 0.401524/0.407094 is below the required 0.418375. All five repeats agreed;
observed vehicle-row coverage passed. Full results and frozen provenance are in the
[report](../../../docs/lidar/operations/containment-cardinality-guard-cloud-2026-11.md).
