# l5id0019: the November fixes, benchmarked, and the next fixes they point to

**Question.** Between November 6 and 8 seven default-off options landed on main: two-point
velocity initialisation and covariance hygiene (#969), the robust likelihood cost (#983), the
solid body's face state (#970, #982, #989), the published box's extents from that state (#984),
one orientation shared by the box and the solid body (#985) and track-conditioned membership for
the fragment join (#986). Each was screened on its own question. What does each do to identity,
geometry and noise, measured with the same tools on the same captures, and what do the measured
tables say the next background-noise (L3, L4) and tracking (L5) fixes should be?

**Arms.** One option at a time against `a0` (the defaults), and one combined arm. Arm 5
(`face-state`) is a shadow: its tracks must equal `a0`'s. `near-edge` puts the same solid body on
the tracked filter, which needs `solid_body_full_members`. `box-extent` changes the published box
and, through the L3 track guard, the tracks. `join-membership` is l4clus0010's `fix` arm, without
the face state. `combined` is two-point, robust, `shared-orientation` and `join-membership`
together. Any part of an arm the replay refuses is reported verbatim and dropped, and the arm runs
without it.

**Cases.** kirk0 (`20251205T1417_80858-75c_kirkham-9th`), labelled, whole, 6 s warm-up. For the
label-free measures, also the first two five-minute segments of
`20260901T1225_80858-0ac_ashbury-downey` and `20260901T1259_80858-0ac_haight-divisadero` from
`banshee-data/sf-street-speeds`: the dataset's first three captures, none of them the held-out
embarcadero-folsom. At spec time (2026-11-08T22:04Z) the dataset's file CDN
(`cas-server.xethub.hf.co`, `us.aws.cdn.hf.co`) was refused by this host's network policy, so if
that still holds the placements' rows are not read and the report says so.

**Measures.**

1. **Identity** (kirk0). `lidar-ground-truth-eval perframe` on the reconstructed reviewed pack
   (`lidar-pack-rebuild`, as the [cloud labelled run](../../../docs/lidar/operations/cloud-run-kirk0-labelled-2026-11.md)
   built it), split drafted with `-from-seconds 6 -split kirk0-tuning`, `obj_788622b5` ignored,
   online estimates as declared baselines: HOTA, IDF1, identity switches and fragmentations, with
   the paired bootstrap against `a0` (2,000 draws of the scored objects, seed 20261108).
2. **Geometry** (kirk0). The physical scorer on the 22 reviewed poses of
   [geom0015](geom0015.md)'s three vehicles, gate 3 m: the published box's matched count and median
   centre error, overall and by vehicle; for the solid-body arms the solid body's as well; the
   believed and published truck lengths against the reference lengths.
3. **Noise and counts** (each case). Duplicate pairs per hundred vehicle tracks (`lidar-box-eval`);
   brief tracks, near-static tracks, spurious clusters a frame (`lidar_noise_screen.py`); clutter
   split into artefact, stationary road user and fragment of a mover, and the unowned-cluster
   recurrence grid (`lidar_clutter_origin.py`, D13 and D14); moving tracks, lost, shortened and
   added against `a0` (`lidar_flip_cost.py --match footprint`); the moving count and its p85 and
   p98 against `a0` (`lidar_figure_sensitivity.py`).
4. **Cost** (kirk0). Tracker.Update and frame wall time, p50 and p99, from `-campaign-metrics`.

**Reading rule, written before any replay (2026-11-08).** A screen on a tuning capture and on
label-free data. Nothing is adopted and no default changes, whatever the result.

- **R1, identity.** An arm gains on HOTA or IDF1 when its paired 5th-to-95th interval lies wholly
  above zero, and on switches or fragmentations when wholly below; it loses when the interval lies
  wholly on the other side; otherwise there is no measurable change. It is non-inferior when the
  5th percentiles of the HOTA and IDF1 deltas are at or above −0.02 and the 95th of the switch
  delta at or below +10 (l4clus0010's J1).
- **R2, no-op.** An arm is a no-op on kirk0 when its tracking baseline equals `a0`'s byte for byte
  and, for a geometry arm, its box rows score as `a0`'s.
- **R3, geometry.** An arm moves the box when the median centre error over the matched reviewed
  poses moves by 0.05 m or more overall, or by 0.25 m or more for one vehicle, with no fewer
  matched poses; it moves truck length when the median absolute length error over the referenced
  truck instants moves by 0.5 m or more (l4clus0010's J3 bar).
- **R4, noise.** On each case an arm costs noise when, against `a0`, brief tracks rise by more than
  3, clutter by more than 5 per hundred moving tracks, or duplicate pairs per hundred vehicle
  tracks rise at all; it costs counts when the moving count moves by more than 5 % or p85 by more
  than 0.5 m/s.
- **R5, cost.** An arm whose Tracker.Update p99 or frame p99 is more than 25 % over `a0`'s is
  flagged for perf0001's Pi timing.
- **R6, what goes forward.** An arm is named for a held-out read on label0003's window when it
  gains on R1 or moves R3 toward the references, is R1 non-inferior, and costs no noise under R4.
  A no-op or a loss is named as such. The ranking of next fixes is written after the tables, from
  them, with each fix's evidence row, expected effect and what would falsify it; measured and
  inferred are kept apart.

**What could mislead.** kirk0 is one tuning capture with about 20 scored objects, two of them
trucks that carry most of the switches; its HOTA interval half-width is 0.063
([l5id README](README.md#the-kirk0-anchor)). The pack is reconstructed: its points are decoded by
the current parser. Label-free counts on kirk0 are tens of tracks, so a 5 % count bound is one or
two tracks. Timings are replay wall times on a cloud container, not the Pi. The L3 track guard
carries any L5 change into L3 and L4, so a tracking arm's noise figures are not only its own
layer's.

**What it does not establish.** Anything held out, anything on the corpus beyond the cases read,
physical truth beyond the 22 reviewed poses, or Pi cost.

**Answer (2026-11-08).** kirk0 only: the placements were refused at the dataset's CDN. The robust
likelihood cost gains on all four identity measures (switches 90 to 6, HOTA +0.0115 with the
interval +0.0023 to +0.0222) but costs brief tracks (+4) and clutter (+10.5 per hundred), so R6
does not name it. R6 names `box-extent` (a 0.0016 HOTA gain, while its box moves car 8 0.25 m
away) and `join-membership` (IDF1, spurious clusters −7 %). Hygiene and the face-state shadow are
no-ops. `near-edge` loses HOTA and IDF1, and two-point initialisation is not non-inferior on
switches. A bisect puts a 1.6 m loss in truck 2's solid-body length on #989. Every solid-body arm
is flagged for Pi timing. Nothing is adopted. See the
[report](../../../docs/lidar/operations/tracking-geometry-fixes-benchmark-2026-11.md).
