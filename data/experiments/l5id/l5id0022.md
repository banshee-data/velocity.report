# l5id0022: where the near-edge tracked filter loses identity

**Question.** The benchmark of the November fixes
(l5id0019, on the open benchmark branch, report
`docs/lidar/operations/tracking-geometry-fixes-benchmark-2026-11.md`) measured `near_edge_track` on
kirk0 at HOTA −0.061 (5th to 95th percentile −0.116 to −0.021) and IDF1 −0.106 against `a0`, with
switches 90 to 33 but duplicate pairs 10 to 23, and duplicates rising at all five cases (pooled 26
to 87). L3 and L4 hand it the same clusters, so the loss is in association or lifecycle. Its ranked
fix 4 asks for the zero-code arms first, to locate the loss, and then a fix of the cause. Which step
loses identity, and does a fix there restore it?

**Arms.** `ne` is the benchmark's `near-edge` arm. The four controls each add one thing to it:
`ne-a1` gates body-centre tracks on the medoid against the centre (the A1 ablation); `ne-robust`
ranks admitted pairings by the Student-t likelihood with the gate's own S; `ne-tentative` lets
tentative tracks choose first among their own clusters; `ne-overlap` is the overlap-end identity
set (`identity-body-older-tentative.json`: tentative priority, the overlap end at one frame on the
body, keeping the older track). The fix arm (`fix`) is the option this question implements, named
here by its replay experiment once it exists and before it runs. Nothing else changes: every arm
runs the defaults' L1 to L4.

**Case.** kirk0 (`20251205T1417_80858-75c_kirkham-9th`), whole, 6 s warm-up; reviewed pack
`ds_67842cb1b22b70e7` as reconstructed by `lidar-pack-rebuild`, split drafted with
`-from-seconds 6 -split kirk0-tuning`, `obj_788622b5` ignored. kirk0 only: the placements are not
read in this question.

**Measures.**

1. **Identity.** `lidar-ground-truth-eval perframe`, online stage as declared baselines: HOTA,
   IDF1, switches, fragmentations, missed reference points and tracks. The paired bootstrap
   (2,000 draws of the scored objects, seed 20261108), each arm against `a0`, and each `ne`
   variant (the four controls and `fix`) against `ne`.
2. **Duplicates.** `lidar-box-eval`'s duplicate vehicle pairs, pair-frames and vehicle tracks.
3. **Diagnosis** (`ne` and `fix`, unscored). For each body-centre track and frame, the pairings
   refused before the cost, split by the plausibility check and by the d² gate, and the clusters
   it lost in the assignment. For each birth, whether its seed cluster lay within 3 m of a live
   body-centre track's predicted position or of its medoid-referenced position (the position
   less the last re-reference offset), and why that track did not take it. The counts come from a
   scratch build and are evidence for the mechanism, not a score.

## Rule written before replay

- **R0, anchor.** `a0` reproduces l5id0019's kirk0 figures (HOTA 0.4284, IDF1 0.4728, 90
  switches, 46 fragmentations, 10 duplicate pairs) and `ne` its `near-edge` row (HOTA 0.3539, IDF1
  0.3514, 33 switches, 51 fragmentations, 23 duplicate pairs). If either does not, the report says
  so and every comparison is against this build's own `a0` and `ne`.
- **R1, recovery.** A control recovers the loss when, against `ne`, its HOTA and IDF1 5th
  percentiles are above zero and, against `a0`, it is non-inferior (l4clus0010's J1: HOTA and IDF1
  5th percentiles at −0.02 or above, switches 95th at +10 or below). It removes the duplicates when
  its duplicate pairs are at most `a0`'s.
- **R2, location.** Read in this order, the first that applies names the step:
  - `ne-a1` recovers: the A2 face-residual distance is the cause. If it does not, the A2
    distance formula is not the cause (l5id0019's falsifier for its fix 4).
  - `ne-robust` recovers: the ranking among admitted pairings is the cause.
  - `ne-tentative` recovers: young tracks taking clusters from body-centre tracks is the cause.
  - `ne-overlap` removes the duplicates without recovering: the duplicates are cleaned up after
    they form, and the cause is upstream of the lifecycle.
  - none recovers: the cause lies before the cost, in what a body-centre track is compared with,
    and the diagnosis's largest category of births on a live body-centre track names it.
- **R3, the fix.** The fix is a default-off Go option behind a replay experiment, byte-identical
  to `ne` and to `a0` when off. It passes when, against `ne`, its HOTA and IDF1 5th percentiles
  are above zero, and its duplicate pairs are at most `a0`'s. Its non-inferiority against `a0`
  (J1) is reported and is not part of the pass.
- **R4, nothing adopted.** kirk0 is tuning data that l5id0019 has already read. A pass makes the
  fix the near-edge candidate for the placements' label-free read and the held-out window; it
  changes no default.
