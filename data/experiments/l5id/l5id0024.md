# l5id0024: the likelihood birth gate on two placements drawn by lot

**Question.** l5id0023 ranked `likelihood-birth` first of its three forms: it matches the robust
cost's identity on kirk0 and passes K2 on the four segments, but fails K3 at haight-divisadero's
second segment, where the moving count fell from 28 to 26. Its report traced the count move to the
moving test, not to lost vehicles: a track joined from pieces has a different whole-life median
speed and first-to-last distance from its pieces, so the test can cross its threshold either way
when a candidate does what it was built to do. This spec reads the same arm on segments it has not
seen, with a moving test fixed before they are read that a join cannot move.

**Arms.** `a0` and `likelihood-birth` (`likelihood_cost` with `robust_birth_gate`), both on the
defaults.

**Cases.** kirk0 as the anchor. The first two five-minute segments of columbus-north-point and
lombard-broderick, drawn by lot with the seed in the box step's note. No report has read them for
this candidate; they have been read label-free for identity and noise by earlier screens, so this is
a screen, not a held-out read.

**Rule, written before any replay (2026-11-10).**

- **L1, the anchor.** On kirk0 the paired bootstrap's 5th percentiles of the HOTA and IDF1 deltas
  against `a0` are above 0 (l5id0023's K1).
- **L2, noise.** On each of the four segments under l5id0019's R4: brief tracks rise by 3 at most,
  clutter by 5 per hundred of `a0`'s moving tracks at most (moving by the peak test below), and
  duplicate pairs per hundred vehicle tracks do not rise. It passes on three of the four segments
  at least, and on none loses a lasting moving track by footprint.
- **L3, counts, with the peak test.** A track is moving when it lasts 2.0 s or more and its
  maximum speed (1.0 s rolling median) is 1.5 m/s or more. On each segment the moving count moves
  by 5 % at most, after crediting every `a0` moving track that the candidate carries in one track
  together with another `a0` moving track (a join, listed per segment); and p85 moves by
  0.5 m/s at most.
- **Outcome.** A pass on L1 to L3 names `likelihood-birth` for label0003's spans, read once under
  l5id0023's K5 when they exist. A fail names its clause. Nothing is adopted.

**Reported, not decided.** L3 under the old median test beside the peak test, for both arms, so
the change of measure is visible; the joins listed with their rows' times; the births the gate
absorbs; duplicate pair-frames per segment.

**What could mislead.** The peak test was written after l5id0023 saw the median test fail, so it is
a corrected measure chosen with knowledge of one result; it applies to `a0` and the candidate alike
and is read here only on segments the candidate has not seen. Crediting joins trusts the footprint
match, which on a long body can join two vehicles in a queue. Two quiet-to-moderate placements are
a thin screen; one track still moves a clause.
