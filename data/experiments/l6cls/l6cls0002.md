# l6cls0002: a track-level quality gate

**Question.** After the refined defaults of #901, the first three corpus sites still show 12 to
25 % of their lasting tracks near-static, and most are under a metre long and classed `bird` or
`dynamic`. PCL's point-level outlier filters repeat what DBSCAN's neighbour count already does, so
the lever left is at the track. How many of those tracks can a gate remove, across sites and not
just at the busiest, without touching a road user?

**Candidates, fixed before any gate is scored (2026-11-02).** A near-static track has net
displacement under 2 m and p95 speed under 0.5 m/s; a brief track lasts under 1 s. A track's
class is the classifier's, and `quiet` means `bird`, `dynamic` or none.

- `g1`: near-static and quiet.
- `g2`: `g1`, plus brief, quiet and under 2 m net, so that no moved track is read.
- `g3`: `g2`, plus near-static `pedestrian` tracks observed in under half of the frames of their
  life (flicker; a person standing still is seen in most frames).

**Rule, written before any gate is scored (2026-11-02).** Read on the 23 first-segment sites and
kirk0. Sites are the unit: no clause reads a pooled total, because a sum lets the busiest sites
decide.

1. **Target.** Clutter tracks (near-static plus brief) fall by 50 % or more at the median site,
   and fall at 20 or more of the 23.
2. **No one site decides.** The median reduction, recomputed leaving out each site in turn, stays
   at 40 % or more. Each site's share of the removed tracks is reported.
3. **No road user lost.** No track that moved (2 m net or more) is removed at any site, and on
   kirk0 per-frame false negatives and identity switches do not rise, false positives fall, and
   HOTA and IDF1 each stay within 0.001 of `a0`.
4. **Held out.** The candidate that passes, the largest if several do, is read once on the second
   segment of 22 sites (warm-up 370 s): its median reduction must keep at least 80 % of its
   first-segment median, and clutter must fall at 18 or more of the 22.

A pass proposes the gate and changes no default: kirk0 is one labelled site, and no labelled
held-out window exists until label0001. Standing people are the known risk, which is why `g1` and
`g2` never read a `pedestrian` track.

**What it does not establish.** That a removed track was not a real object at the 23 unlabelled
sites; whether the classifier's labels are right; Pi cost; anything outside San Francisco or the
2.3 m mount.
