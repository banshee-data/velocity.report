# l6cls0003: the gate and the overlap end together

**Question.** The overlap end leaves a brief track wherever it ends a duplicate, so it raises
clutter while it removes duplicates. On kirk0 and the first and fourth segments, `g4` of
[l6cls0002](l6cls0002.md) applied to `body-older-tentative` removes that clutter and adds no false
negative. That pairing was chosen after seeing those results, so they cannot pass it. Does it hold
on segments nobody has read for it?

**Rule, written before the second segments are read (2026-11-02).** Sites are the unit. Against
`a0`, on the second capture of the 22 sites that have one:

1. Clutter tracks (near-static plus brief) after the gate fall by 40 % or more at the median site
   and fall at 16 or more of the 22.
2. That median, recomputed leaving out each site in turn, stays at 30 % or more.
3. No moved track (2 m net or more) is removed, and the gate removes no track at or over 1.5 m
   long or wide.
4. Duplicate pairs per hundred vehicle tracks fall at 20 or more of the 22, and observed vehicle
   frames are at least 0.99 of `a0`'s pooled.

The bars are lower than l6cls0002's because they compare the pairing with `a0`, and the overlap
end adds brief tracks that the gate must first remove. A pass names the pairing as the candidate
for label0001's held-out window; it changes no default.

**What it does not establish.** That a removed track at an unlabelled site was not real; any
identity or HOTA figure off kirk0; Pi cost, where the overlap end's replay wall time rose about
40 % on this host.
