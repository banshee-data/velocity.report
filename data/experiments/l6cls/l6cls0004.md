# l6cls0004: what clutter is, and where spurious clusters come from

**Question.** The November gates removed stopped cars 3.8 × 1.7 m and 2.9 × 1.5 m on kirk0,
because "clutter" (brief under 1 s, or near-static under 2 m net with p95 speed under 0.5 m/s)
does not distinguish a parked car from a bird. The overlap end's added brief tracks are counted as
clutter though most may be re-births on a real body. And no report says where a site's spurious
clusters (observations no lasting track took) come from. Before any further gate or filter, what
is clutter made of at each site, and do spurious clusters recur in place, which points at the
background, or beside moving tracks, which points at edge paint and mixed returns, or neither?

**Definitions, fixed before any evidence is read (2026-11-05).** A clutter track is brief or
near-static as `lidar_noise_screen.py` defines them. Footprints are drawn boxes grown by 0.25 m.

- **(a) Artefact:** a clutter track whose rows lie within 1 m of no moving track's footprint at
  the same frames, and whose mean box is under 1.5 m long and under 1.5 m wide.
- **(b) Stationary road user:** a near-static track whose mean box is 1.5 m or more in length or
  width, or classed `pedestrian` and observed in half or more of the frames of its life.
- **(c) Fragment of a mover:** a brief or shortened track at least one of whose rows lies inside a
  moving track's footprint at the same frame, or within 1 m of it.
- **Unclassed:** the remainder, reported as such.
- **Recurrence:** every observation no lasting track took, binned in 0.5 m cells; a cell recurs
  when it holds such observations in 10 or more distinct seconds of the scored window. A
  recurring cell is **beside a path** when within 1.5 m of a moving track's footprint at any
  frame, otherwise **in the background**.

**Rule (2026-11-05).** A measurement; each reading names the layer that owns noise at a site.

1. At each site, the share of clutter tracks in (a), (b), (c) and unclassed, under `a0` and under
   `body-older-tentative`; the median site and the range.
2. If (c) is the largest class at the median site under `body-older-tentative`, the overlap end's
   noise cost is identity work (l5id0014), not a gate's. If (b) is 20 % or more of clutter at the
   median site under `a0`, every later label-free rule reads stationary road users as their own
   column and never as clutter. If (a) is the largest class under `a0`, the next noise lever is at
   L3 or L4, and the recurrence read says which.
3. Spurious observations per site: the share in recurring cells, and of those the share in the
   background against beside a path. Background recurrence over half points at the grid (a learned
   surface that moved, vegetation, an edge); beside-path recurrence over half points at edge paint
   or mixed returns at the body's edge; neither points at fragments and one-offs, which the
   identity work owns.

No default changes; the report proposes which spec runs next and which definitions
`lidar_noise_screen.py` adopts.

**What could mislead.** (a) against (b) is a size prior, not truth: a small stationary object
(a bin, a bollard) reads as an artefact, a motorcycle parked reads as a road user. A fragment of a
mover within 1 m of a footprint can be a cyclist alongside. The evidence holds confirmed tracks
only, so a tentative track's clutter is not here. The first and fourth captures have been read by
the gates and by l5id0006, so nothing here is held out; it is a description.

**What it does not establish.** That any track at an unlabelled site is what its class says;
anything about what a filter would remove, since none is built.
