# l6cls0005: small artefacts refused at birth, or absorbed

**Question.** The November benchmark (l5id0019, on PR #994's branch) found small artefacts the
largest clutter class at every case it read, 54 of 93 clutter tracks pooled and 6 of 15 on kirk0.
D14's recurrence grid found 0 to 2 recurring cells per case holding at most 15 % of unowned
clusters, so most artefacts are one-offs that a background filter cannot reach. Its ranked fix is
to remove them at birth or absorb them. Can L5 refuse to confirm a small, near-static, sparsely
supported tentative track that has not persisted, and halve kirk0's artefacts, without the
l6cls0002 failure: removing stopped cars, or costing reviewed objects frames?

**Arms.** Each a default-off Go option, reachable only by its replay name.

- `a0`: the defaults.
- `birth-gate` (`artefact_birth_gate`, `TrackerConfig.ArtefactBirthGate`). A tentative track that
  has earned `hits_to_confirm` is not confirmed while all four of these hold, and stays tentative,
  so the shipped tentative miss rule (`max_misses`, 3) ends it if it flickers:
  1. **small:** every cluster it has taken is under 1.5 m long and under 1.5 m wide (D13's class
     floor, the smallest car's width);
  2. **near-static:** its latest cluster centroid is under 0.5 m from its first (D13's near-static
     speed, 0.5 m/s, held over the 1 s of clause 4);
  3. **sparse:** its clusters average under 24 returns (three times L4's shipped minimum cluster
     of 8);
  4. **not persistent:** under 1.0 s of capture time since its first observation (D13's brief
     bound).

  Clauses 1 and 4 are the exemptions. A stopped car's cluster is 1.5 m or more on one side, so it
  is never held. A person standing still is seen on most frames, so it persists past 1 s and
  confirms then (D13's class (b) needs a class and half its frames observed, which a track does
  not have at birth; persistence is what a birth rule can read in its place). A walking person
  passes 0.5 m within about 0.4 s. No track is deleted by the gate: it is held tentative, and
  whatever happens to it then is the shipped rule's.

- `absorb` (`artefact_absorb`, `TrackerConfig.ArtefactAbsorb`). An unassociated cluster under
  1.5 m long and wide with under 24 returns, whose centroid lies within 1.25 m of the drawn box of
  a confirmed track matched this frame (D13's footprint margin of 0.25 m and its 1 m), seeds no
  track: it is taken as that body's returns. The track's state is not updated from it. Expected:
  absorb reaches D13's class (c), fragments of a mover, and few artefacts, which D13 defines as
  away from moving tracks; it is run because the ranked fix names it, and a fail on clause 1 is
  the likely outcome.

**Case.** kirk0 (`20251205T1417_80858-75c_kirkham-9th`), whole, 6 s warm-up, the reconstructed
reviewed pack (`ds_67842cb1b22b70e7`) as l5id0019 read it, split drafted with
`-from-seconds 6 -split kirk0-tuning`, `obj_788622b5` (the parked car the reviewers did not label
through) ignored.

**Rule, written before any arm is replayed (2026-11-09).** kirk0 only. Each arm against `a0`.

1. **Target.** Artefact tracks (D13 class (a), `lidar_clutter_origin.py`) fall by at least half:
   from `a0`'s count to half of it or fewer.
2. **False negatives.** The per-frame evaluator's paired bootstrap (2,000 draws of the reviewed
   objects, seed 20261108) does not establish a rise: the 5th percentile of ΔFN (arm less `a0`) is
   not above zero. The full comparison's ΔFN is reported beside it.
3. **No reviewed object loses frames.** For every reviewed object of the split, the arm's matched
   frames (the bootstrap's per-object point: the object alone, with the tracks attributed to it)
   are at least `a0`'s.
4. **Stopped cars and road users kept.** Every `a0` stationary road user (D13 class (b)) has an
   arm track within 1.0 m of it on 90 % or more of its `a0` rows, and `lidar_flip_cost.py`
   reports no moving track lost.
5. **Identity.** l4clus0010's J1 non-inferiority: the paired HOTA and IDF1 5th percentiles at
   −0.02 or above and the identity-switch 95th at +10 or below.

An arm passes when all five hold. A pass proposes the arm for the wider read named below and
changes no default. Reported but deciding nothing: brief and near-static tracks, every clutter
class, spurious clusters a frame, the moving count and its p85, duplicate pairs, and the gate's own
counters (tracks held, held tracks later confirmed, clusters absorbed).

**What could mislead.** kirk0 is a tuning capture, and its `a0` clutter list (the l5id0019 `a0`
evidence, tracking-baseline digest `79d9abff…`) was read before this rule was written: its six
artefacts are 0.6 to 1.4 m long with 8 to 44 returns, and five live under a second. The 24-return
and 0.5 m values were chosen with that list in view, from the physical anchors above, so clause 1
on kirk0 is weaker evidence than a held-out read would be. Six artefacts is a small count: one
track is 17 %. D13's (a) against (b) is a size prior, not truth; a bin or a bollard reads as an
artefact. The evidence holds confirmed tracks only, so what the gate holds and the shipped rule
then ends is counted from the tracker's counters, not seen in the rows.

**Wider read owed.** A pass is read next on the first two five-minute segments of
ashbury-downey and haight-divisadero, as l5id0019 read them, and then on the d2 first-segment
corpus with the second segments held out. Neither is read here.

**What it does not establish.** That a held or absorbed track was not a real small object; that
the values hold at another mount height, frame rate or site; Pi cost.

**Answer (November 9, 2026).** Both arms fail. `birth-gate` fails clauses 1 to 4: artefacts go
from 6 to 5, the paired ΔFN is +1 / +9 / +23, two reviewed pedestrians and a car lose frames, and
the standing pedestrian keeps 86.8 % of its rows. `absorb` fails clause 1 alone: artefacts go from
6 to 8, while fragments fall from 3 to 1 and false negatives from 265 to 261. No stopped car is
touched in either arm. See the
[report](../../../docs/lidar/operations/artefact-birth-gate-kirk0-2026-11.md).
