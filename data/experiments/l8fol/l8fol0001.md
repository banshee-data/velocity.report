# l8fol0001: both parties observed (B1)

**Question.** The behaviour plan's B1 asks how often both parties of an interaction are observed
at a real roadside site, and calls it the question nothing else in the plan matters as much as.
It has never been measured. Across the 23 corpus sites' first segments, how many pair-seconds
of vehicle following does the tracker see in five minutes, at which sites, and how much of that
is one vehicle held by two tracks?

**Method.** Label-free. Each case is replayed under the defaults and under l5id0004's one track
per vehicle (`body-older`), and the segment finders read each evidence database: the `following`
finder's pair-seconds, pairs, followers and leaders, `leader_changes`, and the closest gap, in
every 10 s window and summed per case. The difference between the arms is the duplicates' share:
a pair a coasting track makes with the live track that took its vehicle reads as a follower a
metre behind, and the overlap end removes exactly those.

**Reading, written before the replays (2026-10-18).** A measurement, not a candidate: no default
changes and no rule passes or fails. What is read, per site and pooled:

1. pair-seconds per vehicle-track-minute under each arm, and the share `body-older` removes;
2. the distribution of the closest gap per pair under `body-older`, with pairs under 1 m listed
   apart (a split vehicle, not a follower, until the labels say otherwise);
3. followers and leader changes per minute, which are what the following gate's strata and the
   R5 opportunity accounting will have to hold;
4. the ranking of sites by pair-seconds under `body-older`, which chooses where following
   references are worth the operator's hours, with the lot-drawn held-out site read last and
   reported apart.

A site with no pair in five minutes is a finding, not a failure of the screen.

**Definitions fixed before the replays (2026-10-20).** The 23 sites are l3bg0023's: every corpus
site but embarcadero-folsom, which no screen has read and which stays unread here so that
label0001 can still draw its window there by lot. Item 4's "lot-drawn held-out site" is therefore
not read by this experiment, and the ranking covers the 23. Each case replays 200 s after a 70 s
warm-up, as l3bg0023 did. A vehicle-track-minute is a minute of life of a vehicle-sized track:
one that lasts 2 s or more and whose associated clusters' longer box side has a p90 of 3.0 m or
more (l3bg0023's screen). The standard `following` selector counts gaps of 3 m to 40 m along the
follower's heading, so item 2's pairs under 1 m cannot appear in it: the screen reads each case a
second time with the minimum gap at 0 m, every other parameter the selector's own, and lists the
pairs with a closest gap under 1 m and under 3 m from that pass apart from the standard counts.

**What it does not establish.** A rate of real following: the finder reads tracks, so
fragmentation adds pairs and occlusion hides them. The tracker's count is an upper bound on pairs
and the encounters with both parties observed a lower bound; the labelled window (label0002) is
where the two meet. The second segments are not read, so they stay held out for this measure.
