# l8fol0003: the published box as the physical endpoint source

**Question.** On kirk0 the solid body fits 7 of 60 follower paths, because a body-centre
reference exists only after a near-edge fix, and so the field run finds 3 encounters with no
valid time. The published box is now the default: it holds every return of every vehicle frame,
its axis is 1.6° from the direction of travel at the median, and since migration 000062 it is
recorded on every observation. Read as the body, does it place a front and a rear the gap can
use? On kirk0's 11 reference gaps, is its gap within the reference bound as often as the
near-edge arm's, and how many follower paths and encounters does the field run then fit on kirk0
and on the corpus's first segments?

**Method.** A reader, not a tracker change: `TrajectoriesFromBoxes` beside the two existing
trajectory sources, off the production path, with the box's endpoints always `temporally
inferred` because the box records no face. The physical scorer then scores the box's gaps beside
the near-edge arm's on the frozen tuning split, and the field run counts paths and encounters per
corpus case under the box, the box with one track per vehicle, and the near-edge shadow.

**Rule, written before the replays (2026-10-18).** The box source is worth carrying into the
field run (still labelled provisional, no default changed) when:

1. on kirk0's reference gaps it scores at least as many gaps as the near-edge arm (7 of 11), and
   its gap error is within the reference bound on at least as many of them;
2. on the corpus, follower paths fitted rise to at least half of the confirmed vehicle tracks at
   the median site, from the solid body's 7 of 60 on kirk0.

The truck gaps are read apart: the box draws kirk0's trucks at 0.62 of their reviewed length
until the identity option and the W3 fix, so a truck's rear will be short by the same amount
and the report says so rather than averaging it in. If the box passes clause 1 and fails clause
2, the paths are the constraint and l8fol0001's finder figures say which sites.

**Definitions fixed before the replays (2026-10-20).** Found while building the reader, recorded
before any scored replay; the rule's thresholds are unchanged. (1) A replay's evidence database
held no box before this change (the harness disables track persistence); the harness now writes
each online estimate's `lidar_tracks` and `lidar_track_observations` rows, box included, so every
arm is replayed with this branch's harness. (2) The baseline is restated at this commit: on kirk0
the solid body fits 12 of 105 follower paths (the 7 of 60 was an older build), and the box 23 of
105 in the implementation's smoke replay, which is not the scored replay. (3) Clause 2's
"confirmed vehicle tracks" has no label-free count; it is read as l8fol0001's vehicle-sized
tracks (lasting 2 s or more, p90 of the associated clusters' longer box side 3.0 m or more), and
a track's path counts as fitted when the field run's `data.json` lists it as a follower with no
path condition. The clause is the median over the 23 sites of fitted vehicle-sized tracks over
vehicle-sized tracks, at least 0.5, for the `box` arm; `box-body-older` and `near-edge` are read
beside it. (4) The 23 sites are l3bg0023's and l8fol0001's: embarcadero-folsom stays unread for
label0001. Each case replays 200 s after a 70 s warm-up. (5) Clause 1's comparator is the near-edge arm replayed here at this commit, and the pilot's 7 of
11 (an older build) is kept as a floor: the box passes clause 1 only when it scores at least as
many gaps as both, and is within the bound on at least as many as the near-edge arm here and as
the pilot's three. The implementation's smoke replays scored the box at 10 of 11 and a near-edge
replay at 4; those are not the scored replays and are not read for the rule. (6) The implementation smoke found
`class_not_supported` at every evaluated instant on kirk0, so valid following time is expected
to be zero under every arm; the rule does not read valid time, and the report says so.

**What it does not establish.** Physical accuracy beyond kirk0's three vehicles, or anything held
out: the split is tuning. A gap from an unseen end is the box's held extent, and the strata say
`temporally_inferred` for every box endpoint. The metric stays provisional whatever the result;
promotion is l8fol0005's.
