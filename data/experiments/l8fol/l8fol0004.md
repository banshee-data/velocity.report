# l8fol0004: the following limits, predeclared

**Question.** Physical scoring refuses every held-out split until the following limits are pinned
on tuning data and the scorer cites the record, and the held-out gap scorer still keys encounters
to track ids, cannot check the leader and tests every bound on a point estimate. What limits do
kirk0's references support, and with the scorer reading the reviewed following records, what
does the tuning split score under the box and the near-edge arm?

**Method.** Three manual steps and one machine step. More pairs first: the pilot's two pairs
are two of the slow trucks and the one car between them, and the plan says add pairs before
reading rates. Then the scorer gains what R6 asks and the W0 record has for geometry. Then the
limits are drafted from the tuning scores and the references' own precision, and pinned by a
decision before any held-out window is read (l8fol0005).

**Rule, written before the replays (2026-10-18).** The limits are chosen against the display and
report resolution and the references' precision, never against a held-out result, and never
loosened after one. Where a reference's half-width is wider than the resolution a limit needs
(kirk0's drafted gaps carry 1.5 to 2.0 m), the record says so: the limit stands as
`insufficient reference precision` until keyframed references (label0002, W7) narrow it, and no
gap scored against such a reference counts toward a pass. Leader choice is scored as right,
wrong, or refused where the record says `no_leader` or `ambiguous`; a wrong leader with an
accurate gap is a wrong leader. Strata are counted in bodies and body pairs, and a stratum's
verdict is its bootstrap interval's, with the multiple-comparison correction stated.

**What it does not establish.** Any held-out figure; the limits' adequacy beyond one site's
slow traffic; the along-path gap, which waits for persisted paths (R5) and is scored along the
reference follower's axis until then.
