# l8fol0005: the first held-out following score

**Question.** With the following limits pinned (l8fol0004) and a window nobody tuned on
labelled, fitted and frozen with its following records (label0001, label0002), what do the box,
the box with one track per vehicle, and the near-edge arm score on it: endpoint and gap error per
stratum, leader choice, coverage, missing and suppressed opportunity?

**Method.** One replay per arm of the held-out site, one physical score each against the frozen
held-out split, verdicts by the pinned record. The window is read once for this measure; a
second read is a new window.

**Rule, written before the replays (2026-10-18).** Pass, fail or insufficient per stratum and
pooled, as the record defines them; nothing is retuned on the result, and a fail is reported as
one. A pass promotes nothing by itself: the metric gate also needs G-GEO-1, G-UNC-1 and G-SMO-1,
and this score is the metric gate's evidence, read beside them. If no arm passes, the report
names the stratum and the reason (reference precision, endpoint source, leader choice, class), and
the next spec follows that reason.

**What it does not establish.** A second site; a rate over more than one window's encounters;
anything about a promoted field report.
