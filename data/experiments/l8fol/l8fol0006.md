# l8fol0006: current-baseline evidence reproducibility

The [weekend plan PR](https://github.com/banshee-data/velocity.report/pull/1022) provides context.
The owner authorised an eight-hour maximum engineering campaign. Its first question is whether
current defaults produce portable per-site following and experimental DRAC records whose
provenance, exclusion accounting and regeneration are reproducible. It is diagnostic, with no
physical measurement acceptance or model adoption.

## Frozen rule

Before replay, pin the code/build, tuning file and fingerprint, ordered capture hashes,
cohort manifest, existing protection registry and evidence/output paths. Do not use validation
or qualification cohorts for development diagnostics. Do not retune or modify algorithms.

The engineering slice passes only if the selected source joins explicitly to its permitted
site, stage and estimator version; the same completed evidence DB exports twice byte-for-byte;
the readable follower intervals reconcile exactly with mutually exclusive accounting buckets;
missing or invalid evidence produces explicit suppression rather than an observed value; and
all source/configuration/build identities remain explainable. All-suppressed physical values
are acceptable engineering evidence if the suppression and accounting are correct.

Raw-replay reproducibility is a separate result. The baseline tool repeats pipeline processing;
compare its declared analytical summary and digest checks, recording volatile runtime/run/track
identifiers separately. Do not claim byte-identical independently written DBs or silently invent
an identifier correspondence. A changed identifier or analytical field is reported and explained;
unresolved correspondence makes the separate raw-repeat claim insufficient rather than being
normalised away after inspecting the result.

## Bounded expansion and stop rules

The pilot scores 30 seconds after a 20-second warm-up. If it passes, a further Kirk window may
score at most the remaining capture duration. Up to two additional development-only first-segment
screens, Columbus at Broadway and Embarcadero at Bryant, may run after source hash/cohort preflight:
70-second warm-up and 30-second score each, identical baseline tuning. Freeze their exact source
selection before execution in an additive spec amendment; do not consume a new cohort or arm.

Stop starting runs by 15:23 UTC on November 14, 2026, reserving the final hour to the 16:23 UTC
deadline for reports and PR checks. Stop earlier if evidence answers the question. Internal scratch
has a 10 GB floor; pause owned replay processes on storage pressure rather than killing them.
Archive completed evidence and verify hashes before removing any local copy. A failed access,
eligibility, settling or reproducibility gate stops expansion and is reported as a blocker.

## Evidence limits

Report per-case candidate and exclusion yields, exact accounting, supported/suppressed outcomes,
cost and provenance. These are observed software outputs, not true vehicle identity, bumper
geometry, relative-speed accuracy, crash risk or calibrated DRAC. No reference score or held-out
promotion is claimed. Labels/physical references may be unavailable even when protection-registry
inputs are readable. Any follow-up geometry-attribution or tracker intervention needs its own rule.

## Observed result

The Kirk engineering slice passed: both same-DB exports were byte-identical, all readable
follower-time reconciled, source/site/policy joins were explicit and invalid evidence remained
suppressed. The tool separately reported repeat-equal tracking summaries. See the authored
report for the exact retained-versus-scoring populations, counts and limitations. Optional S2
expansion is frozen separately as l8fol0007; no overlapping Kirk expansion was executed.
