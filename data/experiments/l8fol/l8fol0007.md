# l8fol0007: development-site fixed-prefix evidence

Extend the successful bounded Kirk engineering pilot to two already permitted development sites.
This specification freezes the source selection before either site's replay or diagnostic read.
It shares the unchanged baseline arm with l8fol0006 but is a separate operational experiment,
report and source cohort. It is not a tuning sweep or a held-out acceptance read.

## Source selection and rule

Read only the canonical first capture for Columbus at Broadway and Embarcadero at Bryant,
as listed in the checked-in two-case corpus and first-segment source index. Cohort revision 2
assigns both to development, and their raw SHA-256 preflight matches the assignments. No
validation or qualification source substitutes for an unavailable development capture.

For each source, replay a fixed prefix: 70 seconds of warm-up and up to 230 seconds of scoring,
subject to available packet duration, with settled background required. The baseline tool's
ordinary raw replay repeat is checked under its existing frozen comparator. Retain the first
run's DB. The full retained DB can include warm-up evidence: report its actual timestamps,
frame counts and interval accounting separately from the 230-second scoring summary. Do not
trim or relabel retained estimates as a scoring-only population.

The engineering slice passes if two exports from that same completed source DB are byte-identical,
the source/site/version/policy joins are explicit and valid, readable follower-time reconciles
exactly across mutually exclusive accounting buckets, and unsupported/predicted/record-gap
evidence does not become an observed DRAC value. Raw-repeat equality of the baseline summary
is a separate result; no independently written DB byte-equality or physical accuracy is claimed.

Stop expansion on any access, eligibility, settling, accounting or repeatability failure.
No algorithm or parameter changes follow observed yield during this experiment. Do not choose
a different window because a source is sparse or suppressed. The common campaign deadline is
16:23 UTC on November 14, 2026; start no new replay after 15:23 UTC. A storage watchdog pauses
only owned replay processes below the internal 10 GB or capture-volume 20 GB floor. Run one
replay lane, archive completed cases with hash verification and retain local evidence until
verified. Stop early when the fixed cases answer the question.

## Interpretation

Report per-site timing, retained-source/sample counts, follower-time accounting, DRAC outcomes,
dominant evidence exclusions, provenance and missing physical references. Intervals from multiple
followers overlap in scene time; pair records are not independent physical encounters. Missing
joint uncertainty remains missing. Positive engineering checks adopt no threshold, tracker,
classifier or claim about true vehicle geometry, relative speed or collision risk.
