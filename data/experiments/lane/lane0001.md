# lane0001: lane segments at kirk

**Question.** The stitching plan constrains vehicles to drawn lane segments. Before a linker
depends on it: does a map drawn by hand from the point cloud hold the reviewed vehicles, does it
assign each to one segment without flicker, and how long does a placement take to draw?

**Method.** Draw one map for the kirk placement without looking at tracks. Project every observed
sample of each reviewed vehicle object (truth, not tracker output, so map error is not mixed with
tracking error) and of each confirmed vehicle track.

**Rule, written before the drawing (2026-10-21).** The map is fit for l5id0008 when, over the
reviewed vehicle objects' observed samples:

1. at least 95% lie in exactly one segment's corridor and at most 2% in none;
2. the median absolute heading error to the segment tangent is under 10°;
3. at least 95% of reviewed objects that stay on one road change segment only through a declared
   successor or transition, with the hysteresis on;
4. the drawing takes under 45 minutes.

A failing clause is reported with the samples behind it. Clauses 1–3 failing means the map
format or projection needs work before l5id0008; clause 4 failing alone means maps fitted from
trajectories move up the plan, and l5id0008 still runs.

**What could mislead.** kirk's geometry may be simpler than a merge site; l5id0009 draws a second
placement with a declared merge. Turning vehicles at the edges of coverage may sit outside every
corridor by design; the report counts them separately.

**What it does not establish.** Whether lanes help linking; that is l5id0008.
