# lane0002: Gerardus's proposals against drawn lanes

**Question.** Gerardus grades lane proposals `high` or `medium` from the summed lane fields
(LANE-PROPOSALS §4). Before an operator is told to approve `high` ones with little checking, and
before Phase E of the lanes-from-tracks plan: do `high` proposals sit on the lanes a person draws
without seeing them, and is the lane count per direction right?

**Arm.** The defaults, `config/tuning.defaults.json`: the experiment scores Gerardus's proposals,
not a tuning change, and a parameter set under `config/experiments/` must change a key, so the arm
names the defaults file, as lane0001's does. Its fingerprint goes in the report's provenance.

**Method.** At kirk and columbus-broadway, the owner draws every travel lane in the painter with
the proposals and the traffic layer hidden, from the reference, the kerbs and the background only,
and saves them under the author name `lane0002`. Lanes approved from a proposal are never part of
the reference. The scoring tool pairs each `high` proposal with the
drawn lane of the same bound whose centreline is nearest over the proposal's length, and measures
the lateral offset at every metre of the proposal's overlap with that lane.

**Rule, written before the drawing (2026-11-04).** Gerardus's `high` grade is fit to approve with
a glance at a site when, at each site separately:

1. every `high` proposal's lateral RMSE against its paired drawn lane is under 0.30 m (SPEC-V1
   §6's acceptance against an independent reference);
2. per direction (street and bound), the number of `high` and `medium` proposals equals the number
   of drawn lanes;
3. at least 80 % of drawn lanes are found by a `high` proposal (paired within 0.30 m RMSE over at
   least 70 % of the drawn lane's length);
4. no `high` proposal pairs with no drawn lane (a false lane).

Clauses 1, 2 and 4 failing at either site mean the grade's thresholds move (handed to Gerardus's
LANE-PROPOSALS §4.7 in the report); clause 3 failing alone means `high` is conservative and
`medium` proposals carry lanes an operator must look at.

**Measures the tool prints.** Per site and per direction: drawn lanes, `high` and `medium`
proposals, pairs, per pair the lateral RMSE, mean signed offset (positive left of travel) and
overlap fraction; per site the share of drawn lanes found and the false `high` count.

**What could mislead.** The owner's drawing is a reading of the street, not a survey; where paint
is faint the drawn lane can itself be off by a few decimetres, which counts against the proposal.
Two sites, both with heavy traffic; a quiet site may produce fewer proposals. Gerardus's grade
thresholds were set before this run, so this measures them, it does not fit them.

**What it does not establish.** Whether attribution by lane is right for speed reports (that needs
labelled tracks); whether proposals generalise to the 22 sites without an accepted pose.
