# perf0001: the Pi's frame times under the defaults' L3

**Question.** l3bg0024 made the substituted replay L3 (closeness 2.0, safety margin 0.3 m,
neighbour confirmation 5) the defaults, which every deployment runs, since the defaults are built
into the binary. On kirk0 it doubles the clusters a frame against the L3 before it, and the full
profile's p95 frame rises from 24.7 to 35.2 ms on the Mac and from 32.3 to 46.1 ms on a CI runner,
of the 98 ms budget. Does a Pi 5 keep the new defaults inside the budget, and if not, does the L3
before them, or CMR, which has a third of their spurious clusters?

Only a worker advertising `pi` can claim it; the Mac's timings are not evidence for the target.

**Rule, written before the runs (2026-10-19).**

1. **Within budget:** an arm whose share of frames over the 98 ms budget is at most 1 % (the perf
   harness's `-max-frames-over-budget-pct`) in the median of its five rounds, with no round
   throttled.
2. **If `defaults` is within budget,** the defaults stand and the Pi's baseline is captured from
   them.
3. **If it is not,** the L3 the Pi can afford becomes the defaults everyone runs, not a Pi-only
   profile, which would part live input from replay again. `cmr` is the first candidate if it is
   within budget, and is scored beside `defaults` on the labels before it is adopted (l3bg0024
   found it 0.009 below on kirk0 HOTA and 0.063 below on IDF1). If neither is, and
   `pre-consolidation` is, the cost is the L3's and the next step is an L3 between the two. If
   none is, the full profile does not fit the Pi at kirk0's load under any L3 measured, and the
   question becomes the pipeline's.

**What could mislead.** kirk0 is 832 frames from one busy site; a quieter site has more headroom.
The tail is noisy: identical code over the same capture on one machine has given worst frames from
86 to 329 ms, which is why the rule reads the share over budget, not the worst frame. A Pi without
its deployed case and cooling throttles sooner than a deployed one, and a harness replay has no
radar ingest or HTTP load beside it.
