# perf0003: pacing does not change a replay, asked again

**Question.** perf0002 asked whether analysis mode records what a paced replay records and loses
nothing, and could not answer it: its arms ran several replays per server, and the server carried
L3 state from one replay to the next (l3bg0020, l3bg0021). On the eight replays it ran on new
processes, analysis mode, 0.25x and 2x were identical row for row. Two things have changed since:
l3bg0021 makes every replay start from nothing, and l3bg0023 decides what L3 the defaults carry
now that every replay runs its tuning file's L3 (the paced substitution is gone). The question is
asked as perf0002 meant to ask it, on one server without restarts, under the L3 the scenes will be
republished with, so that the pacing result holds for the configuration that ships.

**Arms.** Analysis mode, 0.25x, 0.5x and 2x, every one under `tuning.defaults.json`, named on
each start. 2x is the fastest paced ratio that kept its pace on two of perf0002's three sites;
4x fell behind on all three and is not an arm, since a replay behind its pace is not a test of
pacing.

**Rule, written before the replays (2026-10-18).** Analysis mode is adopted for scene publishing
(`SCENE_SPEED_MODE=analysis` in the Makefile and `publish-scenes.py`) when, on every case:

1. VRLOG frames equal the capture's rotations, with no frame throttled or dropped, in both
   analysis repeats;
2. the two analysis repeats are identical row for row;
3. analysis is identical row for row to x0.25, to x0.5 and to x2 over the frames they share,
   for every paced replay that kept its pace (perf0002's 2 % was written for an effect that
   turned out not to exist); a paced replay that fell behind is reported with its lag and its
   differing frames, and does not decide;
4. analysis takes at most a quarter of x0.5's wall time.

If clause 3 fails on a paced replay that kept its pace, pacing changes what is recorded after all,
and the report says from which frame; 0.5x then stays. Republishing the existing scenes under the
new speed and the consolidated L3 is a separate, outward-facing decision: this experiment changes
the default for the next batch and says what a republish would change.
