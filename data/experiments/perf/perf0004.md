# perf0004: the near-edge arm's frame times on the Pi

**Question.** The maths decision review (E-04) made the near-edge cost screen a frame-level
screen and made a Pi frame measurement a condition of any promotion. On the Mac, A2 moves the
frame p99 by 2 to 7 percent at the three timing sites, every frame p99 under 58 ms of the 98 ms
budget. Does a Pi 4 keep A2 inside the screen at the same sites?

**Rule, written before the runs (2026-10-22).** At each site, in the median of four runs:

1. A2's frame p99 is at most 1.5 times B0's, and under the 98 ms budget.
2. A2's share of frames over the budget is at most 1 % (the perf harness's
   `-max-frames-over-budget-pct`), with no run throttled.

A2 passes the cost screen only if both hold at all three sites. Tracker.Update's p99 ratio is
reported beside the frame figures as a regression signal for the tracker and gates nothing.
Passing the cost screen does not promote A2: its geometry breaches and physical validation are
separate gates.

**Depends on perf0001,** whose B0 timing on the same Pi says whether the defaults themselves fit
the budget; if they do not, this spec's ratio is read against a baseline that already fails.

**What it does not establish.** Live-sensor cost, where a frame over budget is dropped rather
than delayed; cost on any other site; or anything about A2's geometry.
