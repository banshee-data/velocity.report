# pose0001: register the 24 archive sites

**Question.** MVP 0 step 5 asks for a registration record for every one of the 24 archive sites.
Only columbus-broadway has one, from the R1 spike. How many of the 24 register from their
eye-read position with the defaults, which gates stop the rest, and which of the search settings
nobody has varied (heading hint, seeds, correspondence distance, voxel size, range, search radius,
DataSF box walls as reference) move a site across a gate without moving an accepted pose? And does
the L3 track guard, which keeps moving vehicles out of the background, change the registration?

**Arms.** Two backgrounds per site, each the first 300 s of the capture replayed with the full
pipeline: `defaults` (the track guard on, as shipped in #854) and `guard-off`
([track-guard-off.json](../../../config/experiments/track-guard-off.json)). Thirteen registration
arms on them, all from the eye-read position with the site's L16 token given:

| Arm                                         | On                                                        | What changes                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `base`                                      | defaults                                                  | nothing: 20 seeds, ±5 m, ±30°, 1.0 m correspondence, 0.15 m voxels, 60 m range, 30 m search                                                                                                                                             |
| `heading`                                   | defaults                                                  | the hint carries the heading 180° − `north_azimuth_deg`; the coarse search covers every heading and reads the hint's heading only for the summary, so this arm is expected to repeat `base` exactly and serves as the determinism check |
| `seeds40`                                   | defaults                                                  | 40 seeds                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| `rseed2`                                    | defaults                                                  | random seed 2: run-to-run repeatability                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| `corr05`                                    | defaults                                                  | 0.5 m correspondence distance                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| `vox10`                                     | defaults                                                  | 0.10 m scan and reference voxels                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| `range80`                                   | defaults                                                  | scan points used out to 80 m                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| `radius15`                                  | defaults                                                  | coarse search 15 m around the start                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| `boxes`                                     | defaults                                                  | DataSF box walls, corrected by any measured wall planes, as extra reference                                                                                                                                                             |
| `perturb-e15`, `perturb-n15`, `perturb-e25` | defaults                                                  | the hint moved 15 m east, 15 m north, 25 m east: the basin of attraction                                                                                                                                                                |
| `base-guardoff`                             | guard-off                                                 | `base` on the backgrounds without the track guard                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| `r1bg` (added during the run)               | the 83 s backgrounds of the 2026-10-14 replays, two sites | `base` on R1's backgrounds, to separate the background's length from the search                                                                                                                                                         |

**Rule, written before any run (2026-10-24, 20:10 PDT).**

1. The outcome is the table of 24 states under `base` (CANDIDATE, FAILED, with the failing gates),
   which becomes REGISTRATION-STATUS §1's outcomes table whatever it says. A CANDIDATE here is a
   pose every gate passed; it becomes `registered` only when an operator accepts it in the pane.
2. An arm _helps_ when it makes a CANDIDATE of a site `base` failed, and at every site `base` made
   a CANDIDATE its best pose stays within 0.5 m and 1° of `base`'s and its ground p95 does not rise
   by more than 0.02 m. An arm that moves an accepted pose by more than that is not adopted
   whatever else it gains.
3. `rseed2` must land within 0.10 m and 0.5° of `base` at every site, the seed-agreement
   tolerance; a site outside it is reported as unrepeatable and its state is not trusted.
4. A perturbed start _converges_ when its best pose is within 0.20 m and 0.5° of `base`'s best.
   The share that converges, by displacement, is the basin; a site that converges from 25 m but
   not 15 m is noted, since that says the coarse search found a different peak.
5. `base-guardoff` against `base`: a pose difference over 0.10 m or 0.5° at a site, or a gate
   that changes state, is attributed to the guard; the report says which way.
6. Nothing is adopted as a Gerardus default from one night. A setting that helps under rule 2 at
   two or more sites and harms none becomes a proposed default in Gerardus, to be confirmed on the
   Kirkham control site with its reviewed poses before it changes `Params::new`.

**Cases.** The 23 placements of the corpus case set plus embarcadero-folsom, the declared held-out
site. Registration tunes nothing in the pipeline the hold-out protects, and the site was already an
R1 spike site, so it is registered with the others; the spec's case count is the corpus's 23.

**What this does not establish.** No pose here is checked against a surveyed position: the gates
are internal consistency against the aerial survey. Five minutes of background, not the whole
capture, so the point counts (about 70 k) match R1's full-run exports but the far, rarely seen
cells may be thinner. The 2026-09 eye-read positions are the only start.

**Result (2026-10-25).** CANDIDATE at 6 of 24; separation fails at the other 18, alone at nine, with
the hint's heading at 13 of them. No arm helped under rule 2: heading, seeds and random seed
reproduced the defaults exactly, and every other setting lost one to three passing sites or moved a
passing pose (`boxes`, 0.46 m). The track guard gained four candidates and lost two; the 83 s R1
backgrounds registered where the 300 s ones failed at embarcadero-folsom. The one Gerardus change
argued for is a definition of a distinct hypothesis outside the seed basin. Report:
[gerardus-static-registration-2026-10.md](../../../docs/lidar/operations/gerardus-static-registration-2026-10.md).
