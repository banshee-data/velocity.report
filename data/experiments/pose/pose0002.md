# pose0002: the background's length

**Question.** In [pose0001](pose0001.md), five sites registered on a wrong peak, 40° to 173° from
the hint's heading: fulton-divisadero, broadway-gough, laguna-eddy, hyde-ofarrell and
embarcadero-folsom. Each background was the first 300 s of its capture. At embarcadero-folsom,
R1's background, from an 83 s replay on 2026-10-14, registers as a CANDIDATE with the same code,
while every 300 s background of that capture fails. Is the window's length the cause? If a shorter
window registers the wrong-peak sites, does it cost the sites that already pass?

**Cases.** The five wrong-peak sites, and as controls five of the six pose0001 CANDIDATEs:
ashbury-downey, lombard-laguna, columbus-broadway, van-ness-sacramento and franklin-mcallister.
embarcadero-bryant, the sixth, has no facade gate (too few held-out wall points) and is left out,
so every control is scored on all six gates. bush-powell (36° off, seeds 0.00) is not one of the
five and is not run.

**Arms.** Backgrounds of the first 60, 90, 180 and 300 s of each capture, with the defaults
(the L3 track guard on). `w300` is pose0001's `base` background and result, read again rather
than replayed. Registration is pose0001's `base`: the defaults from the eye-read position, the
site's L16 token given, no heading.

**Rule, written before any replay (2026-10-25, 13:20 PDT).**

1. _Recovered._ A wrong-peak site is recovered at a window when that window's registration is
   CANDIDATE. A run that is not CANDIDATE but whose best pose carries the hint's heading within
   6.4° (pose0001's widest at a passing site) with seed agreement at least 0.8 is reported as
   _right-headed_, and is not counted as recovered.
2. _Cost._ A window costs a control when the control is no longer CANDIDATE. It also costs a
   control when the best pose moves more than 0.5 m or 1° from the `w300` pose, or the ground
   p95 rises by more than 0.02 m. These are pose0001 rule 2's limits.
3. _A window is better than 300 s_ when it recovers at least two of the five wrong-peak sites and
   costs none of the five controls. With more than one window better, the shortest of them is
   chosen: it is the cheapest to replay, and the review can rerun all 24 sites at it.
4. _What a better window changes._ It becomes the window for registration backgrounds, a step in
   the export procedure and not a velocity.report or Gerardus default. A follow-up reruns all 24
   sites at that window, under pose0001's `base` arm, before REGISTRATION-STATUS changes.
5. _No window better._ Then the window is not the cause at these sites, and the embarcadero-folsom
   difference belongs to the code between 2026-10-14 and 2026-10-24. That difference includes
   the track guard, but pose0001's `base-guardoff` already argues against the guard. The report
   says which.
6. _Mechanism, reported, not scored._ For each site and window: the exported point count, and the
   best hypothesis's position and heading. A pose that changes between windows is read against
   how the settled cells grew; nothing is inferred from one site.

**What this does not establish.** No pose is checked against the world. Ten sites at four windows
on one night. A window counts from the start of the capture only. A later window, or a window
chosen for low traffic, is a different question.

**Result (2026-10-25).** No window is better than 300 s. 60 s recovers fulton-divisadero and
laguna-eddy but costs lombard-laguna and van-ness-sacramento on ground p95. 90 s recovers none, and
180 s recovers laguna-eddy and costs columbus-broadway. No window is adopted. Rule 5's inference
(no better window, so the window is not the cause) does not hold. At embarcadero-folsom every short
window finds R1's pose, failing only facade p75, and 300 s does not. At the other four sites, outcomes
alternate between peaks from window to window, with point counts within 0.3 %. The proposed
follow-up is [pose0003](pose0003.md). Report:
[gerardus-background-window-2026-10.md](../../../docs/lidar/operations/gerardus-background-window-2026-10.md).
