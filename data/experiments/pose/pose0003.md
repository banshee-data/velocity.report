# pose0003: registration by agreement across windows

**Question.** [pose0002](pose0002.md) found that, at the wrong-peak sites, which coarse peak wins
depends on the background's window. It also found that ground p95 at a fixed pose moves 0.05 to
0.08 m from window to window at two passing sites. One background is one draw. Is the pose that
most windows agree on a better answer than any single window's pose? And does accepting a site
only when the windows agree keep every pose that pose0001 passed?

**Cases.** All 24 archive sites: the corpus case set's 23 and embarcadero-folsom. Four windows
each: 60, 90, 180 and 300 s.

**Rule (fixed at approval, 2026-11-03, before any replay is read).**

1. _Agreement._ A site's windows agree when at least three of the four best poses lie within
   0.10 m and 0.5° of one another, the seed-agreement tolerance. Their median is the site's
   _consensus pose_.
2. _Registered by agreement._ A site with a consensus pose is registered by agreement when, at
   that pose, at least three of the agreeing windows pass every gate, separation included.
3. _The procedure is adopted_ for MVP step 5 when all three of these hold:
   - Every pose0001 CANDIDATE is registered by agreement, within 0.10 m and 0.5° of its pose0001
     pose.
   - No site is registered by agreement whose consensus heading is more than 6.4° from the hint's.
   - At least two of pose0002's five wrong-peak sites are registered by agreement.
4. _Not adopted._ If any of those fails, single-window registration stays, and the report says
   which clause failed and at which sites.

**The rule's reading, fixed at approval.**

- _Best pose._ A run's best hypothesis (rank 1): its site-frame x and y and its heading of +Y.
  Distances are horizontal; headings differ by the smaller angle.
- _Agreement._ The largest set of three or four windows whose poses are pairwise within 0.10 m
  and 0.5°. If two three-window sets qualify, the one with the smaller largest pairwise distance.
  The consensus pose is the median of x, of y and of the heading over that set.
- _Gates at the consensus pose._ Gerardus's CLI evaluates gates only at a run's own best pose,
  so clause 2 reads each agreeing window's gates at its own best pose, which lies within 0.10 m
  and 0.5° of every other agreeing pose. A window "passes every gate" when its run is CANDIDATE.
- _Hint heading._ 180° − `north_azimuth_deg` from `tools/s2-archive/site-index.json`, as
  pose0001 and pose0002 read it, from the eye-read position in that file today (Broadway's
  corrected to Franklin by #891).
- _Wrong-peak sites._ pose0002's five: fulton-divisadero, broadway-franklin, laguna-eddy,
  hyde-ofarrell, embarcadero-folsom. pose0001's CANDIDATEs: ashbury-downey, lombard-laguna,
  columbus-broadway, van-ness-sacramento, franklin-mcallister, embarcadero-bryant, compared with
  pose0001's `base` poses.
- _Backgrounds._ All four windows of every site are made in this run with today's defaults, on a
  scratch server in analysis mode from the whole placement; pose0001's 300 s exports are not
  reused, because the defaults' L3 has changed since.
- _Registration._ The installed `gerardus` with its defaults, from the eye-read position without
  a heading, the L16 cell from `REGISTRATION-STATUS`, on a copy of the server's data directory.
  No pose is accepted and nothing is written to the shared server: acceptance is the owner's.

**What this does not establish.** No pose is checked against the world. Agreement across windows
of one capture is not independence: the windows overlap and share the sensor's position. The
windows here all count from the start of the capture.
