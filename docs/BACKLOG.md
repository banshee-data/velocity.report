# Backlog

Single source of truth for project-wide work items in velocity.report.
Tasks use priority after the optional PR and workstream last: optional `[#PR]`, **P0|P1|P2**, optional `(#issue)`, description, `[design](plans/lidar-state-estimation-plan.md)`, size `M`, optional annotation, workstream `S-GEO`. <!-- link-ignore -->
tasks without a design doc fill the other fields.
Individual docs in `plans/` describe single projects, not priority lists.

**Governance:** Never delete agreed backlog items: split, consolidate, or complete them. Outstanding agreed work stays tracked here until delivered. When consolidating overlapping items, create distinct non-overlapping work units and move completed sub-tasks to the Complete section. Design documents may retire scope by marking phases complete or out-of-scope and linking to the PR where the scope change landed.

**Limits (D-32):** A release slot holds at most twelve outstanding items; overflow moves to the next slot, never out of this file. An item's narrative (results, branch names, what landed where) belongs in its plan, linked from the item in the same change.

**Formatting:** Backlog items describe outstanding work only, with one independently deliverable outcome per item. Prefer at most two short sentences; split mixed outcomes and keep rationale in the linked plan. When sub-tasks complete, move them to the Complete section and simplify the parent item to show only what remains. Do not use strikethrough to track done sub-tasks inline: the Complete section is the record of delivered work.

## 05x: qualified physical measurements — execution rubric (D-36 / D-37)

**Authority:** BACKLOG.md schedules outstanding work; canonical plans own maths and gate definitions. 05x establishes static physical measurement validity, 06x is exclusively mobile SLAM and portable measurement transfer, and 07x carries deferred operator, platform, radar, publishing and research work. Earlier plan-local version references do not override this schedule.

**One-line format:** optional `[#PR]`, then **P0**, optional `(#issue)`, deliverable and `[design](plans/lidar-state-estimation-plan.md)`, size `M`, optional `{math}` or `{frontend}`, and final `S-GEO` workstream. Priority follows the PR; the final workstream follows the design link, original size and any annotation. P0 blocks first qualified static measurement, P1 advances later capabilities, P2 covers research/polish/parked. The original `S/M/L/XL` size notation is unchanged.

**Workstreams:** `S-REF` physical references and held-out evaluation; `S-OBS` observation integrity; `S-GEO` physical state and continuity; `S-UNC` uncertainty and finality; `S-MET` physical interaction metrics; `S-SCENE` L7 scene relationships/Gerardus boundary; `S-EVID` reproducibility and measurement delivery. Other ownership tags: `S-MOBILE`, `S-CLASS`, `S-RADAR`, `S-UI`, `S-PUBLISH`, `S-PLATFORM`. Workstreams cross layers; they do not rename L1–L10.

**Promotion rollups:** G-REF, G-OBS, G-GEO, G-TEMP, G-UNC, G-MET and G-FIELD track pass/fail/insufficient evidence and both measured and unmeasured populations. Existing G-GEO-1, G-UNC-1, G-SMO-1 and G-PER-1 retain their authoritative criteria. The 05x versions are _engineering targets_, not automatic scientific qualification: publish a value only within a proven population and operating envelope, with bias, correlated errors, suppression and coverage explicit.

**Migration ledger:** all 182 former v0.5.3–v0.5.23 outstanding items are preserved: 75 retained and reallocated to the focused 05x slots, 49 rescheduled into sequential 07x slots, 57 explicitly parked, and one handheld reference instrument moved to 06x; four prior 07x/Parked items pulled forward and three smaller gate-contract items introduced. 07x and 09x slot sizes remain ≤12.
