- Planned the route SLAM minimum around the archive's drives between registered static sites, which are bridges between two known poses rather than loops, and wrote an oracle brief of 32 questions for an independent review of the Gerardus implementation, including how SLAM and the ego track should run at 10 and 20 Hz: [plan](../../plans/lidar-route-slam-minimum-plan.md), [brief](../../research/lidar-route-slam-oracle-brief.md), [hydra evaluation](../../../data/experiments/try/route-drive-bridge-first-pair.md).
- Added `lidar-motion-export`, which writes a motion segment as sensor-frame LAS 1.4 with ring, azimuth and rotation extra bytes and a manifest, running L1 decode and L2 rotations only; its per-rotation azimuth clock replaces packet arrival time, and on the 20 Hz perf capture recorded block times scattered 252 µs RMS about the rotor line, which is the jitter and missing block offset the clock removes.
- Added `lidar-ego-track`, which derives platform speed, acceleration, jerk, yaw rate, stops and a vibration spectrum from a trajectory with stated 1σ from local weighted cubic fits; synthetic tests check the stated σ against empirical scatter, and found that the residual over-reads a 2 Hz vibration by about a quarter through the fit's negative response, so the spectrum now carries the fit's gain and a gain-corrected amplitude.
