## Motivation

Every capture's sensor position is eye-read from a field-map photo. Gerardus MVP 0 replaces that with a surveyed world pose for the 24 archive captures in S2 L10 cell 808581, by registering each settled background against USGS 3DEP aerial points and OSM. Gerardus owns the reference data, registration and its alignment log; velocity.report owns the pose on its records and the visualiser bridge (Gerardus ADR-0006, amended by ADR-0007). ADR-0006 expected the bridge's "overlay and pose pane" to land in velocity.report as one small plan. This is that plan.

Without the bridge the operator has no way to see a capture against the reference, and the MVP's manual path, which is the only one until Gerardus builds ICP, cannot run.

## Design / approach

The contract and its reasoning are Gerardus's: `align.proto` and `docs/INTERFACE-VISUALISER.md` in banshee-data/gerardus. Three choices were made here.

1. **Heading.** No velocity.report API serves `north_azimuth_deg`, so the operator types it and the pane remembers it per site. Serving it needs a stored column; that column belongs to the step-2 pose record rather than to a second place. The hint sends `(180 − north_azimuth_deg) mod 360`, the bearing the Gerardus registration found for the sensor's +Y at three sites. The hub doc gives the evidence.
2. **Nudges move the drawing.** A nudge moves the reference by one step along the sensor's axes, or turns it about the sensor, because those are the axes the operator sees. The pose becomes `P·N⁻¹`.
3. **Colours ride in the point layout.** The point shader's table mode reads a palette from fragment buffer 0. The reference binds its own palette there and puts a one-byte code (ASPRS class ramp and intensity shade) in the intensity slot, so the shader is unchanged.

## Scope

### Item 1: overlay and pose pane (built)

**Summary:** Reference over the scan, hint placement, local nudges, register, accept and reject, and the site's records.

**Milestone:** this change.

### Item 2: pose scoring on release

**Summary:** Call `EvaluatePose` when the operator stops nudging and colour the background by per-point residual, with a Snap button for `refine`.

**Steps:**

1. Call it on a pause after the last nudge, never per frame; Gerardus's budget is 200 ms for 100k points.
2. Colour the background by `point_residuals_m` in upload order, through a second palette.
3. Show `FitQuality` and the weak directions beside the pose.

**Milestone:** built, with Gerardus PR 8's registration: hypotheses and gates in the pane, Accept of a hypothesis, scoring on rest, Snap and residual colouring.

### Item 3: tie points

**Summary:** Pick two or three scan and reference point pairs and solve the pose with `SolveTiePoints`, with `level` set for two.

**Milestone:** when Gerardus builds `SolveTiePoints`.

### Item 4: write the accepted pose to velocity.report

**Summary:** Send the accepted `RegistrationRecord` as protojson to `PUT /api/lidar/sites/{l16}/pose` and fill `CoordinateFrameInfo` from it; the TODO in `AlignmentSession.accept()` marks the place.

**Milestone:** with Gerardus MVP step 2 (pose record and frame), which also gives the pane a stored heading to start from.

### Item 5: the 24 sites registered

**Summary:** MVP 0 step 5 from velocity.report's side: every archive site's settled background registered against 3DEP, so the pane has candidates to accept. Run unattended on 2026-10-24 under [pose0001](../../data/experiments/pose/pose0001.md), with the backgrounds exported by `cmd/tools/bgasc`: [report](../lidar/operations/gerardus-static-registration-2026-10.md).

**Milestone:** the outcomes table; acceptance in the pane follows, and write-back waits on item 4.
