# Lane keeping, lane departures and lane changes

- **Evidence:** Ali et al., _SAE International Journal of Transportation Safety_ (2026), described in the briefing as using over 36 million naturalistic miles from more than 3,800 participants. Reported approximately three million lane changes and 300,000 lane/road departures, with many routine departures at shallow 0–2° crossing angles. Confirm exact cohort, definitions and angle distributions against the full paper.

- **Separate outcomes:** (1) lateral lane-position variability when lane assignment is stable; (2) lane-boundary crossings/departures, with crossing angle and direction; (3) intentional lane changes with duration, lateral velocity and accepted front/rear gaps. A roadside sensor generally **cannot determine intention** from trajectory alone; label observed crossing versus inferred lane-change intent separately.

- **Fixed-sensor constraints:** a reliable lane map and stable pose are prerequisites; lane-position SD can be biased by curved lanes, sensor range and truncated tracks. Report opportunity and visibility denominators and avoid comparing lane-change rates without accounting for road geometry.

- **Validation plan:** labeled straight/curved roads, deliberate changes versus small boundary encroachments, pose perturbations, occlusions, lane-map offsets and sampling-window sensitivity. The 0–2° finding is a descriptive distribution, not a new safety cutoff.
